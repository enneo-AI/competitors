## Erster Satz

"Crescendo is strong if you want to outsource your entire customer service including staff. We come in where you want to keep the work in your own hands and your data in Germany."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Crescendo is not a pure software provider but an AI contact center with its own staff: AI agents on all channels, backed by its own team and, since 2026, a partnership with the service provider Alorica. Billing is per resolved request, with guarantees on satisfaction and time to launch. References come from US e-commerce, SaaS and consumer goods. We found none in energy, insurance or DACH. Our advantage lies in data storage in Germany, ready-made utility processes and the depth of email handling. Crescendo leads on certifications, staff and around-the-clock operation.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **German company, data in Germany:** enneo is a Berlin GmbH without investors and stores data at Hetzner in Germany, or in the customer's data center on request. Crescendo is based in San Francisco and names neither its cloud provider nor its region.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply. At Crescendo this is not documented.
- **Attachments and photos in emails:** enneo reads PDFs and photos, for example a meter photo. Crescendo shows images in chat and on the phone. For email attachments we found nothing.
- **Ready-made processes for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. Crescendo's ready-made integrations target shop and helpdesk systems such as Shopify, Gorgias and Zendesk.
- **Documents open, control with the customer:** enneo's DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. The customer describes the AI agents in plain language and changes them via chat with Neo.

## Wo Crescendo vorn liegt

- **Staff included:** Crescendo brings its own CX specialists, with locations including Portland, Greensboro, San Pedro Sula and Manila. Through Alorica, additional staff from a workforce of over 100,000 in 16 countries can be added as needed. enneo delivers software, not staff.
- **Certifications:** SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA according to the Trust Center, ISO 27001 according to a press release. enneo has none of its own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are under audit, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified.
- **Outcome guarantee:** Payment is per resolution. According to the guarantee, the fee is waived if satisfaction falls below the previous provider's, and setup is free if launch doesn't succeed within 30 days.
- **Breadth of the contact center:** Crescendo names workforce planning and forecasting as built in and scores all conversations automatically. enneo has no workforce management.
- **Speed and capital:** By its own account, over 500 AI deployments worldwide, 50+ languages and several industry awards in 2026. Market entry in Europe has been underway since April 2026.

## Wo Crescendo schwächer ist

- **No connection to DACH and utilities:** References are US e-commerce, SaaS and consumer goods, and in Europe Crescendo names British brands. We found no energy, insurance or German-speaking customers. German is not explicitly named among the 50+ languages.
- **Data storage unclear:** Cloud provider and region are not public, and on GDPR Crescendo only names "GDPR-aligned controls". We found no public DPA or subprocessor list.
- **Prices hard to compare:** The pricing page now redirects to the homepage. According to a third-party analysis, what counts as a "resolution" is not defined, and a fixed monthly fee comes on top.
- **Young company in transition:** Co-founded in January 2024, CEO change in July 2026. We found no reviews on OMR, and according to a third-party analysis none on G2.

## Einwände

- **"Crescendo takes it over including staff, that saves us headcount."** True, enneo can't do that, we deliver software. If you want to keep your customer service in-house and not hand your knowledge to a service provider, enneo is the better fit.
- **"Crescendo has SOC 2 and ISO 27001, you don't."** That's a real lead. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are under audit for us, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, and the data is stored by a German provider in Germany.
- **"With Crescendo I only pay when it's resolved."** A good model, as long as it's clear what counts as resolved and which fixed monthly fee comes on top. With us you pay package prices per ticket and voice minute.
