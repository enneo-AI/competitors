## Erster Satz

"Omilia is very strong on the phone, especially with identification and large volumes. The only question is who covers emails, letters and the agent workspace for you."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Omilia is a specialist in phone voice automation with its own speech stack: speech recognition, language understanding, language models and speech synthesis are all built in-house. Omilia is strong with banks and large US companies, with identification on the phone and fraud detection. In DACH, Omilia is just getting started: sales since June 2026 through the partner valantic, and we found no reference customers in DACH. We also found neither email as a channel nor an agent workspace of its own.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email and letters with AI:** enneo reads emails and letters, identifies requests and customers, posts in the target system and replies, also fully automated. Omilia lists phone, chat, messengers, apps and smart speakers. We found email on none of its pages.
- **One system instead of two:** With enneo, the agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one product with skill-based routing. Omilia hands over to NICE CXone, Genesys Cloud, Amazon Connect, RingCentral, Talkdesk or 8x8. For inbox, tickets and agent workspace, the customer needs another product.
- **For utilities: processes ready-made in the product.** Meter readings, installments and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. Omilia's utility references (PSEG, Payless Power) are from the USA.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** enneo handles a meter reading, a new IBAN and a new installment in one email in a single pass, including a PDF and a photo in the attachment. Nothing on this is documented for Omilia.
- **German vendor, storage in Germany:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany. Omilia runs the cloud version on AWS and names only the EU, the USA and Canada in its Trust Center, no country.

## Wo Omilia vorn liegt

- **Own speech stack:** Speech recognition, language understanding, language models and speech synthesis are built in-house. According to Omilia, no conversation data goes to OpenAI, Google or Anthropic. Since 07/2026 there has been its own speech synthesis, Lexis. At enneo, US language models are the default, and the voicebot runs via OpenAI or Deepslate.
- **Identification and fraud protection on the phone:** Its own "Authentication Agents" and TalkGuard against fraud. We have no signature or ID verification.
- **Operations at large scale:** According to Omilia, more than 200 enterprise deployments, a US bank with more than 1 million calls a day, up to 50,000 concurrent conversations at one customer. Leader in the Forrester Wave (Q2 2026) and in the IDC MarketScape 2026.
- **Integration with contact centers:** Ready-made integrations with six contact center platforms. On handover, the context goes along as structured data. We only pass the ticket number in the SIP header outbound and read no context inbound.
- **Certifications and choice of operation:** ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type 2, PCI DSS Level 1, HIPAA. Operation as a shared or dedicated cloud or on the customer's own servers.

## Wo Omilia schwächer ist

- **No written correspondence:** Omilia automates conversations on the phone and in chat. For email, letters and the agent workspace, the customer needs additional systems.
- **Little in DACH:** No reference in DACH found. Sales have been running through valantic since June 2026. German (de-DE) is listed in the docs, but according to Omilia it is not available for all building blocks.
- **Storage location stated only roughly:** The Trust Center (since 08/2026) names only the EU, the USA and Canada for AWS, no country. The security policy was last changed on May 10, 2023, and the platform page still lists ISO/IEC 27001:2013.

## Einwände

- **"Omilia is a Leader at Forrester and IDC."** Rightly so on the phone: its own speech stack and large deployments. For written correspondence and your agents' workspace, Omilia needs another system. With us, it's one product.
- **"With its own language models, no data goes to US providers."** That's a real advantage for Omilia. With us, US models are the default, and EU models such as Mistral or Nebius are possible in the DPA, without storage and without training on customer data. Data is stored at Hetzner in Germany.
- **"We need identification and fraud protection on the phone."** Omilia is ahead there, we don't have signature or ID verification. If a large share of your volume comes in writing, it's worth evaluating phone and written correspondence separately.
