## Erster Satz

"Omilia is very strong on the phone, especially with caller identification and large volumes. The only question is who covers your emails, letters and the agent workspace."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Omilia is a specialist in voice automation on the phone with its own speech stack: speech recognition, language understanding, language models and speech synthesis are all developed in-house. Omilia is strong with banks and large US companies, with caller identification and fraud detection. In the DACH region, Omilia is just getting started: sales since June 2026 via the partner valantic, and we found no reference customers in DACH. We also found neither email as a channel nor an agent workspace of its own.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email and letters with AI:** enneo reads emails and letters, detects requests and customers, posts in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. Omilia names phone, chat, messengers, apps and smart speakers. We didn't find email on any page.
- **One system instead of two:** At enneo, agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one product with skill-based routing. Omilia hands over to NICE CXone, Genesys Cloud, Amazon Connect, RingCentral, Talkdesk or 8x8. For inbox, tickets and agent workspace, the customer needs another product.
- **For utilities: processes ready in the product.** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. Omilia's utility references (PSEG, Payless Power) are from the USA.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles all of it in one pass, including the attached PDF and photo. At Omilia, nothing on this is documented.
- **German provider, storage in Germany:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany. Omilia runs the cloud version on AWS and does not publicly disclose the regions.

## Wo Omilia vorn liegt

- **Own speech stack:** Speech recognition, language understanding, language models and speech synthesis are developed in-house. According to Omilia, no conversation data goes to OpenAI, Google or Anthropic. Since 07/2026 it has its own speech synthesis, Lexis. With us, US language models are the default, and the voicebot runs via OpenAI or Deepslate.
- **Identification and fraud protection on the phone:** Dedicated "Authentication Agents" and TalkGuard against fraud. We have no signature or ID verification.
- **Operation at large scale:** According to Omilia, over 200 enterprise deployments, a US bank with over 1 million calls a day, up to 50,000 concurrent conversations at one customer. Leader in the Forrester Wave (Q2 2026) and the IDC MarketScape 2026.
- **Integration with contact centers:** Ready-made integrations with six contact center platforms. On handover, context is passed along as structured data. We only pass the ticket number outbound in the SIP header and read no context inbound.
- **Certifications and choice of deployment:** ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type 2, PCI DSS Level 1, HIPAA. Deployment as shared or dedicated cloud or on the customer's own servers.

## Einwände

- **"Omilia is a Leader at Forrester and IDC."** On the phone, rightly so: own speech stack and large deployments. For written correspondence and your agent workspace, Omilia needs another system. With us, it's one product.
- **"With its own language models, no data goes to US providers."** That's a real advantage for Omilia. With us, US models are the default, and EU models such as Mistral or Nebius are possible in the DPA, without storage and without training on customer data. Data is stored at Hetzner in Germany.
- **"We need identification and fraud protection on the phone."** That's where Omilia leads, we have no signature or ID verification. If a large share of your volume comes in writing, it's worth evaluating phone and written correspondence separately.
