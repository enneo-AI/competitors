## Erster Satz

"Wilken is a proven billing system for municipal utilities and stays your system of record with us, we come in in front of it: our AI handles emails and calls on its own and books directly in Wilken."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Wilken is first and foremost a billing and ERP provider for utilities: billing, market communication, meter data, devices, portals and app. In that area, Wilken is far deeper than we are, and enneo does not replace a billing system. So far, Wilken's AI comes as the voicebot AIVA (built with its subsidiary Idesia, reference Köthen Energie) and as the AI assistant GY:PT for agents, which answers process and regulatory questions. We did not find an AI at Wilken that handles incoming emails on its own all the way to the booking. That is why Wilken is often the system we connect to, and a real competitor only on the voicebot.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI all the way to the booking:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, books in the target system and replies, also fully automatically. For emails, Wilken describes a template designer and control of incoming and outgoing communication, we did not find AI handling of emails. GY:PT gives agents recommendations, the next stage with agents is announced.
- **Multiple requests, attachments, photos:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply, and the AI reads PDFs and meter photos too. Nothing on this is documented at Wilken.
- **One agent workspace for customer service:** At enneo, ticketing, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one system with skill-based routing. Wilken offers a 360° customer view inside the billing system, and for customer communication the GY platform points to partners such as epilot, CONUTI and Doxee.
- **Channels beyond the phone:** Email, letter, chat, WhatsApp, Telegram and phone all run through the same AI at enneo. Wilken's publicly documented AI in customer contact is so far only on the phone (AIVA).
- **Next to Wilken, not instead of Wilken:** Ready-made agents for meter reading, installment and bank details with plausibility checks, and we build the connection to the billing system per customer as code. Legacy systems without an interface we reach with the RPA bot.

## Wo Wilken vorn liegt

- **Billing and ERP from one source:** Electricity, gas, water, wastewater and heat, grid fees, Redispatch 2.0, market communication, meter data, feed-in, devices, field service, financial accounting. enneo has none of this and does not aim to replace it.
- **Voicebot with access to its own data:** At Köthen Energie, AIVA accesses Wilken data through a shared interface, up to plausibility checks of customer details. Wilken cites 70% of cases resolved without follow-up work, which is a vendor claim. With us, the connection is built per customer.
- **Self-service for end customers:** Portals and app with tariff calculator, installment, bank details, meter reading and push notifications. enneo has no help center or portal for end customers.
- **Certificate and installed base:** ISO 27001 for development and SaaS operation including the GY platform, NIS2 attestation, own data center. 400+ utilities, 48 years in the market, no external capital.
- **Partner ecosystem on GY:** epilot (XRM, with AI summaries and AI reply drafts for emails), CONUTI, Kisters, Doxee, Idesia and others are tightly connected according to Wilken.

## Wo Wilken schwächer ist

- **AI in written correspondence not documented:** For email, letter and chat we found no AI handling of Wilken's own. GY:PT supports agents with process questions, the AI is only meant to act on its own in a later stage, with no date.
- **Voicebot with one public reference:** Documented is Köthen Energie (around 25,000 households), first as a listener producing summaries, then in dialogue. The 70% comes from Wilken itself. Which language model AIVA uses and where it runs is not stated anywhere.
- **Customer service via partners:** For customer communication and XRM, Wilken points to partners such as epilot. For the customer, that means several products and contracts around customer service.
- **Data protection documents:** We found no public DPA, subprocessor list or SLA for AIVA and GY:PT. For AIVA, the only statement is "zu 100% DSGVO-konform".

## Einwände

- **"We have Wilken, the voicebot comes along with it."** Understandable, AIVA knows the Wilken data. Ask what happens with emails, letters and chats: we found no AI handling there at Wilken, while with us everything runs through the same AI and also books in Wilken.
- **"Wilken is building its own AI agents into GY right now."** True, GY:PT has been live since March 2026, as an assistant for agents. Agents that act on their own are announced, with no date. enneo handles emails fully automatically today, all the way to the booking.
- **"We prefer one vendor for everything, and Wilken is ISO certified."** The certificate is a real advantage, ours are ISO 27001 and SOC 2 in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. But for customer service Wilken also relies on partner products, and our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public.

## Fazit

Against Wilken we don't win on billing, ERP, self-service portals or certificates, and we don't need to: Wilken stays the system of record, enneo connects to it.
