## Erster Satz

"Zendesk is a very broad helpdesk, and emails with multiple requests are exactly where we come in: we handle them in one pass with one reply."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Zendesk can do almost everything enneo can, including customer identification and bookings in the ERP. For both, the ERP connection is project work per customer. There are real differences only in the three points below. Everywhere else, enneo at most saves setup work, or Zendesk is ahead.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Multiple requests, one reply:** When a customer writes a meter reading, a new IBAN and a new installment in one email, enneo handles everything in one pass and sends one reply. According to its own documentation, the Zendesk AI agent handles only one process per reply email. The others follow one after another in further emails.
- **Stored in Germany with a German provider:** enneo stores data at Hetzner, on-premises if desired. Zendesk stores data on AWS and Google Cloud, and the company is based in the US. For both, the language models come from US providers.
- **For utilities: processes ready-made in the product.** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks. At Zendesk, you describe each of these processes yourself. We still build the ERP connection per customer.

## Wo Zendesk vorn liegt

- **Broader product:** Help center with community, channels from Instagram to WeChat, more than 1,800 apps, partners and integrations in the marketplace, custom data objects, task lists, approvals and questions to third parties via email, Slack or Teams directly from the ticket.
- **Pay only for success:** An AI case only costs money if a language model confirms after the conversation that it was resolved. Handovers to humans cost nothing. That is a strong argument in procurement.
- **Workforce management and quality:** Workforce management (WFM) and automated scoring of all conversations as a package for €50 per agent per month, plus live dashboards. We have neither.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001, 27017, 27018, 27701, 42001 and SOC 2. We have none of our own.
- **Price and entry:** Licenses from €55 per agent, trial account in a few minutes. One prospect had quotes of around €33k to 34k for 45 seats from Zendesk or Freshdesk versus €61k from us.

## Selbst getestet am 28.10.2026

Trial account Zendesk Suite Professional (Frankfurt data center). Clicked through everything in the admin, set up an email AI agent, tried Copilot on a German ticket. Individual tests, not a series of measurements.

- **Email AI agent:** Set up with only our website as the knowledge source. An email with three requests and a simple question about the website each got only "Ein Mitglied des Support Teams wird sich innerhalb der nächsten 48 Stunden bei Ihnen melden." ("A member of the support team will get back to you within the next 48 hours."). Zendesk shows no reason for this. With careful setup, the result is probably better. So the test shows setup effort, not the limit of the product.
- **"Change installment" flow:** Described in words, the AI draws a flow from it with follow-up questions for customer number and postal code. For the booking, an integration has to be created first, exactly as with us.
- **Topic detection:** An email with a move, an installment and a meter reading got a single topic, and the wrong one: "Request discount or price match". The sentiment "negative" was correct.
- **"More formal" writing aid:** "sorry für Rückruf" (meaning: nobody called back) became "Bitte entschuldigen Sie, dass wir Sie zurückgerufen haben." ("Please excuse us for calling you back."). The meaning was reversed.
- **Done well:** Notice of an open ticket from the same customer for merging, formal or informal address configurable per language, test window directly during setup, reply delay for emails (default 15 minutes).

## Wo Zendesk schwächer ist

- **AI costs hard to plan:** Since May 2026, the only thing included is a credit of USD 5 per Professional agent per month, previously it was 10 resolved cases. At €1.50 to 2.00 per case, that mathematically covers 2 to 3 cases. Everything beyond that is billed monthly, and the AI keeps running by default.
- **Restructuring during operations:** AI agents are managed in a separate interface, everything else in the Admin Center. Zendesk is force-migrating older bots by February 2027. Live reporting will be shut down on Jan 25, 2027, and the successor is not yet finished.
- **Email replies offer little control:** According to the documentation, salutation and structure of the reply email can only be set to a limited extent. Quotes and signatures from the customer's email are removed.
- **Much still in Early Access:** Custom AI agents for background tasks, flows with code, conversation simulator, own MCP server.

## Einwände

- **"Zendesk is the market leader, everyone knows it."** That's true, Zendesk is broader and has more certifications. But on emails with multiple requests, the Zendesk AI agent handles one process per reply email according to its own documentation. We handle all of them in one pass. Test both with your own emails.
- **"Zendesk is significantly cheaper."** On licenses, that's true. At Zendesk, you add the AI at €1.50 to €2.00 per resolved case, Copilot and the workforce management and quality package, and the AI keeps running beyond the small credit by default. Compare the total cost at your volume with our package prices per ticket and voice minute.
- **"We already have Zendesk in house."** Understandable, you've invested in rollout and training. Look at the cases that still remain manual work: emails with multiple requests and energy processes with booking in the ERP. That's exactly where we show the difference using your examples.
- **"At Zendesk, we only pay for a case once, even if the customer follows up three times."** That's true for follow-ups within 72 hours, and it's a fair argument. Ask who decides there what counts as "resolved", and how you verify that across thousands of emails. With us, what counts is the handled ticket, and that's easy to count. And because we handle all requests in an email in one pass, there are fewer follow-ups.

## Fazit

Zendesk is the broader product and cheaper to get started with. We can win when many emails contain multiple requests, when data should be stored with a German provider, and when energy processes are involved. Customer identification, integrations and multiple inboxes are faster to set up with us, but Zendesk can do them too. That alone won't win the deal.
