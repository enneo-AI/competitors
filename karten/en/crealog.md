"CreaLog has been strong on the phone and with outage hotlines for decades. It gets interesting with everything that comes in writing: emails, letters and attachments, handled all the way to the booking in the billing system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

CreaLog has been building voice dialog systems and telephony platforms for over 30 years: voice portals, voicebots, call recording and emergency call routing, mainly for network operators. CreaLog is currently turning this into an AI platform with AI agents for phone, chat and messenger, connected via MCP. CreaLog is strong on the phone and with telcos, with customers among network operators and insurers in DACH. CreaLog does not describe how an AI processes incoming emails, and we found no agent workspace of its own.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI all the way to the booking:** enneo reads the email, identifies the request and the customer, books in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. CreaLog names email as a channel and for outbound contacts. No page describes how the AI reads and handles incoming emails.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply, including PDFs and photos. At CreaLog nothing is documented on this.
- **One system with an agent workspace:** At enneo, agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one product with skill-based routing. CreaLog hands over to agents in the existing contact center. CreaLog does not describe its own agent workspace.
- **For utilities, installment and bank details too:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. For utilities, CreaLog names outage reporting and meter reading, but not installments.
- **Data protection documents public:** At enneo, DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are publicly available. CreaLog mentions the DPA only as part of the project, and subprocessors are not listed in the Trust Center.

## Wo CreaLog vorn liegt

- **Deep telephony:** Own telco platform with SIP application server, media server, call recording including MiFID, emergency call routing and televoting. Customers include Deutsche Telekom, A1 and Swisscom.
- **Outage hotline for utilities:** Outages in gas, electricity and water are prioritized by danger, gas first. The area code determines the network area and control center. Customers include Netze BW, Soluvia and OVAG Netz.
- **Certification:** ISO 27001:2022 from TÜV SÜD until 2029, plus ISO 9001 and 14001. We have no certification of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are under audit.
- **Choice of speech and AI providers:** Speech recognition from Speechmatics, voices from ElevenLabs or Cartesia, OpenAI with EU data residency. Models local, in the cloud or hybrid, operation in Azure, Google Cloud, AWS (EU) or T Cloud. We have less choice.
- **Microsoft Teams and on-premises:** AI agents run as an app in Teams. Operation in the customer's own data center is a standard option. We have no native Teams integration, and on-premises only at high volume.

## Wo CreaLog schwächer ist

- **AI agents not yet proven in production:** The public references cover voice portals, IVR, recording and emergency call routing. We found no customer project with the new AI agents.
- **Written correspondence barely described:** Email only appears as a channel. Nowhere does it say how requests are identified, customers recognized and emails answered.
- **Little transparency:** Which language models run depends on the project, and many providers appear only as a logo. DPA and subprocessors are not public. There are no reviews on OMR yet.

## Einwände

- **"CreaLog has ISO 27001 and runs in our data center if we want."** CreaLog is further along on certification, we say that openly. We store data at Hetzner in Germany, Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified, and our own ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are under audit. Operation in your data center is possible with us at high volume, and DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public.
- **"CreaLog already runs our telephony and the outage hotline."** That doesn't have to go, the outage hotline is a real strength of CreaLog. For emails and letters that should be handled all the way to the booking in the billing system, enneo is the complement alongside it.
- **"CreaLog now has AI agents too."** Ask for a customer project in which the AI handles incoming emails all the way to the booking. We found nothing on this publicly. At enneo that's exactly the core, including multiple requests and attachments.
