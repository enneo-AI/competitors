## Erster Satz

"Ada builds very good AI agents for large international brands. What we add is that the agent workspace, the inbox and the AI are one system, stored by a German provider in Germany."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Ada is a pure provider of AI agents for chat, email and phone, strong with large brands in travel, retail, finance and software. Human agents do not work in Ada but in a third-party helpdesk such as Zendesk or Salesforce, which Ada hands over to. Ada is currently being merged with the US telephony provider Dialpad. We found no references in DACH, at utilities or at insurers.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **One product instead of AI plus helpdesk:** At enneo, the ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one system. Ada hands everything the AI does not solve to a third-party helpdesk such as Zendesk, Salesforce or Freshdesk. That requires separate licenses and in some cases separate email addresses and new email threads.
- **The AI reads attachments too:** enneo reads PDFs and photos, for example a meter photo. At Ada, emails with attachments go to humans by default. On request the agent reads up to three images. The docs do not say that it reads PDF content.
- **Stored in Germany by a German provider:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany, or on-premises on request. DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. Ada stores data at AWS, with Europe only as an option, and lists around 30 subprocessors, most of them only in the USA.
- **For utilities: processes ready in the product.** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks. Ada names no energy customers and no integration with SAP or billing systems.

## Wo Ada vorn liegt

- **Experience and scale in AI agents:** According to Ada, over 550 AI agents in use and billions of conversations handled, customers such as Pinterest, monday.com, Sky, Malaysia Airlines and Cebu Pacific. Revenue more than doubled year over year according to Ada (announcement 03/2026).
- **Languages and channels:** Email agent in 60 languages, voice agent in 42 languages, plus chat and messaging. We have fewer channels.
- **Telephony and contact center:** Integrations with Genesys, NICE CXone, Amazon Connect, Avaya and Twilio, the customer's own SIP trunk is possible. Dialpad adds a contact center with over 50,000 business customers.
- **Certifications:** SOC 2 Type 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS and AIUC-1. We have none of our own.
- **Ecosystem:** Ready-made integrations with Zendesk, Salesforce, Dynamics, ServiceNow, Freshworks and other helpdesks, as well as with airline systems.

## Wo Ada schwächer ist

- **Upheaval from Dialpad:** The merger was announced on Nov 4, 2026. Ada CEO Mike Murchison becomes Chief AI Officer, and the name of the new company is not yet set. Ada writes "Nothing changes as a result of this announcement". What happens to the product and to contacts in Europe is open.
- **Data in Europe only on request:** European storage is a separate option. According to the subprocessor list, language models, speech recognition (Deepgram) and telephony (Twilio) run in the USA.
- **No DACH presence documented:** Among 23 case studies, none from Germany, Austria or Switzerland, none from energy or insurance. Ada is currently hiring its first sales lead for DACH and the Nordics.
- **Email with limits:** Without a Zendesk or Salesforce connector, agents reply from a different address in a new thread. According to the docs, email is not HIPAA-ready, and some building blocks are missing in the channel.

## Einwände

- **"Ada is one of the best-known providers of AI agents."** That's true, Ada has a lot of experience and large brands as customers. Ask for references from Germany and from your industry: we found none publicly. We have ready-made processes for utilities in the product.
- **"We already have Zendesk, Ada just sits on top."** That works, but then you pay for and maintain two systems and two contracts. With us, agent workspace and AI are one system, and the AI also reads attachments like meter photos instead of passing the email to a human.
- **"Ada has SOC 2 and HIPAA, you don't."** That is a real lead. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are under audit for us, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, your data is stored with us in Germany by a German provider, and DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public.

## Fragen an den Kunden

- **Where do your agents work the cases that Ada's AI does not solve?** Expected answer: in Zendesk, Salesforce or another helpdesk with its own license. Transition: "With us, AI and agent workspace are one system, so there's no handover into a second tool."
- **What does Ada do with an email that has a meter photo or a PDF attached?** Expected answer: it goes to a human by default, or it's unclear. Transition: "With us, the AI reads the photo and PDF and still processes the email automatically."
- **Has Ada confirmed in writing where your data is stored and which services process it in the USA?** Expected answer: Europe was promised, details on language models and telephony are still open. Transition: "With us, the data is stored at Hetzner in Germany, and you can read up on our subprocessors today."
