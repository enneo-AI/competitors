## Erster Satz

"Observe.AI is strong at analyzing phone calls and coaching agents live. Emails are where we come in: with us, the AI resolves the case itself, all the way to posting in the target system, with data in Germany."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Observe.AI comes from conversation analytics: automatic quality scoring of all conversations, coaching and reporting for contact centers. Since March 2025 it has its own voicebots (VoiceAI Agents), and since May 2026 the Companion Agent as live assistance for agents. Observe.AI sits on top of an existing contact center system such as Genesys, NICE or Five9 and does not replace it. On its own website, Observe.AI does not mention email for its AI agents, and its customers are almost exclusively US companies.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI through to posting:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, posts in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. Observe.AI describes its AI agents for phone and chat. Email is not mentioned on its own website.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** enneo handles meter reading, new IBAN and new installment from one email in one pass with one reply, and reads PDFs and photos along with it. We found nothing on this at Observe.AI.
- **One product with its own agent workspace:** At enneo, ticketing workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one system. By its own account, Observe.AI runs alongside a contact center system. A ticketing workspace of its own is not described.
- **Data in Germany:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany, on-premises if desired. Observe.AI names AWS without a region and transfers to the USA and other countries.
- **Additionally for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. Observe.AI names utilities as a target industry, but we found no reference at a utility or in the DACH region.

## Wo Observe.AI vorn liegt

- **Conversation analytics and quality:** According to its own figures, Observe.AI scores all conversations automatically, plus coaching, manual evaluation and screen recording. According to a review portal, users mainly praise this coaching loop (G2 4.6 out of 5). We have scorecards, but no analytics and coaching suite this deep.
- **Live assistance on the phone:** Before the call, the Companion Agent summarizes the history, guides the agent step by step during the call, reminds them of mandatory disclosures and writes the summary afterward. Metrigy lists Observe.AI as a MetriStar Top Provider in 2026.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001, SOC 2, ISO 42001, HIPAA and PCI Level 1 according to its own figures. We have no certification of our own.
- **Alongside existing telephony:** Observe.AI works with Genesys, NICE, Five9 and other systems and names over 200 ready-made connectors. Anyone who wants to keep their phone system doesn't have to replace anything.
- **Large deployments in the USA:** DoorDash with 19,000 agents, plus SoFi, Asurion and Cox Automotive. Our ecosystem of ready-made connectors is smaller.

## Wo Observe.AI schwächer ist

- **Email not a focus:** The AI agents are described for phone and chat. Observe.AI announced email back in 2021, but on today's website the channel does not appear for the agents.
- **Unclear data flow:** No hosting region, no subprocessors, no DPA public. The privacy policy allows transfers to the USA and other countries.
- **No DACH presence:** No German references, no information on German as a language found, headquarters in California.
- **Effort and price:** A review portal mentions high effort for setup and integration in complex environments. Implementation, training and overage come on top of the subscription, and pricing is not public.

## Einwände

- **"Observe.AI has many more customers and big names."** That's true, especially in the USA, for example DoorDash with 19,000 agents. We found no reference at a German utility or insurer, so ask specifically about that.
- **"They have SOC 2 and ISO 27001, you don't."** That is a real lead. With us, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, your data stays in Germany with us, and DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public.
- **"We already use Observe.AI for quality and coaching."** That can stay as it is, Observe.AI is strong there. For emails that an AI should resolve on its own all the way into the target system, enneo is the right conversation.

## Fragen an den Kunden

- **How does Observe.AI handle your incoming emails?** Expected answer: Not at all, or only for analytics. The AI agents run on the phone and in chat. Transition: "With us, the AI handles the email from receipt to posting, in one system."
- **Where exactly is your conversation data stored at Observe.AI, and do you have the subprocessor list?** Expected answer: Unclear, only on request. Transition: "Our data is stored at Hetzner in Germany, and all documents are public."
- **In which languages does the Observe.AI voicebot run in production today, including German?** Expected answer: Needs to be asked of the vendor. Transition: "Let's compare that using your own German examples."

## Fazit

We don't win against Observe.AI on conversation analytics, coaching, live assistance on the phone or certifications. We can win where written correspondence should be resolved automatically, data must stay in Germany, and the customer wants one system instead of an additional layer.
