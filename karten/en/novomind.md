## Das Wichtigste vorweg

novomind iAGENT is a contact center system with its own agent workspace for all channels and its own AI bots for email, chat and phone. Of the smaller German providers, novomind comes closest to enneo. According to novomind, its Mailbot has been answering emails with multiple requests automatically since early 2025, plus there are energy references such as EnBW, Yello and DEW21, and ISO 27001. Our edge lies in the details, not in the fundamentals.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Reading attachments and photos:** enneo reads PDFs and photos, for example a meter photo, and uses the values in the same pass. At novomind, nothing on this is documented, neither on the product pages nor in the Mailbot article.
- **Transparent AI:** At enneo you see the resolution plan, the detected values and a preview without posting. How the novomind Mailbot arrives at a reply is not publicly described.
- **Disclosed which models run:** enneo names its language models, EU models are possible in the DPA, and DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. novomind names no language models, and we found no public DPA.
- **Agents in words instead of a toolkit:** At enneo you describe the AI agent in plain language, change it via chat with Neo and check it with test cases. novomind offers a no-code Agent Builder with drag and drop.

## Wo novomind vorn liegt

- **More channels in one agent workspace:** Phone, email, chat, WhatsApp, SMS, letter, fax, video chat and a module for Microsoft Teams. enneo has no native Teams integration, no SMS, no fax and no video chat.
- **Certifications and own data center:** ISO 27001, ISO 22301 (business continuity) and TISAX, own infrastructure in Frankfurt. We have no certification of our own.
- **References in our industries:** Energy: EnBW, Yello, DEW21, EWP Potsdam, RhönEnergie Fulda. Insurance: HanseMerkur, Berlin Direkt, Techniker Krankenkasse. Plus large customers such as Otto Group, Telefónica and Volkswagen.
- **Management and load:** QA cockpit with real-time KPIs, supervisor interface and analytics. According to novomind, over 200,000 tickets a day are no problem. We don't have a live dashboard.
- **Voicebot in the same product:** It authenticates callers, posts via CRM or ERP, such as meter readings and claims, and hands over to agents with context.

## Wo novomind schwächer ist

- **Customer service is only one pillar:** novomind also sells shop, marketplace and product data software. Of nine press releases between March and September 2026, the majority concern shop, product data and partnerships.
- **Little public information on AI:** The public iAGENT docs and release notes mention no AI features and no language models. For details, novomind refers to demos and sales.
- **User criticism:** On OMR (5 reviews, 4.6 out of 5), users complain that poorly configured rules route emails incorrectly, follow-up questions only go to one person and the interface feels sparse.

## Einwände

- **"Many municipal utilities use novomind, it's proven."** True, novomind has strong references among utilities and its own certifications. The question is how far the AI gets in written correspondence: attachments, photos and transparent decisions. Compare both on the same emails with a meter photo.
- **"novomind has ISO 27001 and TISAX, you don't."** That's a fair point, we have no certifications of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress. Data is stored at Hetzner in Germany, Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified, and DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public.
- **"novomind's Mailbot can also handle multiple requests."** Right, novomind has described that since 2025. The difference is whether attachments and photos are read along with the email and whether you see what the AI intends to do before posting.

## Fazit

novomind is a serious competitor: own agent workspace, bots for all channels, certifications and energy references. We can win on attachments and photos, transparent AI decisions and open information about language models. On channel breadth, certifications and references, novomind leads, and we shouldn't argue there.
