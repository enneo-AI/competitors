## Erster Satz

"SOGEDES is an experienced contact center partner with a wide range of options, and email is where we come in: with us, one AI in one system handles everything on its own, from the incoming message to the update in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

SOGEDES is both integrator and vendor: its own contact center software (SOGEDES.X, SOGEDES.ONE, CC4Teams), a reseller of Content Guru's storm platform since July 2026, plus individual AI modules such as WAVE (voicebot), TRAIL (email), STAR (agent assist) and ECHO (conversation analytics). In telephony, Teams integration and quality analytics, SOGEDES is broader than we are. Its email AI TRAIL classifies, routes, replies automatically and can trigger follow-up actions with approval. Multiple requests in one email are not documented. Our advantage is complete AI handling of written requests in one product.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI through to the action:** With enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, posts to the ERP and replies, fully automated if desired. TRAIL mainly describes classification, routing, reply suggestions and an autoresponder. According to SOGEDES, follow-up actions such as an address change in the CRM run "kontrolliert über Guardrails und Freigaben" (controlled through guardrails and approvals).
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** enneo handles a meter reading, a new IBAN and a new installment in one email in a single pass with one reply. We found nothing on this for SOGEDES.
- **One product instead of a toolkit:** Agent workspace, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one system at enneo. SOGEDES deliberately offers "Suite oder Module" (suite or modules): three in-house contact center products, one partner platform, seven AI modules and workflows built on n8n.
- **Ready-made processes for utilities:** enneo ships meter reading, installment and bank details as ready-made specialist agents with plausibility checks; only the ERP integration is built. For utilities, SOGEDES mentions integration with SAP IS-U and Wilken, outage reporting and billing assistance, but no ready-made processes for meter readings or installments.
- **Data protection documents public:** Our DPA, TOM, subprocessors and SLA are available to view. We did not find these documents for SOGEDES, and the language models used are not named.

## Wo SOGEDES vorn liegt

- **Full-range contact center:** Its own ACD, IVR, preview and predictive dialer, recording and supervisor functions. Through Content Guru storm also workforce management, quality management and, according to Content Guru, more than 99.999% availability.
- **Microsoft Teams and Zoom:** CC4Teams has been certified by Microsoft since August 2022 and runs as a Teams extension, plus a Zoom contact center. enneo has no native Teams integration.
- **Conversation analytics and agent assist:** ECHO analyzes conversations, detects sentiment, escalation and churn risk, and cites anonymized results, such as 70% lower QM costs at a telecom customer. STAR provides a live transcript, next steps, knowledge search and call notes, and BRIDGE translates calls live into more than 130 languages.
- **Vendor-neutral consulting and operations:** SOGEDES advises, integrates and operates, also on-premises and in a private cloud. Well-known customers per the website: Vodafone, arvato, Burda, Scout24, Neue Zürcher Zeitung, plus a case study with the insurer Groupama.
- **Certificate:** ISO 27001 according to its own blog. We have no certificate of our own; ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are under audit.

## Wo SOGEDES schwächer ist

- **Many components, many vendors:** Platform from Content Guru, workflows on n8n, speech services from Microsoft Azure, plus in-house modules. SOGEDES itself says "Die beste Lösung für alle gibt es nicht" (there is no single best solution for everyone). For the customer, that means more coordination between products and partners.
- **Thin evidence:** Customer names appear only in the page footer and are not tied to any product. The two case studies (mobile.de, Groupama) give no figures, and the utility case study is anonymous. The CC4Teams page still announces a feature for "Q2 2022". We found no public user reviews on OMR.
- **Technology not transparent:** No prices, no public API documentation and no release notes found. Language models, data center operator and subprocessors are not named, and the privacy policy only links to an external service.

## Einwände

- **"SOGEDES is vendor-neutral and picks the best solution for us."** That is a real advantage for telephony and contact center. For AI handling of emails, it pays to compare with one product that does everything itself, from the incoming email to the posting, without several vendors in between.
- **"SOGEDES is ISO 27001 certified and hosts in Germany."** The certificate is a lead; our ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are under audit, and our hosting provider Hetzner is certified. In return, our DPA, TOM, subprocessors and SLA are public, and we name the language models we use.
- **"SOGEDES has big customers like Vodafone."** True, the names are on the website. Ask which product runs there and whether there is a reference for automated email handling at a utility.

## Fazit

Against SOGEDES, we do not win on the phone system, Teams integration, conversation analytics or depth of consulting. We can win where a lot of written correspondence should be handled automatically: multiple requests, posting to the target system, ready-made utility processes, all in one product with open data protection documents. Where SOGEDES already provides the contact center, enneo alongside it for email and AI is often the better conversation than a full replacement.
