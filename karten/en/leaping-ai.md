## Erster Satz

"Leaping builds very good voicebots that take lots of calls and book appointments. We come in where it goes further: with us, the same AI also handles the emails and the case in the target system, and your agents keep working in the same system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Leaping AI is a young voicebot startup with German roots, now focused on the USA. In the USA it sells almost exclusively to trades and home renovation businesses, in Germany broadly across industries, and its best-known reference is Eurowings. Its channels are phone and SMS, and we found no email handling. Leaping leads on the pure voicebot, outbound campaigns and self-learning improvement. We lead on written correspondence, our own agent workspace and privacy documentation.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Emails and letters included:** enneo handles emails and letters with AI through to the action in the target system, fully automated if desired. At Leaping, we only found phone and SMS.
- **One product with an agent workspace:** At enneo, ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one system. At Leaping, calls the bot doesn't resolve go to a third-party contact center, at Eurowings to an external service provider.
- **Privacy documentation public:** enneo's DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. At Leaping, the privacy policy names no subprocessors and no hosting location, and the terms of service are governed by Delaware law.
- **German GmbH, German hosting:** enneo is a GmbH from Berlin, and data is stored at Hetzner in Germany. Leaping is a US company with a German subsidiary.
- **Ready-made flows for utilities:** enneo brings meter reading, installment and bank details as ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks. We found no Leaping reference in energy, and in insurance only an unnamed case from the USA.

## Wo Leaping AI vorn liegt

- **Pure voicebot with a lot of traffic:** Leaping only does voice. According to the founders, several hundred thousand AI calls a day run over the platform, in Germany and the USA. Eurowings went live in under two months.
- **Self-learning agents:** A dedicated agent evaluates conversations, rewrites prompts and steps, and tests the new version on a small share of calls first. Plus evals, version history and summaries.
- **Outbound and SMS:** Outbound campaigns, calling back leads within seconds, voicemail detection, SMS confirmations from the same number. We don't have a dialer for outbound.
- **Choice of languages and models:** 15+ languages, choice of several language models (GPT, Claude and others), own phone line (BYOC) or hosted telephony, SIP handover.
- **Flexible billing:** According to the founders, some customers only pay for calls with an outcome.

## Wo Leaping AI schwächer ist

- **Phone and SMS only:** For written correspondence, its own agent workspace and posting in the target system, the customer needs additional systems. At Eurowings, after six weeks the bot resolved around 10% of calls on its own, and around 82% were passed on to humans.
- **Privacy barely documented:** Privacy policy without hosting location and subprocessors, no Trust Center, no certification found. The public terms of service (as of February 2024) limit liability to USD 100 and make no availability commitment.
- **Focus is on the USA:** Website, industry pages and blog mainly revolve around US home renovation businesses. We found no reference in energy, and in insurance only an unnamed US case with health insurers.
- **Small, young team:** Founded in 2023, most recently around eight employees according to a report. A comment on the product launch on Hacker News reported frequent misunderstandings in the German demo.

## Einwände

- **"Leaping specializes in voice, so it must be better."** For pure telephony and outbound, Leaping is strong, that's true. But most requests also come in by email and letter, and enneo handles those in the same system through to posting in the target system.
- **"Eurowings uses Leaping, that's a strong reference."** Yes, and it went live quickly. According to Leaping itself, after six weeks the bot resolved around 10% of calls on its own there, and the rest went to humans in the external contact center. Ask where the remaining cases would be handled at your company.
- **"Leaping is also from Berlin."** Its roots are, but according to the terms of service the contracting party is the US company under Delaware law. enneo is a German GmbH, data is stored at Hetzner in Germany, and DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public.
