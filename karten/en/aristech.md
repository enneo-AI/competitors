## Erster Satz

"Aristech is really strong on the phone. The interesting question is who handles your emails and letters all the way to the booking in the target system, and in which system your agents work the rest."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Aristech is first and foremost a voicebot provider with in-house speech recognition and speech synthesis. Its big references are phone hotlines in public administration, mobility and finance. Mail AI and Chat AI exist, but the website describes them only roughly and without a reference. We found no agent workspace: Aristech connects to existing telephony, helpdesks and CRM.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **One system instead of a bot in front of a third-party system:** At enneo, the ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one product, with skill-based routing. At Aristech we found no agent workspace. The customer needs another system for that.
- **Email AI all the way to the booking:** enneo identifies the request and the customer, authenticates, books in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. Aristech Mail AI identifies requests, extracts data and creates replies or tickets. Whether it books in the target system is not documented. At the German Federal Employment Agency (BA), email processing is only planned to follow after the voicebot.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** enneo handles all requests in an email in one pass and replies once. According to a magazine article, Aristech identifies multiple requests, prioritizes them and makes handover to agents easier.
- **For utilities in addition:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. Aristech lists these cases as use cases, but we found no energy reference.

## Wo Aristech vorn liegt

- **Own speech technology:** Speech recognition and speech synthesis are developed in-house, learn industry vocabulary and dialects, and run in the cloud, on-premises or offline. Customers can get their own brand voice. enneo uses OpenAI or Deepslate and has less choice.
- **Large voice references:** German Federal Employment Agency (voicebot "Klara", nationwide rollout announced), Air France-KLM, ADAC, ÖBB, WestLotto. By its own account, over 10 million automated calls per year.
- **Can run fully on-premises:** On request, the AI Suite runs entirely in the customer's IT, and the customer can bring its own language model. At enneo, on-premises operation is only possible at high volume.
- **Group and partners behind it:** Since May 2026 Aristech belongs to Almato (around 200 employees) in the DATAGROUP group. Partners are Accenture, valantic, moinAI and CASERIS. At the BA, Aristech runs the project together with Accenture.

## Wo Aristech schwächer ist

- **Written channels barely documented:** The Mail AI and Chat AI pages name no reference and no metric. Whether WhatsApp is supported and how handover to agents works is not stated.
- **Security without proof:** No certification, no named operator of the EU cloud, no public DPA. The website only says "GDPR-compliant" and "Made in Germany".
- **New in the group:** Aristech has belonged to Almato since May 2026. Aristech is supposed to extend Almato's data platform Bardioc with voice, email and chat. How the AI Suite will be sold on its own going forward is open.

## Einwände

- **"Aristech has the Federal Employment Agency and ADAC as references."** On the phone those are strong references, we acknowledge that. For Mail AI and Chat AI, Aristech names no public reference and no metric. If your focus is written correspondence, let's look together at the path all the way to the booking.
- **"Aristech now belongs to DATAGROUP, that gives us security."** Group backing is a real argument. The acquisition dates from May 2026, and Aristech is supposed to extend Almato's platform Bardioc. With us, agent workspace, AI agents and voicebot are one product from a German GmbH without investors.
- **"Aristech runs entirely in our own data center, and we need that."** Aristech is more flexible there, we say that openly. At enneo, on-premises operation is possible at high volume. Otherwise we store data at Hetzner in Germany, and DPA, TOMs and subprocessors are publicly available.
