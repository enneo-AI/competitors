## Erster Satz

"Sierra builds very strong AI agents for big brands. The question is which system your team works in afterwards and where your customer data is stored."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

With a USD 15.8bn valuation and more than USD 400m in annual revenue, Sierra is one of the largest AI vendors in customer service, with many large US corporations as customers. According to Sierra, one agent runs across chat, SMS, WhatsApp, email, phone and ChatGPT, and Sierra is especially strong on the phone. We did not find its own ticketing agent workspace, and where the data is stored is not stated anywhere publicly. For utilities, there has been a dedicated agent solution with Kraken since June 2026. So far we have not found a reference in DACH.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **One system instead of two:** With enneo, the agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one product with skill-based routing. According to its own site, Sierra hands off to "the right team member" with a summary and supports agents with "Live Assist." We did not find Sierra's own ticketing or inbox for the team.
- **Storage location clearly named:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in data centers in Germany, and for large volumes also on-premises. Sierra does not publicly name its region, cloud provider or language model providers.
- **Attachments, letters and multiple requests:** enneo reads PDFs and photos, such as a meter photo, has letters as a dedicated channel and handles multiple requests in one email in a single pass with one reply. Sierra documents nothing on attachments or multiple requests in one email, and we did not find a letter channel.
- **Ready-made workflows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks, only the ERP integration is built. Sierra's utility solution is tied to the Kraken platform, and which business workflows it includes is not public.
- **Documents without a request:** Our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. Sierra's Trust Center shows no public content, and we did not find a list of subprocessors.

## Wo Sierra vorn liegt

- **Size and speed:** USD 15.8bn valuation, more than USD 400m in annual revenue, and according to Sierra more than 40% of the Fortune 50 as customers. Plus acquisitions such as Fragment (France) and a steady stream of new features, such as Horizon for workflows that run over days and weeks and the Compliance Console for rules per country.
- **Phone and languages:** According to Sierra, voice models tuned for more than 60 locales, dedicated "Voice Personas" for voice and speaking style, and handling of interruptions and background noise. Our voicebot runs on OpenAI or Deepslate, with less choice.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001, SOC 2, ISO 42001, HIPAA, PCI DSS Level 1 and FedRAMP High. We have none of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress.
- **Build, test, improve:** Agents in code (Agent SDK) or without code (Agent Studio), plus "Ghostwriter," which builds agents from SOPs or conversation transcripts. Simulations, experiments and monitoring in production.
- **Utilities via Kraken:** Since June 2026, Kraken has offered dedicated AI agents for utilities built with Sierra, connected to Kraken's data model. According to Kraken, live at a major utility after four weeks, covering 1.3 million customer accounts.

## Wo Sierra schwächer ist

- **Storage and documents unclear:** Neither the hosting region nor the cloud or language model providers are named publicly. The privacy policy allows processing worldwide, and the Trust Center shows nothing without access approval.
- **No own agent workspace found:** Sierra hands off to agents and supports them, but its own ticketing or inbox is not documented. The customer needs another system for that.
- **Thin in DACH:** The Munich office has only been announced since August 2026. We found no reference in Germany, Austria or Switzerland, and no German insurer either.
- **Price hard to pin down:** Sierra bills by outcomes. What counts as an outcome and what it costs is not public.

## Einwände

- **"Sierra is the biggest and best-known vendor."** We don't compete on capital, speed or US brands. We found no Sierra reference in DACH, and the Munich office is new. We bring the utility workflows for the German market ready-made.
- **"Sierra now has a utility solution with Kraken."** True, and for utilities on Kraken that is a real advantage. Ask whether the solution also works with your billing system. With us, the ERP integration is built per customer, and the specialist agents for meter reading, installment and bank details are ready.
- **"Sierra is ISO and SOC 2 certified, you're not."** That is a real lead. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress for us, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, it is public with us where the data is stored and which subprocessors are involved. At Sierra, neither is on the public pages.

## Fragen an den Kunden

- **Which system does your team work in when Sierra hands off to a human?** Expected answer: the existing helpdesk or contact center, licensed separately. Transition: "With us, the agent workspace is part of the product, with inbox, telephony and skill-based routing."
- **Has Sierra already told you which region your data is stored in and which subprocessors are involved?** Expected answer: not yet, only during contracting. Transition: "You can review our DPA and subprocessors yourself today, and data is stored in Germany."
- **Have you seen how Sierra handles an email with three requests and a meter photo attached?** Expected answer: no, nothing is documented on that. Transition: "We're happy to show you live with your own sample emails."

## Fazit

Against Sierra, we don't win on capital, certifications, phone and languages or well-known brands. We can win where the customer needs data in Germany, public privacy documents and an agent workspace for their team in the same product, and wants letters, attachments and multiple requests handled out of the box. Sierra is strong at utilities on Kraken, but still thin in DACH.
