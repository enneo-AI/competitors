## Erster Satz

"Zoho Desk is a very affordable and broad helpdesk, and emails are where we come in: with us, the AI resolves the case itself, all the way to the booking in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Zoho Desk is a mature, very affordable helpdesk from a large software group with its own data centers and its own language models. The AI Zia mainly helps agents: summaries, sentiment, reply drafts from the knowledge base, extracting fields. According to the documentation, the automatic email reply sends one first reply per ticket with help articles, and according to the FAQ the Zia actions only run in English. We did not find any documentation of an AI for Zoho Desk that resolves emails itself, all the way to a booking in a third-party system. That is where our advantage lies.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI through to the booking:** With enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, books in the ERP and replies, fully automated if desired. With Zoho Desk, the automatic email reply responds once per ticket with knowledge articles. The AI agent "Support Specialist" drafts replies from the knowledge base and hands the ticket back to humans if no matching article exists. Actions in third-party systems are not described for it.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** enneo handles a meter reading, a new IBAN and a new installment in one email in a single pass with one reply. We found nothing on this for Zoho Desk, nor on the AI reading attachments and photos.
- **AI agents usable today:** According to Zoho's help center, the Zia Agents for Zoho Desk are only available to selected users in the US and India data centers. At enneo, the AI agents are part of the product and the data is stored at Hetzner in Germany.
- **Ready for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. We did not find any Zoho Desk reference with utilities or insurers in DACH.
- **Phone and voicebot in the same system:** enneo has its own telephony and a voicebot. Zoho Desk handles calls through Zoho Voice (from Professional) or a Twilio extension. We did not find an AI voicebot for Zoho Desk.

## Wo Zoho Desk vorn liegt

- **Price and entry:** Licenses from €7 per agent per month, Professional with telephony for €23, Enterprise for €40. Free plan for three users, trial account right away.
- **Own data centers and own models:** According to its own press release, Zoho operates its data centers itself, in Europe in Amsterdam and Dublin. Zoho's own language models (Zia LLM, Qwen, GLM) also run in the EU data center, and OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and others can be selected with your own key.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001, 27017, 27018, 27701, 9001, SOC 1 and SOC 2. We have none of our own.
- **Help center and channels:** Help center with community and answer bot, in over 40 languages, for multiple brands on the Enterprise plan. Messengers from WhatsApp and Telegram to Line, Instagram and Facebook Messenger.
- **Ecosystem:** Over 360 apps, plus the entire Zoho suite including CRM. According to Zoho, the Agent Studio can access over 700 actions in Zoho products, plus its own MCP server.

## Wo Zoho Desk schwächer ist

- **AI automation tightly limited:** According to Zoho's FAQ, Zia actions only run in English, with at most 1,000 executions per organization per day, after which they stop without notifying the admin. The automatic email reply only fires once per ticket, for the first reply. Zia actions are only available on the Enterprise plan.
- **AI agents not generally available yet:** According to Zoho's help center, the Zia Agents for Desk (Support Specialist, Resolution Expert) are only enabled for selected users in the US and India. Without a matching knowledge article, the Support Specialist hands the ticket back.
- **Support and configuration:** In OMR reviews, users criticize English-language support, a learning curve, many configuration options and unclear prices for add-ons. The Standard plan has no telephony.

## Einwände

- **"Zoho Desk is much cheaper."** On licenses, that is clearly true. But factor in how many emails still have to be handled manually afterwards: according to the documentation, Zoho's automatic reply sends help articles, while with us the case is resolved and booked in the ERP.
- **"We already use Zoho CRM, so Zoho Desk fits in."** Understandable, the Zoho suite fits together well. The question is who resolves the cases in the emails. Test your real emails with multiple requests and attachments in both.
- **"Zoho now has its own AI agents and its own language models."** True, and the own models in the EU data center are a real advantage. According to Zoho's help center, however, the Zia Agents for Desk are only enabled for selected users in the US and India. Ask whether they run in your EU account today.
