## Erster Satz

"Decagon builds very strong AI agents for chat, email and phone. The question is which system your team works in afterwards and where your customer data is stored."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Decagon is one of the fastest-growing AI startups in customer service, with a lot of capital and large US brands as customers. Unlike pure voice vendors, Decagon can also handle email, including long threads and multi-part questions. We did not find an agent workspace of its own: handoffs and the copilot run in Zendesk, Salesforce, Intercom or Front. Data is stored with US hyperscalers. In DACH there is so far one pilot with Deutsche Telekom and no reference in energy or insurance.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **One system instead of two:** With enneo, agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one product with skill routing. Decagon handles emails from within Zendesk, Intercom or Salesforce, and the "Assist" copilot also runs in Salesforce, Zendesk or Front. So the customer always needs a second product.
- **Stored with a German provider:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in data centers in Germany, and on-premises for large volumes. Decagon runs on Google Cloud, AWS and Azure and lists 27 subprocessors, 11 of them only in the US. We did not find an on-premises option.
- **Attachments and letters:** enneo reads PDFs and photos, such as a photo of the meter, and has letters as a separate channel with the same AI as for email. For Decagon, nothing is documented about attachments in email and chat, and we did not find a letter channel.
- **Ready-made workflows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo, only the ERP integration gets built. Decagon does not list energy among its industries, and we did not find an energy reference.
- **Documents without asking:** Our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. At Decagon, the SOC 2 report and security documents are only visible after access is granted in the Trust Center.

- **Capital and pace:** USD 4.5bn valuation, according to press reports more than USD 100m in annual revenue, more than 100 new enterprise customers in the last fiscal year. On Oct 1, 2026 alone, Voice 3, its own speech model Chord, Duet Apprentice and Agent Modules launched, and in October the Experiment Hub. Gartner lists Decagon as a "Cool Vendor" in its 2026 CRM report.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001:2022, SOC 2 Type II, PCI DSS 4.0.1 and HIPAA. We have none of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress.
- **Languages and phone:** According to Decagon, more than 70 languages with automatic switching, its own speech model, plus ElevenLabs and Cartesia. Telephony via Amazon Connect, RingCentral, Twilio or SIP. According to Decagon, Rituals brought its agent to 15 languages in two months. Our voicebot runs on OpenAI or Deepslate, with fewer options.
- **Testing and improving in production:** Live A/B experiments, simulations at large scale and continuous quality monitoring ("Watchtower").
- **Big brands:** Deutsche Telekom (pilot and investment), Avis Budget, Block, Chime, Duolingo, Notion, Rituals, Hertz. Plus a partnership with OpenAI for its new B2B marketplace.

## Wo Decagon schwächer ist

- **No agent workspace of its own:** Decagon relies on an existing helpdesk or CRM. For inbox, handoff and managing agents, the customer needs a second system and a second contract.
- **Many services in the US:** 27 subprocessors, 11 of them located exclusively in the US, including language models, speech output, sign-in and network protection. Whether processing can be limited to the EU is not stated on the public pages.
- **Thin in DACH and in our industries:** According to Decagon, Deutsche Telekom is a pilot (November 2025). We did not find a reference among utilities or German insurers, and the insurance page names no insurer.
- **Ownership unclear:** In October 2026, the press reported that Decagon had hired a bank to explore acquisition interest. ServiceNow denied it, and the talks were considered early. Who will own Decagon in two years is open.

## Einwände

- **"Decagon has billions in capital and Telekom as a customer."** We don't compete on capital and pace. According to Decagon, Telekom is a pilot, and we did not find a reference among utilities or German insurers. We bring the utility workflows ready to use.
- **"Decagon can also do email, even with multiple questions."** True, both are well positioned there. The difference is where your team works and where the data is stored: with Decagon, in your helpdesk and with US hyperscalers. With us, in one workspace and at Hetzner in Germany.
- **"Decagon is ISO and SOC 2 certified, you're not."** That is a real advantage. On our side, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, and we store data in Germany instead of across 27 subprocessors.

## Fazit

Against Decagon, we don't win on capital, certifications, language options or well-known brands, and for email with multiple questions both are well positioned. We can win where the customer doesn't want a second system for their team, needs data in Germany and wants letters, attachments and utility workflows included out of the box. Decagon is still thin in DACH, and especially with utilities and insurers that is our conversation.
