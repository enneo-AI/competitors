## Erster Satz

"VIER is very solid as a phone system and contact center, and emails are where we come in: with us, an AI handles everything on its own, all the way to the booking in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

VIER is first and foremost a contact center provider: phone system, request routing, dialer, recording, analytics. In that area, VIER is significantly bigger and deeper than we are. AI was added later as separate modules, and the agent builder "AI Studio" only in November 2025. Our advantage lies in AI handling of written requests. VIER is ahead on telephony and speech services.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI built as one piece:** At enneo, the same AI reads the email, identifies the customer, books in the target system and replies. VIER routes emails itself. For AI handling, VIER uses an older classification trained on examples, or partner products (Sematell, INEXSO). With Sematell, according to VIER, the reply is created at the partner.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply. Nothing on this is documented at VIER, and neither is reading attachments and photos.
- **One product instead of a construction kit:** At enneo, agent workspace, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one system. At VIER, engage, Copilot, AI Studio, AI Gateway and Voice Gateway are separate products, with engage and Copilot each having their own license tiers. Partner solutions come on top. Users criticize that a new module often has to be licensed for individual features and that configuration is complex.
- **Additionally for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. VIER's energy references mainly concern the phone system and call summaries, not automated email handling.

## Wo VIER vorn liegt

- **Full-breadth contact center:** Routing for up to 9,999 agents, dialer for outbound campaigns, callbacks, call and screen recording, conferencing. According to VIER, 1.23m contacts a day. At EnBW, workforce management is connected.
- **Choice of speech services:** Speech recognition and speech synthesis from Microsoft, Google, IBM, Amazon, ElevenLabs, Nuance or VIER itself, also in the EU. Third-party bots such as Cognigy, boost.ai or Rasa become phone-capable via the Voice Gateway. With Deepslate, we also have an EU voicebot, but less choice.
- **Certificate and seals:** ISO 27001:2022, Trusted Cloud, "Software Hosted in Germany", Telekom Zero Outage. We have no certificate of our own.
- **Model choice with a data protection filter:** According to VIER, the AI Gateway connects more than 60 language models, pseudonymizes beforehand and sets the data protection level per tenant.
- **Installed base at large customers:** EnBW with 700 agents, Stadtwerke Lübeck, rhenag, Helvetia, ADAC, congstar. Plus connectors for SAP, Salesforce, Dynamics, HubSpot and Microsoft Teams, and many sales partners.

## Was hinter der E-Mail-KI von VIER steckt

Checked on Nov 4, 2026 on vier.ai, in the product brochure (as of February 2026) and in the developer documentation. VIER does not offer a test account.

- **VIER engage:** fetches emails, prioritizes and routes them by skills, rule-based and by sentiment. That is routing, not handling.
- **Semantic Intelligence (formerly cognesys):** identifies the request, extracts values such as customer number and bank details, and can reply in a way that closes the case. The technology is a language understanding engine with "200 training samples", the page does not mention language models. The references are undated or older (IKK classic, DHL from 2018).
- **Partners:** INEXSO since 2024, Sematell since June 2025 ("we route customer requests to Sematell"), EITCO since September 2026 for document processes. In 2025, joint projects with Sematell were still only "in preparation".
- **AI Studio:** lists email as a channel. So far, the only references are a voice agent (BetterDoc) and an internal agent. We have not found an email reference.

## Wo VIER schwächer ist

- **Prices hard to pin down:** No public prices, no trial version. According to VIER sales at CCW 2026, AI is billed via "AI Units". How far you get with them, they couldn't say.
- **Acquired and assembled:** According to the shareholder, authensis (2025) was the eighth acquisition. For email AI, there are three paths side by side. Platforms like this mean more coordination between modules and partners for the customer.
- **Data protection claims not substantiated:** "100% GDPR and EU AI Act compliant" without proof. The DPA, subprocessor list and SLA are not available to view. The site says "features such as voice are processed exclusively within the EU", but VIER's own documentation also lists US-hosted speech services.
- **Thin documentation:** Public developer documentation only for the Voice Gateway, with release notes last updated in February 2025. No public API documentation for engage, AI Studio and AI Gateway.

## Einwände

- **"VIER is already our phone system."** Good, VIER does phone system and routing very well. For emails that an AI should handle on its own all the way into the target system, enneo alongside it is often the better conversation than a full switch.
- **"VIER now has AI agents too, with AI Studio."** True, since November 2025, with simulation and evaluation. What is publicly documented so far is one voice agent, and we have not found an email reference. Ask for an email reference with booking in the target system.
- **"VIER is German and ISO certified, you're not."** The certificate is a real lead. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress for us, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. On the other hand, our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. At VIER, they are not available to view.

## Fazit

Against VIER, we don't win on the phone system, the choice of speech services or certifications.
