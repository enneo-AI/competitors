## Erster Satz

"comdesk is a good, affordable phone system with ACD, and where we come in is getting requests resolved: our AI handles emails and calls all the way to the booking in the target system, in one system with your agents' workspace."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

comdesk, formerly CallOne and inopla, is first and foremost a cloud phone system: phone numbers, Teams calling, ACD, Live Center, recording. Its customers are mainly small and mid-sized companies and online retailers. AI comes as a VoiceAgent on the phone and as post-call transcription. We found no email channel and no ticketing system. Against comdesk we win on resolving requests, not on the phone system itself.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI end to end:** At enneo the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, books in the target system and replies, fully automatically if desired. We found no email channel at comdesk, and its own site says comdesk is not a CRM or helpdesk system.
- **One product instead of phone system plus helpdesk:** At enneo, agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one system with skill-based routing. comdesk connects Zendesk, Freshdesk and others for tickets, so the customer needs a second system.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** Meter reading, IBAN and installment in one email are handled by enneo in a single pass with one reply, reading PDFs and meter photos along the way.
- **Ready-made processes for utilities:** enneo ships meter reading, installment and bank details as ready-made specialist agents with plausibility checks. At comdesk, energy customers and such processes are not documented.
- **Data protection documents public:** Our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are publicly available. At comdesk we found no subprocessor list and no name of the data center operator or the language model provider.

## Wo comdesk vorn liegt

- **Phone system for the whole company:** More than 200 features, local phone numbers, devices, Fax2Mail, conferencing, call recording. comdesk replaces the company phone system, not just the service hotline.
- **Teams calling:** A product of its own, including Teams and VoIP in parallel. We have no native Teams integration, only SIP.
- **Affordable and instantly testable:** Public prices from €3.90 per user, 14-day free trial. The ACD is billed by call minutes, not per agent.
- **Live Center and ready-made integrations:** Live dashboard for queues, 17 integrations from Zoho, HubSpot, Dynamics and Zendesk to Power BI and Tableau. We have no live dashboard for contact center management and fewer ready-made connectors.
- **Satisfied users:** OMR Reviews 4.7 out of 5 from 138 reviews, customer support rated especially highly (97%).

## Wo comdesk schwächer ist

- **Phone only:** According to the website, the VoiceAgent handles calls only, and the contact center page describes telephony exclusively. For email, letters or chat the customer needs another system.
- **AI logic sits with the partner:** For voice automation comdesk refers customers to AI partners. According to comdesk, the partner is responsible for AI logic and automations and bills the customer directly, while comdesk provides the telephony.
- **Focus on small companies:** On OMR, 123 of 138 reviews come from small businesses and one from an enterprise. Users criticize an outdated interface, complex routing setup and the cost of add-on APIs.
- **Vague on data protection:** Data center operator, language model provider and subprocessors are not named. The website cites two different availability figures, 99.99% and 99%.

## Einwände

- **"We already use comdesk as our phone system."** Good, as a phone system comdesk is affordable and popular with users. For emails and requests that an AI should resolve all the way into the target system, enneo is the right conversation alongside it, not a phone system switch.
- **"comdesk is much cheaper."** For the phone system alone, that's true. But compare the whole process: with comdesk you add a helpdesk, an AI partner and integration work, with us agent workspace, AI agents and telephony are one product.
- **"comdesk now has an AI VoiceAgent too."** True, with GPT models and integration via webhooks. Ask who builds the logic and the connection to your billing system, according to comdesk that is often done by partners. With us, voicebot, email AI and ready-made utility processes come from one source.
