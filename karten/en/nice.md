## Erster Satz

"As a contact center, NiCE is hard to beat. Our question is who handles your incoming emails, because that's where our AI resolves the case through to posting."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

NiCE is one of the largest contact center providers in the world, a Gartner Leader for eleven years, and much broader and deeper than us in telephony, routing, dialer, workforce management and quality evaluation. Its AI agents have come from Cognigy since 2025, and according to NiCE, full integration into CXone won't be complete until sometime in 2026. For incoming emails, we found no AI agent at NiCE that handles the email itself through to posting. That's where our advantage lies.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI built as one:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, posts in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. According to NiCE's docs, the email goes to agents. Copilot writes a draft there, and the feature is still in preview. Cognigy has no inbound channel for email.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles all of it in one pass with one reply. At NiCE, Copilot lists the topics of the email, the agent selects them and gets a draft.
- **Attachments and photos:** enneo reads PDFs and photos, such as a meter photo. At NiCE, the only thing documented for email is a 40 MB size limit.
- **On top of that, for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. NiCE's energy reference in Germany is about workforce management, quality and analytics, not automated handling of written correspondence.
- **German company, German operator:** enneo is a Berlin-based GmbH without investors and stores data at Hetzner, on-premises if desired. NiCE is a publicly listed group headquartered in Israel and the USA and runs on AWS. Cognigy no longer offers on-premises for new customers.

## Wo NiCE vorn liegt

- **Full-range contact center:** Workforce management with forecasting, AI quality evaluation for up to 100% of contacts, "Personal Connection" dialer for campaigns, analytics and customer feedback. Since June 2026 offered as the "Workforce Empowerment Suite" for humans and AI agents together. Gartner named NiCE a Leader for CCaaS for the eleventh time in 2025.
- **Voicebot and voice services:** Cognigy is a mature voicebot toolkit. Via the Voice Gateway, AWS, Deepgram, ElevenLabs, Google, Microsoft, Nuance, Speechmatics and custom providers can be selected. We have an EU voicebot with Deepslate, but less choice.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001, SOC 2 with HITRUST, PCI DSS, FedRAMP, IRAP. Cognigy also has BSI C5. We have no certification of our own.
- **Microsoft Teams:** Direct Routing, agent workspace directly in Teams and Teams presence sync. We only connect Teams via SIP.
- **Large references in Germany:** AOK Bayern with 225 locations on the EU Sovereign Cloud, with Cognigy as AI phone assistant. The utility e-regio with 130 agents reports 30% lower costs and 98% reachability.

## Wo NiCE schwächer ist

- **Written correspondence not the focus:** Email runs as a channel for agents. NiCE's AI press releases from 2025 and 2026 mention voice and "digital", but never email. The AOK Bayern reference also mentions AI only on the phone.
- **Products in transition:** Autopilot comes in Amelia and Omilia variants, plus Mpower Agents and Cognigy. NiCE has only announced full integration of Cognigy into CXone for 2026. Cognigy's agent workspace ("Live Agent") is in maintenance mode, and no new features are coming there.
- **C5 and origin:** We found no BSI C5 attestation of its own for CXone. The EU cloud only states that its partners comply with C5. The company is headquartered in Israel and the USA.
- **AI costs hard to predict:** Copilot, summaries and AI routing are billed by usage. What a "session" for USD 0.25 is, isn't stated on the pricing page.

## Einwände

- **"NiCE is the market leader, Gartner has named them a Leader for eleven years."** For contact center, telephony and workforce management, that's true, and we don't dispute it. But for incoming emails, NiCE has no documented AI agent that resolves the case through to posting, and that's exactly what we do.
- **"We have NiCE in-house, and the contract is still running."** Then NiCE stays for telephony. enneo takes over email and letters alongside it, without you replacing your contact center.
- **"With Cognigy, NiCE now also has the best voicebot toolkit."** On the phone, Cognigy is strong and offers more voice providers than we do. But Cognigy has no inbound channel for email, and full integration into CXone has only been announced for 2026. With us, voicebot, email AI and agent workspace are one system today.

- **How do your agents select the topics in an email with multiple requests?** Expected answer: Copilot lists the topics, the agent selects them. Transition: "With us, one resolution plan covers all requests and one reply goes out, fully automated if desired."

Transition: "With us, agent workspace, AI agents and telephony are already one product today."

## Intern

- **Too big for many prospects:** A partner (contact center integrator, resells NiCE) sees prospects viewing NiCE as a "behemoth" and being put off by the all-in-one package. Others are convinced precisely by the package with workforce management. *Attio call, Sep 4, 2026, min. 7:44*
- **Weaknesses in the German market and with email:** The same partner experiences "functional weaknesses" with NiCE in projects, especially in the German market. With AI handling of emails up to straight-through processing, he hits limits because his vendors are not specialized in email. *Attio call, Sep 4, 2026, min. 8:47 and 23:48*
- **Cognigy too big and too expensive:** The same partner calls Cognigy "a little bit too big" and too expensive in many places. A second partner (IT service provider) has implemented hardly any projects with Cognigy because it was too expensive for customers. *Attio call, Sep 4, 2026, min. 9:18; Attio call, Oct 19, 2026, min. 43:31*
- **Hard to sell outside large enterprises:** According to the first partner, the vendor (Cognigy, now NiCE) considers smaller segments irrelevant, and outside large enterprises the solution is hard to sell. Existing customers with one to two years left on their contract would rather add AI than switch. *Attio call, Oct 16, 2026, min. 4:42 and 28:47*

## Fazit

We won't win against NiCE on phone system, workforce management, dialer, voice services or certifications. We can win where a lot of written correspondence should be handled automatically through to posting, with multiple requests, attachments and energy processes. Where NiCE is already in place as the contact center, positioning enneo for email and letters alongside it is often a better conversation than a full replacement.
