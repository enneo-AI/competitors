## Erster Satz

"Qualimero is strong when an online shop wants to sell products that need explaining via chat, but for utilities it's about written correspondence that gets handled all the way to the booking in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Qualimero comes from AI product advice for online shops and markets "AI employees" for advice, customer service and lead qualification, with channels such as web chat, WhatsApp, email and phone. In customer service, the AI answers questions on order status, shipping and returns with live data from shop, CRM and ERP. Escalated cases land in third-party helpdesks such as Zendesk, HubSpot or Freshdesk, an agent workspace of its own is not described. All case studies come from online retail and brokerage services, we found no utilities.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **One product with an agent workspace:** At enneo, the ticketing workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one system. At Qualimero, cases the AI can't solve land in a third-party helpdesk that the customer runs on top.
- **Email AI through to the action:** enneo reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, books in the target system and replies, also fully automatically. Qualimero's website mainly describes fast answers in chat. We found nothing on email handling with multiple requests and attachments. One user on OMR still listed the email integration as missing.
- **Multiple requests in one email:** enneo creates one resolution plan for the whole ticket and handles all requests in a single pass with one reply.
- **Ready-made workflows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made specialist agents at enneo, with plausibility checks. Qualimero targets gardening, crop protection, heating technology and other consumer goods that need explaining, and the AI is trained on each customer's own knowledge.
- **Data protection documents public:** enneo's DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are publicly available. At Qualimero, the privacy policy refers to a separate DPA, subprocessors and hosting provider are not named, and the terms contain no SLA.

## Wo Qualimero vorn liegt

- **Product advice and sales:** Identifying needs, recommending products, offering accessories, checking availability live, A/B tests with a control group to measure revenue. This is Qualimero's core, enneo offers nothing comparable here.
- **Fast start in online retail:** Ready-made integrations with Shopify and Shopware, plus Zendesk, Freshdesk, HubSpot, Intercom, Zapier and Make. 14-day free trial, no minimum term, no setup costs.
- **Recognizing images in chat:** According to the website, the AI recognizes plants, damage or products in customer photos and recommends treatment or replacement, also for complaints.
- **Reviews and awards:** 5.0 out of 5 on OMR from 6 reviews. Winner of the Austrian Retail Innovation Awards 2025, top 3 at the EHI Tech Days 2025.
- **Well-known brands as customers:** Logos of BSH, Hornbach, REWE, Mercedes-Benz and Ergo, plus 13 case studies with metrics.

## Wo Qualimero schwächer ist

- **No own agent workspace described:** What the AI can't solve goes to Zendesk, HubSpot or Freshdesk. So the customer still needs a second system for its agents.
- **Email depth not proven:** The website shows chat examples and response times under 10 seconds. We found nothing on handling longer emails with multiple requests and attachments, and an OMR user missed the email integration in early 2025.
- **Little public on security:** No certification named, no list of subprocessors, no hosting provider, no language model. The terms provide the service "as is" and contain no availability commitment.
- **No utility focus:** All 13 case studies come from online retail, brokerage services and events. We found nothing on meter readings, installments or billing systems.

## Einwände

- **"Qualimero is cheaper and ready in minutes."** For a shop chat with a Shopify integration, that's true. At utilities, the effort lies in the integration with billing and ERP and in customer verification, and that's where enneo brings ready-made specialist agents, only the ERP integration is built.
- **"Qualimero has big brands like Ergo and Mercedes-Benz."** The logos are on the website, but the published case studies come from online retail. Ask for a reference with email handling through to the booking in the target system.
- **"We already use Zendesk, Qualimero fits right in."** That's a real advantage for Qualimero. With enneo, you get agent workspace, AI agents, knowledge and telephony in one system and don't have to coordinate two vendors.
