## Erster Satz

"Parloa is one of the strongest providers on the phone. The question is what happens to your emails and letters, and which system your agents end up working in."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Parloa builds AI agents for phone and chat and is one of the strongest providers there, with a lot of capital and large insurers in DACH as customers. Even in November 2026, we found no email channel and no agent workspace of its own. Parloa hands over to third-party contact centers and CRMs. Our advantage lies in written correspondence and in everything running in one system. On the phone, Parloa leads.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email and letters with AI:** enneo reads emails and letters, detects requests and customers, posts in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. On its pricing page, channels page and in the February 2026 product release, Parloa only names phone and chat. We found no email channel.
- **One system instead of two:** At enneo, agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one product with skill-based routing. At Parloa, humans take over in a third-party system such as Genesys, Five9, Salesforce or Zendesk. So the customer always needs a second product and a second contract.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles all of it in one pass with one reply, including the attached PDF and photo. For written requests, Parloa has documented nothing on this. In chat, Parloa accepts files, but it doesn't say whether the AI analyzes their content.
- **Stored with a German provider:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany, and at large volumes also on-premises. Parloa runs on Microsoft Azure, and we found no on-premises option.

## Wo Parloa vorn liegt

- **Phone and voice:** According to Parloa, over 140 languages and variants, SIP directly to phone system, contact center and WebRTC, switching from chat to a call without losing context. Speech synthesis can be set to EU or US per agent (ElevenLabs). Our voicebot runs via OpenAI or Deepslate, with less choice.
- **Integration with third-party contact centers:** Ready-made integrations with Genesys, Amazon Connect, Cisco, Avaya, Five9, Talkdesk, RingCentral, Vonage and others, plus SAP, Salesforce, Dynamics and ServiceNow. On handover, the human gets a transcript or summary. As of Oct 16, Parloa's docs also described context via SIP header (UUI) in both directions, for example with Genesys. We only pass the ticket number outbound in the SIP header and read no context inbound.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001:2022, ISO 42001, SOC 2 Type 1 and 2, PCI DSS, HIPAA. We have none of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress.
- **References in our industries:** Insurers such as Swiss Life, BarmeniaGothaer, Württembergische (33% less waiting time according to Parloa), Schwäbisch Hall, Uelzener, Münchener Verein and DOMCURA. Plus utilities such as MAINGAU, evm and rhenag.
- **Capital and partners:** Over USD 560m in funding. Since Oct 26, 2026, SAP has been selling the platform as "SAP Conversational Agent Management by Parloa". Teams is connected to bring experts into the conversation. We don't have a native Teams integration.

## Wo Parloa schwächer ist

- **Phone and chat only:** For email, letters and the agent workspace, the customer needs a second system. Even the new "Context Intelligence" (10/2026) talks about "any channel" but doesn't mention email.
- **Price hard to predict:** No price list. The per-minute rate depends on the complexity of each use case and is set by sales.
- **Little that can be verified publicly:** The developer docs are no longer public, and former pages lead nowhere. The Trust Center only lists "Major Cloud Provider" as hosting and names the subprocessors (Google, Twilio, Datadog, MongoDB, Microsoft) without purpose or location.

## Einwände

- **"Parloa is the market leader and has many times more capital."** On capital and on the phone, that's true, and we won't argue with it. What matters for you is how much of your volume that covers: Parloa names phone and chat, while email and letters stay in a second system. With us, both run in one product.
- **"Parloa is cheaper per minute."** It can look that way in a first comparison. Ask which calls are billed, including those that are only forwarded, and add the second system for written correspondence and agents. With us, inbox, agent workspace and telephony are included in the product.
- **"We can buy Parloa through our SAP contract."** Since October 2026, SAP has been selling Parloa itself, which makes procurement easier. For email, letters and the agent workspace, you still need another system. With us, it's one product with one contract.

## Fragen an den Kunden

- **How do emails, letters and scans get into Parloa, and who handles them there?** Expected answer: A reference to chat and messaging, or to another system. Transition: "With us, email and letters are channels of their own, with AI through to the action in the target system."
- **Which system does your team work in when Parloa hands over to a human?** Expected answer: In the existing CRM or contact center, which the customer provides and licenses themselves. Transition: "With us, the agent workspace is part of the product, with inbox, telephony and skill-based routing."
- **Which calls does Parloa bill, and which per-minute rate applies to your use cases?** Expected answer: Rate per use case only in the offer, billing per call. Transition: "Let's compare the total cost, with all minutes and the second system for written correspondence."

## Intern

- **Specialist, cheaper in the standard calculation:** A prospect (retail) calls Parloa "not bad by all accounts" and "very specialized", and cheaper in his standard calculation. The higher price would have to be justified by enneo's added value for email, since Parloa is "really just voice and chat". *Attio call, Jun 17, 2026, min. 22:28 and 5:41*
- **Every call is billed:** A partner (implementation partner for both vendors) explains the price gap in the same comparison: Parloa bills every incoming call, no matter what happens afterward, even those that are only forwarded. Anyone comparing per-minute prices one to one gets a skewed picture. *Attio call, Jun 17, 2026, min. 41:34*
- **Business unit against Parloa:** A partner (implementation partner) reports from an ongoing comparison: the prospect's business unit wants enneo, and Parloa is out on functionality "because Paloa can only do autopilot", meaning no mode in which the AI prepares the case for agents. Parloa is only still in the race on price. *Attio call, Aug 26, 2026, min. 14:28 and 15:29*
- **Channel silos at an existing customer:** An existing customer (utility, large account) uses Parloa for voice and chatbot, and enneo for written requests. As a result, logic is rebuilt per channel, and it's hard to see which channels a customer used before calling. *Attio call, Oct 27, 2026, min. 35:09*
- **Two service providers under review:** The same existing customer sees enneo's voice demo as "the counterpart to Paloa" and is checking whether it "makes no sense at all to run two service providers". *Attio call, Oct 27, 2026, min. 47:50 and 51:19*
- **Wins tenders on experience:** A partner (consulting and integrator, Parloa is its largest partnership) says that in tenders people often ask why Parloa costs so much. In the end, "the winner is Palor" anyway, because of experience with large volumes. *Attio call, Nov 3, 2026, min. 17:41 and 59:49*

## Fazit

We won't win against Parloa on the phone, on certifications or on integration with large contact centers. We can win where there is a lot of written correspondence and the customer doesn't want to run two systems: email and letters through to posting, multiple requests, agent workspace and AI in one product, stored with a German provider. Where Parloa is already on the phone, enneo for the written channel is the obvious conversation.
