## Erster Satz

"Insiders has been strong in documents and inbound mail for decades, especially with insurers, and we come in where it goes further: the AI handles the request itself, all the way to the reply to the customer."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Insiders comes from document analysis: recognizing and classifying documents and mail, extracting data and passing it to the right process. In that area, Insiders is big, certified and widely used by insurers and banks. For customer communication there is the product Flow (formerly smart FLOW), which routes messages, suggests replies and also answers typical requests on its own. Our advantage lies where an email contains several requests, the customer has to be identified automatically and the AI should work all the way to the action in the target system and the reply.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **The full process instead of pre-sorting:** enneo reads the email, identifies the request, identifies and verifies the customer, executes the action in the ERP and replies, also fully automatically. For Flow, Insiders describes routing, reply suggestions and automatic replies to typical requests. In their example, an agent transfers the address change into the CRM "with one click".
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply. We found nothing on this at Insiders, neither for Flow nor for its AI OvAItion.
- **AI agents in plain language:** At enneo, you describe an AI agent in words, change it with Neo via chat and check it with test cases. For specialist extraction, Insiders relies on the low-code toolkit GenerAItor and its own models trained for documents.
- **Ready-made processes for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. Insiders names insurance, banking, healthcare, industry and the public sector as its industries. Utilities are not among them.
- **One product with telephony and voicebot:** At enneo, agent workspace, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one system. Flow lists phone as a channel, but we found no voicebot at Insiders.

## Wo Insiders Technologies vorn liegt

- **Certifications:** ISO 27001 for the cloud services and a BSI C5 Type 2 attestation for the Insiders Cloud. We have no certification of our own.
- **Documents at high volume:** According to Insiders, ING processes 7 million documents with OmnIA, EOS around 2.5 million per year, BGV more than 650,000 incoming documents at around 95 percent classification accuracy. On top of that, e-invoicing and invoice processing for purchasing.
- **Insurers as an installed base:** References such as HUK-COBURG for Flow, plus Debeka, BGV, IDEAL, Baloise and a long list of other insurers. In this industry, Insiders is much broader than we are.
- **On-premises operation:** Language models can run in the customer's infrastructure if desired, so documents never leave the network. At enneo, on-premises is only possible from high volumes.
- **A group behind them:** Since 2025, Insiders has been part of the Proalpha Group. Its products are being built into Proalpha's ERP and remain available separately.

## Wo Insiders Technologies schwächer ist

- **Email is a side branch:** The product pages focus on inbound mail, documents and e-invoicing. Flow has its own page, but it only shows one example with a single request. Whether Flow covers multiple requests or attachments in the reply process is not stated there.
- **Rules instead of AI in the installed base:** A Debeka representative describes the future on the Insiders website as rule-based systems complemented by AI. Existing customers are therefore likely to still work with rules in many cases.
- **No utility templates:** Utilities do not appear among the industries, and we found no ready-made processes for meter reading or installments.
- **Transition:** Acquisition by Proalpha in 2025, change at the top on Oct 1, 2026. How independently Flow will be developed alongside the Proalpha ERP is open.

## Einwände

- **"Insiders is certified, you're not."** That's true, ISO 27001 and C5 are a real advantage. At enneo, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, so your data protection team can review them right away.
- **"We already use Insiders for our inbox."** Good, Insiders is strong for mail and documents. For customer emails that an AI should handle on its own all the way to booking and reply, a comparison with your own sample emails is worthwhile, especially with several requests in one email.
- **"Insiders has many more references."** With insurers and banks, yes. Ask for a reference where Flow books and answers emails with several requests fully automatically in the target system.
