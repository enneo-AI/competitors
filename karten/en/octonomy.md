## Erster Satz

"octonomy is strong when knowledge from manuals and wiring diagrams is needed. With us, it's about the complete service center: inbox, agent workspace, phone and AI agents in one system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

octonomy is a young, well-funded Cologne startup that positions itself as an "AI expert system" for technical support, field service and customer service. Its strength is understanding technical documents including tables and drawings, plus agents that look things up and take action in ERP, CRM and ticketing systems. octonomy sits on top of an existing ticketing system such as Zendesk, Dixa or SAP C4C. We found no agent workspace of its own. On certifications, octonomy is clearly ahead of us. On ready-made processes for utilities and on an all-in-one system, we are ahead.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **One system instead of an add-on:** At enneo, ticketing workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one product with skill-based routing. According to its own customer stories, octonomy works in the customer's ticketing system, for example Zendesk, Dixa or SAP C4C. So the customer still needs a second system.
- **Ready-made processes for utilities:** enneo brings meter reading, installment and bank details as ready-made specialist agents with plausibility checks. octonomy's energy reference is a wind farm operator focused on field service and fault diagnosis, not a utility's customer service.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** enneo creates a resolution plan for the entire ticket and handles all requests in an email in one pass with one reply, fully automated if desired. We found nothing on this at octonomy.
- **Letter as a dedicated channel:** At enneo, scanned letters are handled by the same AI as emails. octonomy names chat, email, phone and ticketing systems, but not letters.
- **Operations and models of your choice:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany, can run on-premises at large volumes and can agree EU language models such as Mistral in the DPA. octonomy runs on AWS and uses models from Anthropic, Google and OpenAI. We found no on-premises option.

## Wo octonomy vorn liegt

- **Certifications:** ISO 27001:2022, ISO 27701, ISO 9001 and SOC 2 Type II, plus TISAX listed in the Trust Center. We have no certification of our own. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress.
- **Technical documents:** The "Visual Cortex" reads PDFs as images and, according to octonomy, understands wiring diagrams, tables and drawings. In the Tolly Group benchmark published by octonomy, 96% answer accuracy versus 58% for Microsoft Copilot.
- **Field service and SAP:** Dedicated integration for SAP Field Service and Asset Management, for example spare parts search and checklists in SAP Smartforms. Reference wind farm operator with 50,000 pages of documentation and, according to octonomy, half as many site visits per fault.
- **Insurance reference:** An InsurTech uses octonomy for chat, voice agent and claims handling, according to octonomy over 600 claims per week in production and over 50% less support effort.
- **Sovereign cloud and capital:** AWS European Sovereign Cloud option with EU personnel, supported by the BSI. Around USD 25m in venture capital and about 120 employees, expanding in the USA.

## Wo octonomy schwächer ist

- **No service center of its own:** octonomy needs a ticketing system next to it. At one customer, chat, email and phone run via Dixa, at another, tickets run via SAP C4C. For customers without a modern ticketing system, that means two providers.
- **Young and not very public:** Founded in 2024. No public docs, no release notes, no pricing. We found no user reviews on OMR or G2.
- **Results often narrowly scoped:** At the stationery customer, the 70% automation applies to two request types (shipment status and FAQ), which together make up around 16% of contact volume. There, one to two people look after the system almost full time.
- **Statements on models not fully consistent:** The website speaks of an "octonomy LLM" and says no data leaves the EU. The Trust Center names Anthropic, Google and OpenAI as subprocessors with standard contractual clauses.

## Einwände

- **"octonomy is certified, you're not."** That's true and a real lead. With us, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified, and our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are publicly available.
- **"octonomy is more modern, they're AI from the ground up."** octonomy is technically modern, especially with technical documents. The difference lies elsewhere: we deliver the whole service center including agent workspace and telephony, while octonomy sits on top of an existing ticketing system. Ask which system your agents will then use every day.
- **"We're currently testing octonomy for our knowledge."** Good, preparing knowledge pays off with any provider. For the next step, resolving emails with multiple requests all the way to posting in the ERP, we're happy to show you the comparison with your own sample emails.
