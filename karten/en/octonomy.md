## Erster Satz

"octonomy is strong when knowledge from manuals and wiring diagrams is needed. With us, it's about the complete service center: inbox, agent workspace, phone and AI agents in one system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

octonomy is a young, well-funded startup from Cologne that positions itself as an "AI expert system" for technical support, field service and customer service. Its strength is understanding technical documents including tables and drawings, plus agents that look things up and act in ERP, CRM and ticketing systems. octonomy sits on top of an existing ticketing system such as Zendesk, Dixa or SAP C4C. We did not find an agent workspace of its own. octonomy is clearly ahead of us on certifications. We are ahead on ready-made processes for utilities and on a single integrated system.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **One system instead of an add-on:** With enneo, the ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one product with skill-based routing. According to its own customer stories, octonomy works inside the customer's ticketing system, such as Zendesk, Dixa or SAP C4C. The customer still needs a second system for that.
- **Ready-made processes for utilities:** enneo ships meter readings, installments and bank details as ready-made specialist agents with plausibility checks. octonomy's energy reference is a wind farm operator focused on field service and fault diagnosis, not a utility's customer service.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** enneo creates a resolution plan for the whole ticket and handles all requests in an email in one pass with one reply, also fully automated. We found nothing on this for octonomy.
- **Letters as a separate channel:** At enneo, the same AI that handles emails processes scanned letters. octonomy lists chat, email, phone and ticketing systems, but not letters.
- **Operations and models of your choice:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany, can run in the customer's own data center for large volumes, and can include EU language models such as Mistral in the DPA. octonomy runs on AWS and uses models from Anthropic, Google and OpenAI. We did not find an on-premises option.

## Wo octonomy vorn liegt

- **Certifications:** ISO 27001:2022, ISO 27701, ISO 9001 and SOC 2 Type II, plus TISAX listed in the Trust Center. We have no certification of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress.
- **Technical documents:** The "Visual Cortex" reads PDFs as images and, according to octonomy, understands wiring diagrams, tables and drawings. In the Tolly Group benchmark published by octonomy: 96% answer accuracy versus 58% for Microsoft Copilot.
- **Field service and SAP:** Its own integration for SAP Field Service and Asset Management, for example spare parts search and checklists in SAP Smartforms. Reference: a wind farm operator with 50,000 pages of documentation and, according to octonomy, half as many site visits per fault.
- **Insurance reference:** An insurtech uses octonomy for chat, a voice agent and claims handling. According to octonomy, more than 600 claims per week in production and more than 50% less support effort.
- **Sovereign cloud and capital:** Option of the AWS European Sovereign Cloud with EU staff, supported by the BSI. Around USD 25m in venture capital and about 120 employees, expanding in the USA.

## Wo octonomy schwächer ist

- **No service center of its own:** octonomy needs a ticketing system alongside it. At one customer, chat, email and phone run through Dixa, at another, tickets run through SAP C4C. For customers without a modern ticketing system, that means two vendors.
- **Young and not very public:** Founded in 2024. No public documentation, no release notes, no pricing. We did not find user reviews on OMR or G2.
- **Results often narrowly scoped:** At the stationery customer, the 70% automation applies to two request types (shipment status and FAQ), which together account for about 16% of contact volume. There, one to two people look after the system almost exclusively.
- **Statements on models not fully consistent:** The website talks about an "octonomy LLM" and says no data leaves the EU. The Trust Center lists Anthropic, Google and OpenAI as subprocessors with standard contractual clauses.

## Einwände

- **"octonomy is certified, you are not."** That's true and a real advantage. Our ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified, and our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are publicly available.
- **"octonomy is more modern, they've been AI from the start."** octonomy is technically modern, especially with technical documents. The difference lies elsewhere: we deliver the whole service center including the agent workspace and telephony, while octonomy sits on top of an existing ticketing system. Ask which system your agents will then use every day.
- **"We're currently testing octonomy for our knowledge base."** Good, preparing knowledge pays off with any vendor. For the next step, handling emails with multiple requests all the way to posting in the ERP, we're happy to show you the comparison using your own sample emails.

## Fazit

We win against octonomy where a customer wants to renew the whole service center and needs to automate a lot of written correspondence through to posting in the target system, especially at utilities. octonomy wins on certifications, on technical documentation with drawings and in field service, especially with SAP. Where octonomy is already being tested for knowledge, our lever is the next step: emails with multiple requests, letters and telephony in one system.
