## Erster Satz

"BOTfriends knows municipal utilities well and is strong in chat. The question is what happens to your emails and letters, and where your agents continue working after the handover."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

BOTfriends is a bot provider that has targeted municipal utilities for years: dedicated solution pages, a study on municipal utility customer service, a long list of logos. BOTfriends is strong in self-service via chat and phone, with templates for Wilken and Schleupen and meter readings going straight into the billing system. At BOTfriends, email is mainly sorting, forwarding and replying. BOTfriends does not describe its own agent workspace. The bot hands over to existing systems.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email all the way to the booking:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and authenticates the customer, books in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. For email, BOTfriends describes sorting, forwarding, replying and data extraction. The page mentions handover to a customer system only for meter readings.
- **Multiple requests and attachments in one email:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply. enneo also reads PDF and photo attachments. At BOTfriends, neither is documented for email.
- **Agent workspace and AI in one product:** enneo brings ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot in one system with skill-based routing. BOTfriends hands conversations over to agents, and its copilot works "in existing ticket systems". So the customer additionally needs a ticket system or contact center.
- **Data protection you can verify:** At enneo, DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, data is stored at Hetzner in Germany, and on-premises at high volume. BOTfriends names neither its hosting provider nor its language models nor its subprocessors and is available only as cloud.
- **Letters as a separate channel:** At enneo, scans via email or API run through the same AI as emails. At BOTfriends we found no letter channel.

## Wo BOTfriends schwächer ist

- **Email is a side channel:** The solution pages revolve around chat and phone. For email, there is no information on multiple requests, incoming attachments or bookings beyond meter readings.
- **No agent workspace:** What happens after the handover to agents takes place in a third-party system. BOTfriends does not name a specific ticket system.
- **Data protection only claimed:** "100% GDPR-compliant" and "EU AI Act compliant" without proof. Hosting provider, language models and subprocessors are not public, and BOTfriends names no certifications.
- **Criticism in reviews:** On OMR, reviewers mention reporting, complex configuration of advanced features and recurring bugs, and in one case unnatural pauses on the phone.

## Einwände

- **"BOTfriends has over 40 municipal utilities as references."** That's a strong list, especially for chat and phone. Ask how many of them automate incoming emails all the way to the booking. That's exactly our core.
- **"BOTfriends has ready-made templates for Wilken and Schleupen."** That's true, at enneo the ERP integration is built per customer. In return, our interfaces are code in any format, and we reach legacy systems without an interface with the RPA bot.
- **"BOTfriends' price is public, and we can test for free."** That's a real advantage. But add the ticket system for your agents and the email handling that BOTfriends doesn't describe. With us you pay packages per ticket and voice minute for one system that includes the agent workspace.
