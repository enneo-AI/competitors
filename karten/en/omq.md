## Erster Satz

"OMQ is very mature in self-service with its help page and chatbot. Where we come in is email: with us, one AI also handles multiple requests through to posting in the target system, with one reply."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

OMQ comes from self-service: help page, contact form and chatbot that answer questions from a knowledge base before they reach the service team. The email bot also answers from this knowledge base and plugs into third-party ticketing systems such as Zendesk, Freshdesk or Zammad as a plugin. Actions in the backend (change address, cancel subscription, order status) are handled by a separate module, the AI Agent, documented mainly for chat and forms. OMQ has no agent workspace of its own, and the voicebot has only existed since August 2026.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email through to posting:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, posts in the target system and replies, also fully automated. According to the product page, OMQ's email bot answers emails from the knowledge base. We found no documentation that it posts in the target system.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** enneo handles a meter reading, a new IBAN and a meter photo in one email in a single pass with one reply. At OMQ, nothing is documented on multiple requests or on reading attachments.
- **One system instead of a plugin:** enneo brings the ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony in one product. For cases the bot can't resolve, OMQ requires a third-party ticketing system.
- **Ready-made processes for utilities:** Meter readings, installments and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. OMQ's energy references are chatbots and help pages that answer questions about meter readings and installments. Capturing data in the billing system is not described there.

## Wo OMQ vorn liegt

- **Self-service for end customers:** Help page, smart contact form and chatbot answer questions before they become tickets. We don't have a help center for end customers.
- **Open pricing and an easy trial:** Packages from €160 to €3,000 per month are on the website, 14-day trial, no onboarding costs. We have no public price list.
- **Quick to connect to existing systems:** Plugins for Zendesk, Freshdesk, Zammad, OTRS, Salesforce, plus shops (Shopware, Shopify), retail ERP (Xentral, JTL, Business Central) and parcel services. Anyone who already has a ticketing system doesn't need to replace anything.
- **Long reference list and good reviews:** Tchibo, Deutsche Bahn, Mister Spex, MAGIX, myphotobook (83% of emails answered automatically), in energy Stadtwerke Düren and Stadtwerke Stendal. OMR: 4.6 out of 5 from 95 reviews.
- **Many languages:** According to OMQ, more than 30 languages for chatbot, email bot and voicebot.

## Wo OMQ schwächer ist

- **No agent workspace of its own:** Anything the bot can't resolve goes into a third-party ticketing system or by email to the service team. So the customer needs two systems.
- **Email bot mainly answers from knowledge:** The product page describes answers from the knowledge base. It says nothing about multiple requests, attachments or posting in the target system. Users on OMR mention problems understanding complex requests and high effort maintaining the knowledge base.
- **Voicebot is young:** Only since release 10.5 in August 2026, telephony only via sipgate, no public voice reference found.
- **Thin security evidence:** No certification found, TOM catalog from March 2023 not public, website privacy policy from 2022.

## Einwände

- **"OMQ is cheaper, the prices are even on the website."** True, the packages are open and the entry price is low. Ask what integration costs come on top for the email bot from Business upward, and which ticketing system you pay for separately for the remaining cases. With us, agent workspace and AI are one product.
- **"OMQ has municipal utilities as references."** Right, Stadtwerke Düren and Stendal use the chatbot for questions about contracts, invoices and meter readings. For emails in which the customer wants to change something, meter readings, installments and bank details are ready-made processes with posting in the system at enneo.
- **"We already run OMQ's chatbot."** Good, OMQ does website self-service very well. For the written correspondence that still comes in, adding enneo alongside it is often the better conversation than a complete switch.
