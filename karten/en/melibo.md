## Erster Satz

"melibo is really good in online retail for order status and returns. For utilities, it's about more: multiple requests, meter photos and phone in one system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

melibo comes from the website chatbot and is expanding it into a platform for customer service automation, clearly tailored to online retailers. The "Helpdesk Agent" classifies emails and tickets, suggests replies and actions, and also answers clearly defined, recurring requests fully automatically, for example cancellations or return labels. Since fall 2026, melibo has been building its own inbox with live chat, macros and SLA deadlines, but still also works inside Zendesk, Freshdesk and similar tools. We found no phone, no letter channel and no references at utilities or insurers.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email through to posting, including complex cases:** At enneo, the AI identifies the request and the customer, verifies the customer, posts in the ERP and replies, fully automated if desired. According to its own website, melibo only answers "wiederkehrende, klar definierte Anfragen" (recurring, clearly defined requests) fully automatically, and new automations start as suggestions. How customers are identified and verified in the email is not described at melibo.
- **Multiple requests and attachments in one email:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles all of it in one pass with one reply, and reads PDFs and meter photos along with it. At melibo, according to the docs, each request is assigned to one topic with one workflow. Multiple requests per email and analysis of attachments are not documented.
- **Phone and voicebot in the same system:** enneo brings ticketing, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot in one product with skill-based routing. At melibo we found neither telephony nor a voicebot, and WhatsApp runs via the partner ChatWerk.
- **Built for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made specialist agents at enneo with plausibility checks, plus a dedicated letter channel for scans. melibo's templates and integrations revolve around orders, returns and shipping.
- **Verifiable data protection:** At enneo, DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, and EU language models are possible under the DPA. melibo sends the DPA by email on request, names OpenAI as the model provider and no data center operator.

## Wo melibo vorn liegt

- **E-commerce in depth:** Ready-made connections to shop systems, inventory management, returns services and over 20 shipping providers, plus a Shop Agent for product advice with its own analytics. According to the docs, over 100 tools can be connected, including SAP via SAP Gateway and Microsoft Dynamics 365. Our range of ready-made connectors is smaller.
- **Fits into the existing ticketing system:** melibo works directly in Zendesk, Freshdesk, GREYHOUND, Zammad, Salesforce, OTRS, HubSpot or Outlook with suggested replies and one-click actions. Anyone who wants to keep their helpdesk doesn't have to change anything.
- **Visible references and numbers:** Fahrrad XXL with 57% automation, FC Augsburg with 94.1% in chat since February 2025, ramp-up on average 30 business days across over 170 projects. All figures from melibo.
- **Reviews and openness:** OMR 4.7 out of 5 from 121 reviews, customer support praised most often at 97%. Public docs with a changelog almost every week, REST API and MCP server since August 2026.
- **Simple pricing logic:** Fixed monthly amount with no billing per chat or resolution. However, melibo does not publish concrete figures.

## Wo melibo schwächer ist

- **Built for online retail:** Industry pages exist for e-commerce and gyms, and integrations and templates revolve around shop, shipping and returns. We found no references at utilities or insurers.
- **Automation for simple cases:** melibo itself recommends starting with processes without many special cases and warns against too many similar topics. On OMR, users mention time-consuming training of topics and limits with complex requests.
- **No phone:** Neither telephony nor voicebot found. Anyone who wants to relieve the hotline needs a second provider.
- **Data protection only partly documented:** No certification of its own, data center operator not named, models from OpenAI with no EU alternative mentioned. DPA only on request, TOMs and subprocessors not found publicly.

## Einwände

- **"melibo has lots of ready-made integrations and good reviews."** That's true, especially for shops, shipping and helpdesks. For utilities, what counts is what happens to meter reading, installment and bank details in one email, and for that we bring ready-made specialist agents.
- **"melibo works directly in our Zendesk, we don't have to switch anything."** That's a real advantage if emails are mostly simple standard questions. Ask how an email with three requests and a photo attached gets handled. That is not documented at melibo.
- **"melibo is cheaper and goes live faster."** melibo doesn't have public pricing either. Add telephony and the emails that don't run fully automatically. With us, both are in the same product.

## Fazit

We win against melibo at utilities and anywhere emails involve multiple requests, attachments and verification before posting, and where phone is part of the picture.
