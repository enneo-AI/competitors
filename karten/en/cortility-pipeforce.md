## Erster Satz

"cortility and Pipeforce are very close to SAP IS-U, and that's a real advantage. The question is who replies to your customer after the booking, and what happens to emails that contain several requests."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

cortility and Pipeforce are going after exactly our strongest field: emails and letters to utilities, handled by AI all the way to the booking in SAP IS-U or S/4HANA Utilities. Pipeforce identifies and classifies the request, extracts data from text and photos and checks the AI results. cortility passes the data via an AI connector on SAP BTP to the SAP process and checks it there before booking. Our lead is in the reply to the customer, multiple requests, additional channels, our own agent workspace and target systems beyond SAP. They lead on SAP proximity.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **All the way to the reply to the customer:** enneo reads the email, identifies and authenticates the customer, books in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. cortility and Pipeforce describe: identify the request, extract data, pass it to SAP, and automatically ask back if information is missing. They do not describe a reply to the customer after the booking. Elsewhere, Pipeforce talks about draft replies that agents decide on.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply. At cortility and Pipeforce this is not documented, and all examples have one request per email.
- **One product with agent workspace and all channels:** At enneo, a ticketing agent workspace with skill-based routing, email, letter, chat, WhatsApp, Telegram, phone and voicebot belong to one system. The joint solution covers email and letters. Whatever the AI can't handle goes to agents in their existing tools. We found no voicebot at either company.
- **Not just SAP:** enneo builds interfaces as code, in any format, and with the RPA bot also reaches legacy systems without an interface. The joint solution is explicitly built for SAP S/4HANA Utilities and IS-U.
- **One provider:** At enneo, AI, agent workspace and integration come from one source. Here two companies share the path, Pipeforce for the AI, cortility for SAP.

## Wo cortility und Pipeforce vorn liegen

- **SAP IS-U from the inside:** cortility has been building SAP add-on solutions for utilities since 2011. The AI connector on SAP BTP is meant to provide a standardized connection to S/4HANA, SAP Utilities and clean core architectures. At enneo, the ERP integration is built per customer.
- **No change in day-to-day operations:** According to cortility, agents stay in their familiar tools, all data and documents are stored in SAP, no additional system. For an IS-U organization that doesn't want a new agent workspace, that's a strong argument.
- **Check in SAP before the booking:** Pipeforce checks AI decisions in several stages and documents data and rules. cortility additionally checks the data directly in SAP before the process runs. According to cortility, unclear cases are not "processed blindly".
- **Public numbers:** From initial implementations, Pipeforce cites 99.2% accuracy in classification, 98.7% in extraction and up to 80% less manual work. These are vendor figures without a named customer, but they appear in the trade press.
- **Broad use of Pipeforce:** According to its own site, Pipeforce also classifies and routes inbound items from portals, forms, chat and phone, checks contracts against DORA, KRITIS and NIS2, and lets business departments define processes themselves.

## Wo cortility und Pipeforce schwächer sind

- **Little evidence:** No named customers, no case study, no public product documentation for the joint solution. The numbers come from Pipeforce's "initial implementations". In June 2026, the trade paper ZfK still wrote that cortility is "developing" the connector.
- **Young partnership of two companies:** The collaboration has been running since 2025. Who is responsible for operations, errors and further development of the joint path is not stated publicly anywhere.
- **Narrow scope:** Standard requests such as meter reading, move and installment, one request at a time, only email and letters, only SAP. Anything beyond that ends up with agents again.
- **Claims without proof:** "Up to 100%" automation and meeting "KRITIS-relevant requirements" appear on the site without evidence.

## Einwände

- **"cortility has known our SAP IS-U for years."** That's true, and it's a real advantage. Ask which utilities are already using the joint solution live. Publicly there is no customer name for it yet.
- **"We don't want a new agent workspace, our people stay in their tools."** Understandable. That leaves open what happens to the emails the AI can't handle, and who replies to the customer. With us, AI, reply and agent workspace are in the same system.
- **"Pipeforce cites over 99 percent accuracy in classification."** Those are vendor figures from initial implementations, without a named customer. With us you see the solution plan, extracted values and a preview without booking, and you test it with test cases on your own requests.
