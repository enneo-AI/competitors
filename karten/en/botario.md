## Erster Satz

"botario is well positioned in chat and on the phone, including its own speech technology, and we focus on email: with us, the AI handles the request all the way to the booking, and your agents keep working in the same system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

botario builds chatbots, voicebots and live chat, plus its own speech recognition and speech synthesis that can run entirely on-premises. botario has belonged to NFON since 2024, and since August 2026 the company has been merged into NFON AG. Its references with utilities and health insurers are real, but the publicly described cases run via chat and phone. botario lists email as a channel but does not describe how an AI processes emails, and it does not offer a ticketing agent workspace.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email all the way to the booking:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and authenticates the customer, books in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. botario lists email as a channel on its industry pages but describes no AI processing of emails. The AI Agent Studio names only chat and phone.
- **Multiple requests and attachments in one email:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply, and the AI also reads PDF and photo attachments. At botario, neither is documented for email. Uploads exist in chat.
- **Agent workspace and AI in one product:** enneo brings ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot in one system with skill-based routing. botario offers a live chat with text snippets and notes. According to botario, a ticket system is connected, not included.
- **Letters as a separate channel:** At enneo, scanned letters run through the same AI as emails. We found no letter channel at botario.
- **Data protection you can verify:** At enneo, DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. botario shows badges such as "EU AI Act konform" and "DSGVO-konform", but we did not find the underlying documents publicly.

## Wo botario vorn liegt

- **In-house speech technology:** According to botario, speech recognition, speech synthesis and language models are developed in-house, in the EU and without US providers, more than 25 languages, custom voices via cloning. We use OpenAI or Deepslate.
- **On-premises for everything:** All modules run in the cloud, on-premises or hybrid, and the speech services even without any internet connection. CEWE runs botario on its own infrastructure. With us, on-premises is only possible at high volume.
- **Free choice of language models:** Current GPT models or models on the customer's infrastructure, and the provider can be switched. With us, US providers are the default, EU models are possible via the DPA.
- **Visible references:** Utilities such as swb, ENTEGA, E-Netz Südhessen and Pfalzwerke, more than 25 statutory health insurers, plus Tchibo MOBIL with numbers: about 50% of tariff changes fully automated, calls about 20% shorter.
- **Corporate backing:** NFON is publicly listed, runs the cloud phone system Cloudya with a BSI C5 Type 2 attestation and cites more than 300 integrations. Whether the C5 attestation also covers botario is not stated there. It applies to Cloudya.

## Wo botario schwächer ist

- **Written correspondence barely described:** All public case studies (swb, CEWE, Tchibo MOBIL, Steinbach) concern chat and phone. For AI processing of emails there is neither a feature description nor a reference.
- **No own agent workspace:** The live chat is a handover window, not a ticket system. For emails and follow-up cases, the customer needs another system.
- **Upheaval from the merger:** botario has been part of NFON AG since August 2026. NFON also markets its own AI assistants (FrontDesk, Workdesk). NFON does not say what will happen to the brand and products.
- **Little evidence:** Badges such as "EU AI Act konform" without evidence, no public documentation, no release notes, no certificate of its own. The ISO 27001 on its pages is Hetzner's.

## Einwände

- **"botario has more references with utilities and health insurers."** True, the list is long and visible. Ask for a reference where emails are processed automatically all the way to the booking. Only chat and phone are publicly described.
- **"botario runs completely in-house for us, including speech technology."** That is a real advantage for organizations that don't want anything to leave the building. With us, everything sits at Hetzner in Germany, on-premises at high volume, and you can review our DPA, TOMs and subprocessors today.
- **"We already have the botario chatbot."** Good, then chat is covered. For the inbox and your agents' workspace, enneo alongside it is the better conversation than replacing the chatbot.

## Fragen an den Kunden

- **What share of your requests comes in by email or letter, and who handles them today?** Expected answer: a large share, manually in the ticket system. Transition: "That is exactly where we come in. botario describes no AI processing for it."
- **Which system do your agents continue working in when the bot hands over?** Expected answer: a separate ticket system or contact center. Transition: "With us, bot, inbox and agent workspace are one system under one contract."
- **Do you already know how NFON will continue botario after the merger?** Expected answer: no, that is open. Transition: "For us, the product is the core of the company, and we can show you the roadmap."

## Fazit

Against botario, we don't win on in-house speech technology, operation without internet or the length of the reference list. We can win where a lot of written correspondence should be handled automatically and agents need a workspace: email and letters all the way to the booking, multiple requests, attachments, all in one product. Where botario already provides the chatbot, enneo alongside it is the better conversation for the inbox and the agent workspace.
