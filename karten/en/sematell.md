## Erster Satz

"Sematell has known email at utilities for many years. The question is whether you want your emails just sorted and answered, or handled all the way to the booking in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Sematell specializes in email response management: ReplyOne for large service organizations, and since July 2025 ReplyX for e-commerce teams. Its strengths are classification, routing and reply suggestions, plus long-standing energy customers such as lekker Energie (since 2011), badenova and ENTEGA PLUS. We have not found a voicebot. Since June 2025, Sematell has been a VIER partner: VIER forwards emails to Sematell, Sematell creates the reply.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **All the way to the booking in the target system:** enneo identifies requests and customers, verifies identity, books in the ERP and replies, also fully automated. Sematell classifies, extracts customer number and contract data and answers standard cases fully automatically. Sematell only mentions bookings in the target system as examples (returns via the ERP, tariff change into the CRM).
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** enneo handles all requests in an email with one solution plan in a single pass. Sematell writes that the AI analyzes requests holistically so that all questions get answered. Whether multiple processes are completed in the target system is not documented.
- **Voicebot in the same system:** enneo has telephony and voicebot in the product, with its own telephony or the customer's SIP line. Sematell lists telephony as a channel, but we have not found a voicebot.
- **Additionally for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. On its energy page, Sematell mentions installments and tariff changes, meter readings only as a request type, and bank details and moves not at all.

## Wo Sematell vorn liegt

- **Long experience with email in energy:** lekker Energie has used ReplyOne since 2011, along with badenova, ENTEGA PLUS and, since September 2025, an unnamed energy group. spread.group has processed more than 1.2m email requests a year with it.
- **Certificate with a validity period:** ISO 27001:2022 for development and SaaS operations, valid until February 2028, hosting only in Germany. We have no certificate of our own.
- **Retention on cancellations:** The AI detects cancellations and triggers defined win-back steps. lekker Energie reports 34% fewer cancellations.
- **Access via VIER:** For VIER customers, Sematell is the designated partner for email AI. VIER brings the phone system and routing, Sematell the reply.

## Wo Sematell schwächer ist

- **Two products side by side:** ReplyOne for large customers, ReplyX for e-commerce. Public documentation exists only for ReplyX, with no information on language models. The release notes for ReplyOne 7.8 list no AI updates.
- **AI in energy barely proven:** At the energy group from September 2025, ReplyOne starts with email, AI features are to follow later. The lekker reference describes cancellation detection, not automated handling of meter readings or installments.
- **Change in leadership:** Dr. Andreas Seidl has been in charge since July 2026, his predecessor held the role for about 1.5 years. The company does not disclose who is behind Sematell.
- **Two companies in the VIER partnership:** VIER distributes the email, Sematell writes the reply. In June 2025, joint customer projects were still only in preparation.

## Einwände

- **"We've been running Sematell for years, why switch?"** Understandable, Sematell has long experience with email in the energy sector. But when two systems run side by side, work gets duplicated. With us, inbox, agent workspace and AI are one system, and the AI handles cases all the way to the booking.
- **"Sematell has ISO 27001, you don't."** That's true, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress for us, and our hosting provider Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. On the other hand, our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are publicly available. At Sematell, the DPA and subprocessors are not.
- **"Through VIER, we get telephony and email AI together."** Then you're working with two companies: VIER distributes the email, Sematell writes the reply. In June 2025, joint customer projects were still only in preparation. With us, telephony, voicebot and email AI are one product.
