## Erster Satz

"Fin is one of the strongest AI agents on the market. We focus on where it gets concrete for utilities: meter reading, installment and bank details with posting in the target system, stored in Germany with a German provider."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Intercom is a helpdesk from the USA whose AI agent Fin has become its main business. Fin also runs on third-party helpdesks and is billed per outcome. Since September 2026, the company has belonged to Salesforce. We won't win against Fin on certifications, maturity or ecosystem. We win on energy flows, storage in Germany, letters and PDF attachments, and emails with multiple requests.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Ready-made flows for utilities:** At enneo, meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks. With Fin, you describe each flow yourself as a skill or procedure. We found no public energy reference for Fin. We still build the ERP connection per customer.
- **Stored in Germany with a German provider:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany, on-premises if desired. Intercom stores data at AWS, in the EU only in Dublin and only for new workspaces on the higher plans. According to Intercom, billing and admin data can leave the EU. The company now belongs to Salesforce.
- **Multiple requests and attachments in one email:** enneo handles meter reading, IBAN and installment from one email in one pass with one reply, and reads PDFs and photos. According to its docs, Fin reads images (JPG, PNG, GIF, HEIC), and the docs don't mention PDF. We found no documentation of how Fin works through multiple requests in one email with postings.
- **Letters as a channel of their own:** At enneo, scanned letters are handled by the same AI as emails. We found no letter channel at Intercom.
- **A German provider without a corporation behind it:** enneo is a German GmbH without investors. DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. At Intercom, the security reports sit behind an access request in the Trust Center.

## Wo Intercom vorn liegt

- **Maturity and size:** According to Salesforce, over 30,000 customers. According to Fin, over 12,000 Fin customers and on average 76% of conversations resolved in which Fin was involved (vendor figure). Its own AI models (Apex 2.0) and an AI research team of over 60 people.
- **Fin on any helpdesk:** Fin also runs on Zendesk, Salesforce, HubSpot, Freshworks and others, with no platform fee. A customer doesn't have to switch ticketing systems for it.
- **Pay only for outcomes:** $0.99 per resolved conversation or completed procedure, one outcome per conversation, even if Fin performs several actions. Handovers without an outcome cost nothing. 14-day trial account without a credit card.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001, 27018, 27701, 42001, SOC 2, HIPAA and HDS. We have none of our own.
- **Ecosystem and help center:** Over 350 ready-made integrations, help center for end customers, messenger for website and app. According to OMR, users praise its ease of use.

## Wo Intercom schwächer ist

- **EU hosting with restrictions:** Only for new workspaces on Advanced and Expert. Existing workspaces can't be migrated, and old conversations can't be carried over. Billing, admin and usage data can leave the EU, and support outside business hours partly comes from outside the EU.
- **Costs hard to plan:** An outcome is also counted when Fin completes a procedure and then hands over to a human. Seats, Copilot and Pro come on top. On OMR, price is the most frequent criticism (15 mentions), and features are said to be often priced as separate modules.
- **Change of ownership:** Since May 2026 the company has been called Fin, and since September 2026 it has belonged to Salesforce. According to Salesforce, Fin's offerings are meant to complement Agentforce. What this means long term for product, pricing and helpdesk has not been publicly defined.
- **Little visibility in the German market:** We found no German-language reference in energy or insurance. A question in Intercom's community about German Fin users has gone unanswered since August 2026.

## Einwände

- **"Fin is the market leader and, according to Intercom, resolves three quarters of all requests."** Fin is strong, and the 76% is the vendor's figure for conversations in which Fin was involved. Ask for a reference at a German utility with posting in the ERP. We'll show you meter reading, installment and bank details on your own sample emails.
- **"With Fin, we only pay when something gets resolved."** That's a fair point. But a completed procedure with handover to a human also counts, and seats and add-on modules come on top. Compare the total cost at your volume with our package pricing per ticket and per voice minute.
- **"Salesforce is behind Intercom now, that's safer."** Salesforce brings size, that's true. At the same time, Fin is currently being integrated into a corporation, and the data sits with a US provider. With us, it's stored at Hetzner in Germany, and DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public.

## Fazit

We won't win against Fin on maturity, certifications, ecosystem or use on third-party helpdesks. We can win with utilities that want ready-made energy flows with posting in the ERP, want to keep their data with a German provider and have a lot of written correspondence with attachments, multiple requests or letters. The change of ownership to Salesforce is a reason to start a conversation, but not proof of weakness.
