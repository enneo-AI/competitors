## Erster Satz

"Verint is strong in workforce management and quality management, and that's exactly where we come in alongside it: emails that an AI handles on its own all the way to the booking in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Verint is above all strong in workforce management, quality management, recording and analytics, and on top of that adds more than 50 individual AI bots that mostly run alongside a third-party contact center such as Genesys, NICE or Amazon Connect. Since June 2026, the "Agent Factory" for building AI agents has been added. We have not found an AI at Verint that handles incoming emails all the way to the booking in the target system. That's where our advantage lies. Verint is clearly ahead on workforce management, quality and certifications.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI across the full flow:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, books in the ERP and replies, also fully automated. At Verint, email is a channel in the agent workspace with routing. The bots are described for voice and digital channels, and we have not found automated handling of incoming emails.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply. enneo also reads PDFs and photos, e.g. a meter photo. Nothing is documented on either at Verint.
- **Ready-made processes for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. Verint's utility references concern workforce management, transcription, quality and after-call work, not automated case handling.
- **One product, German provider:** Agent workspace, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one system at enneo, stored at Hetzner in Germany, on-premises if desired. Verint is a US company, stores data on AWS, Azure or Google, and is currently merging two product worlds (Verint and Calabrio).

## Wo Verint vorn liegt

- **Workforce management and quality:** Workforce management, automated quality scoring, real-time coaching, recording and analytics for voice and text. Since June 2026, also Workforce, Quality and Desktop Intelligence. We have no WFM and no live dashboard.
- **Runs alongside any contact center:** Verint advertises integration with all major platforms, naming Genesys, NICE CXone, Five9, Amazon Connect and Talkdesk. The customer doesn't have to replace anything. Verint records Teams calls with "Certified for Microsoft Teams".
- **Certifications:** SOC 2 Type II, PCI DSS Level 1, HIPAA, FedRAMP, and in the Trust Center also ISO 27001:2022 (certificate issued to Calabrio). We have no certifications of our own.
- **Broad Agent Assist:** Individual bots for after-call work, knowledge suggestions, real-time coaching, transcription and redaction. Our Agent Assist covers drafts, summaries and live transcription.
- **Large existing customers:** A US utility with 1,800 contact center agents uses four Verint bots. Plus insurers such as Nationale-Nederlanden (workforce management) and, in Germany, Sky (knowledge management).

## Wo Verint schwächer ist

- **No email automation:** The public pages on email contain only one sentence about customer expectations, no AI feature. Bots and Agent Factory are described for voice and digital channels. Anyone who wants to automate written correspondence mainly gets routing and Agent Assist from Verint.
- **Restructuring after acquisition:** New owner, interim CEO and merger with Calabrio within one year. Verint says "we're not migrating anyone". Info-Tech warns that "no forced migration" does not mean "no later consolidation" and sees Verint as more of a latecomer in agentic AI.
- **Many individual parts, hard to calculate:** According to Verint, each bot does exactly one task and is booked individually. Prices are not public. A user on AWS Marketplace calls the licensing model complex and expensive, especially for smaller contact centers.
- **Little evidence in DACH:** The references we found are mostly from the UK, the US and Benelux. In Germany, we only found Sky (knowledge management), no utility or insurer.

## Einwände

- **"We already use Verint for workforce management and quality."** That's good, Verint is strong there, and we don't have workforce management. For written correspondence that an AI should handle all the way into the target system, we are the better conversation alongside it rather than as a replacement.
- **"Verint now has AI agents too, with Agent Factory."** True, since June 2026. They are described for voice and digital channels, and we have not found automated handling of incoming emails all the way to the booking. Ask Verint for a reference for exactly that.
- **"Verint has SOC 2 and ISO 27001, you don't."** That's a fair point. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress for us, and our data center operator Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. On the other hand, we are a German GmbH without investors, store data in Germany, and our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are publicly available.
