## Erster Satz

"Cresta is strong at supporting agents live on the phone, and email is where we come in: with us, the AI resolves the case on its own, all the way to the booking in the target system, with data in Germany."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Cresta comes from real-time support for agents on the phone: in-call guidance, coaching, conversation analytics and quality scoring. On top of that came autonomous AI agents for phone and chat, and in October 2026 the Deputy Agent for back-office workflows, still in limited availability. Cresta builds on existing contact center systems such as Genesys, NICE or Five9, and its customers are almost exclusively large US enterprises. For email, the only publicly documented offering is reply drafts for agents, and the servers are in the US.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI all the way to the booking:** With enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, books in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. Cresta added email to Agent Assist and conversation analytics in August 2025: the AI writes drafts that agents use. Cresta does not describe autonomous email handling on its own product page.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** enneo handles a meter reading, a new IBAN and a new installment from one email in a single pass with one reply, and reads PDFs and photos along with it. For Cresta, neither is documented for email.
- **Data in Germany:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany, on-premises if requested. Cresta states in its privacy policy that the servers are in the US.
- **One product with its own agent workspace:** With enneo, ticket workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one system. Cresta needs an existing contact center system underneath. We did not find a ticket workspace of its own.
- **For utilities in addition:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. We did not find a Cresta reference among utilities or in the DACH region.

## Wo Cresta vorn liegt

- **Live support on the phone:** Agentic Assist gives agents guidance, knowledge, suggested replies and summaries during the call. Plus coaching, real-time translation and a Knowledge Agent. This is Cresta's core and much deeper than our Agent Assist.
- **Analytics and quality for every conversation:** According to Cresta, Conversation Intelligence and Quality Management automatically score 100% of conversations, and an AI Analyst answers questions across all conversations. We have scorecards, but not such broad analytics.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001, 27701, 42001, SOC 2, PCI DSS, HIPAA and TISAX. We have no certifications of our own.
- **Fits large contact center systems:** Ready-made integrations with NICE CXone, Genesys Cloud, Five9, Amazon Connect, Twilio, LivePerson and Salesforce, also sold through AWS and Google Cloud Marketplace. Anyone who has one of these doesn't need to replace anything.
- **Size and voicebot:** More than USD 280m in funding, customers such as United Airlines, Marriott, CVS Health and Cox. According to Cresta, the AI agent speaks more than 30 languages including German and is tested against synthetic customers built from real conversations.

## Wo Cresta schwächer ist

- **Hardly any presence in the DACH market:** No reference found in Germany, energy or German-speaking insurance. The only EMEA customer mentioned is an unnamed airline. In 2025, Cresta was only just looking for its first "Head of EMEA" in London.
- **Data in the US:** The privacy policy names servers in the US, and the language models come from OpenAI and Anthropic, among others. We did not find EU data residency.
- **Email is a side issue:** Cresta itself puts email at just under 17% of support volume and publicly offers only drafts and analytics for it. The Deputy Agent for back-office workflows has only been available since October 2026 and, according to the website, in limited availability.
- **Expensive and complex to set up:** Agent Assist alone costs USD 150,000 per year according to AWS Marketplace. Users on G2 criticize inaccurate knowledge suggestions and suggestions that don't fit the conversation, and that the AI needs time to understand the business.

## Einwände

- **"Cresta is funded by Sequoia and a16z and works with United Airlines and Marriott."** True, Cresta is large and well positioned with US enterprises. We did not find a reference among utilities or in the DACH region, so ask specifically about that.
- **"Cresta has ISO 27001 and SOC 2, you don't."** That is a real advantage. On our side, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, your data stays with us in Germany, while at Cresta it sits on servers in the US according to the privacy policy.
- **"We have Genesys, Cresta just plugs right in."** For live support on the phone, that's a good route. For emails that an AI should resolve on its own all the way into the target system, enneo alongside it is the better conversation, because Cresta publicly offers only drafts for agents there.

## Fazit

Against Cresta, we don't win on live support on the phone, conversation analytics or certifications. Cresta is deeper and larger there. We can win with customers in the DACH region who want to handle a lot of written correspondence fully automatically, need their data in Germany and want utility workflows ready out of the box.
