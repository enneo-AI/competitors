## Erster Satz

"Wonderful builds very good phone agents for large enterprises, backed by a lot of money and their own on-site teams, and we bring the ready-made utility workflows and the workspace for your team right out of the box, stored in Germany."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Wonderful is the fastest-growing AI agent company in European customer service: founded in 2025, valued at USD 5bn in September 2026, 650 employees in more than 35 markets. The model is engineering teams that build every agent and every integration on site at the customer. All publicly described customer projects are phone and WhatsApp, for example a utility in Romania handling billing questions. We found no agent workspace for employees, no email reference and no reference in Germany.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **One system with a workspace:** At enneo, the ticketing workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one product with skill-based routing. In its references, Wonderful hands off to the existing contact center, for example Genesys or Amazon Connect. We found no ticketing or inbox for the team at Wonderful.
- **Email through to the booking, multiple requests:** enneo reads the email, identifies the customer, books in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired and with multiple requests in one pass. Wonderful lists email as a channel but only shows phone and WhatsApp projects. Nothing is documented on multiple requests in one email.
- **Ready-made workflows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo, only the ERP integration is built. At Wonderful, an on-site team builds every workflow from scratch. At the Romanian utility, the first version took four weeks.
- **Stored in Germany:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in data centers in Germany. Per its subprocessor list, Wonderful uses AWS, Google and Azure in the USA, EU and India, and Anthropic in the USA only. Which region applies to a given customer is not public.
- **A fit for mid-sized companies:** enneo bills in packages per ticket and voice minute. Wonderful sells annual contracts to large enterprises, listed on AWS Marketplace at USD 2.5m for 12 months.

## Wo Wonderful vorn liegt

- **Money and speed:** USD 286m raised through the Series B, then a USD 550m Series C at a USD 5bn valuation, 650 employees. Plus partnerships with McKinsey, Anthropic and KPN Ventures, and FC Bayern as official partner.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, BSI C5, ISO 42001, PCI DSS and more, audited in 2026. We have none of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress.
- **Phone with proven numbers:** Utility in Romania: handling time down from 6 to 1.5 minutes, 77% resolved without agents. Telefónica Colombia 91.5%, OTE in Greece (a Deutsche Telekom subsidiary) with a Genesys integration. Many speech services to choose from (ElevenLabs, Cartesia, Deepgram, Speechmatics, Soniox). Our voicebot runs on OpenAI or Deepslate.
- **On-site teams:** Engineers and deployment leads work at the customer, build integrations and then hand over. In DACH, per the careers page, in Munich, Vienna and Zurich, and per Wonderful with Swiss retailers, insurers and telcos as partners.
- **Deployment options:** Multi-tenant, single tenant, in the customer's cloud, or on-premises fully air-gapped. Plus an AI Gateway for model choice, cost and permissions across the whole enterprise.

## Wo Wonderful schwächer ist

- **No reference found in Germany:** Public projects are in Romania, Greece, Colombia, Turkey and Israel. For Switzerland, Wonderful only names industries without customer names. FC Bayern is a partner, we found no customer reference in DACH.
- **Dependent on the on-site team:** Every agent and every integration is built by Wonderful engineers at the customer. How quickly the customer can keep building on their own afterwards, and what the teams cost, is not public.
- **Many data paths:** 18 subprocessors, including Anthropic in the USA only, Alibaba Cloud in China and Singapore, and several speech services. The DPA lists "Training Data" as a data category used to improve the service. What applies to a given customer has to be clarified one by one by data protection.
- **No review platform:** We found no publicly readable user reviews on OMR or G2.

## Einwände

- **"Wonderful is everywhere right now, with a 5 billion valuation."** True, Wonderful is growing very fast and has strong phone references. For a German utility, what counts is what is ready on day one: with us, meter reading, installment and bank details are already built, and the team works in the same system.
- **"Wonderful has BSI C5 and ISO 27001, you don't."** That is a real lead, our ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, we store data in Germany with a German provider instead of choosing regions across several US clouds.
- **"Wonderful builds everything for us on site, that's convenient."** An on-site team is convenient as long as it is there. With us, your people describe agents in plain language and change them with Neo via chat, without waiting for the vendor's engineers.

## Fazit

Against Wonderful, we don't win on financial strength, certifications or phone references at large enterprises. We can win with utilities and insurers in DACH that handle a lot of written correspondence, need a workspace for their team and want ready-made workflows instead of a build project. The smaller the customer, the less Wonderful's enterprise model with on-site teams fits.
