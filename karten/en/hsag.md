## Erster Satz

"hsag knows the processes of municipal utilities very well, and emails are exactly where we come in: our AI handles multiple requests and attachments in one pass through to posting, in one system with your agents' workspace."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

hsag is first and foremost a service provider for utilities: case processing, billing, consulting and outsourcing with more than 700 employees. Its AI comes from the chatbot Isa, which hsag has been selling to municipal utilities for years. That grew into the AI Suite with four modules: izy for phone, isa for chat, mail-in for email and enna as an assistant for agents. Almost nothing is public about hosting, language models, multiple requests, attachments or the agent workspace, and that is exactly where we come in.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI end to end:** With enneo, the AI identifies the request and the customer, verifies identity, posts in the ERP and replies, fully automatically if desired. hsag describes mail-in as classifying, routing, extracting business data and handling "routine cases autonomously". How far this goes in the target system is not publicly described.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email, plus a meter photo: enneo handles it in one pass with one reply. We found nothing on this for hsag.
- **One product with a workspace:** Ticketing, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one system with skill-based routing at enneo. hsag documents no workspace of its own. In 2021 the chatbot handed conversations to agents via the third-party product iAdvize.
- **Data protection you can read up on:** enneo stores data with Hetzner in Germany, and the DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. hsag names neither the storage location nor the model provider.

## Wo HSAG AI Suite vorn liegt

- **People on top:** With more than 700 employees, hsag also takes over customer service, billing and receivables management as outsourcing. A utility can buy AI and case processing from a single source. We don't offer that.
- **Access to municipal utilities:** Isa is listed in the SAP Store, and the AI Suite has been in the rku.it NextGen Market (more than 130 customers) since July 2026. Plus partnerships with Kraftwerk, powercloud and partners around the Thüga billing platform.
- **Ready-made industry content:** According to ZfK, Isa already knew about 280 typical utility requests in 2021. hsag names integrations with SAP, MBS, SIV, powercloud and Schleupen and closing tariff contracts in the chat.
- **Certificates:** ISO 27001:2022, ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and IDW PS 951. We have none of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are under review.
- **Long in the market:** The chatbot has been running at municipal utilities for years, for example at e.wa riss, handling about 25% of requests directly in the bot.

## Wo HSAG AI Suite schwächer ist

- **Hardly any technical details public:** The product page says nothing about storage location, data center or language models, and there is no DPA and no list of subprocessors. hsag does not say whether the ISO certificate covers the AI Suite.
- **New modules without proof:** Public references exist only for the chatbot. For mail-in, izy and enna we found no named customer, no documentation and no release notes.
- **No workspace of its own documented:** No ticketing system is described for cases the AI doesn't close. For chat, the handover to agents ran through a third-party product in 2021.
- **Energy only:** The suite is explicitly built for utilities. Nothing is described for other business units of a municipal utility, such as public pools or local transit.

## Einwände

- **"hsag has known the energy industry for 20 years."** True, and as a service provider hsag can even take over case processing. Our AI was also built first for utilities, with ready-made agents for meter reading, installment and bank details. Ask both of us to show how a real email with three requests and a photo is processed.
- **"hsag is cheaper."** Could be, hsag makes a separate offer for every customer. Compare the full scope: with us, workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one system, billed per ticket and voice minute.
- **"hsag is ISO 27001 certified, you aren't."** That's a real lead. With us, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are under review, and Hetzner is certified. Ask hsag about scope, storage location and model provider. With us, all of that is public in the DPA, TOMs and subprocessor list.

## Fazit

Against hsag we don't win on industry proximity, certificates or the option to outsource case processing as well. We can win where a lot of written correspondence should be handled automatically: multiple requests, attachments, posting in the target system, a workspace in the same system and transparent data protection. Where hsag only provides the chatbot, enneo is the bigger conversation for email, phone and workspace.
