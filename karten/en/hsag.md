## Erster Satz

"hsag knows the processes of municipal utilities very well, and emails are exactly where we come in: our AI handles multiple requests and attachments in one pass through to posting, in one system with your agents' workspace."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

hsag is first and foremost a service provider for utilities: case processing, billing, consulting and outsourcing with over 620 employees. The AI comes from the chatbot Isa, which hsag has been selling to municipal utilities for years. This has grown into the AI Suite with four modules: izy for phone, isa for chat, mail-in for email and enna as an assistant for agents. There is almost nothing public on hosting, language models, multiple requests, attachments and the agent workspace, and that's exactly where we come in.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI across the full flow:** At enneo, the AI detects requests and customers, verifies them, posts in the ERP and replies, fully automated if desired. hsag describes mail-in as classifying, routing, extracting domain data and handling "routine cases independently". How far this goes in the target system is not publicly described.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email, plus a meter photo: enneo handles this in one pass with one reply. We found nothing on this at hsag.
- **One product with an agent workspace:** At enneo, ticketing, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one system with skill-based routing. hsag has no documented agent workspace of its own. In 2021, the chatbot handed conversations to agents via the third-party product iAdvize.
- **Privacy you can read up on:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany, and DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. hsag names neither the storage location nor the model provider.

## Wo HSAG AI Suite vorn liegt

- **People on top:** With over 620 employees, hsag also takes over customer service, billing and receivables management as outsourcing. A utility can buy AI and case processing from a single source. We don't offer that.
- **Access to municipal utilities:** Isa is listed in the SAP Store, and since July 2026 the AI Suite is in the rku.it NextGen Market (over 130 customers). Plus partnerships with Kraftwerk, powercloud and partners on the Thüga billing platform.
- **Ready-made industry content:** According to ZfK, Isa already knew around 280 typical utility requests in 2021. hsag names integrations with SAP, MBS, SIV, powercloud and Schleupen, and closing tariff contracts in chat.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001:2022, ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and IDW PS 951. We have none of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress.
- **Long in the market:** The chatbot has been running at municipal utilities for years, for example at e.wa riss with around 25% of requests resolved directly in the bot.

## Wo HSAG AI Suite schwächer ist

- **Hardly any technical detail public:** Not a word on storage location, data center or language models on the product page, no DPA and no list of subprocessors. hsag doesn't say whether the ISO certificate covers the AI Suite.
- **New modules without evidence:** Public references exist only for the chatbot. For mail-in, izy and enna, we found no named customer, no documentation and no release notes.
- **No agent workspace of its own documented:** No ticketing system is described for cases the AI doesn't close. In chat, the handover to agents ran via a third-party product in 2021.
- **Energy only:** The suite is explicitly built for utilities. Nothing is described for other business units of a municipal utility, such as swimming pools or public transport.

## Fazit

We won't win against hsag on industry proximity, certifications or the offer to outsource case processing along with it. We can win where a lot of written correspondence should be handled automatically: multiple requests, attachments, posting in the target system, agent workspace in the same system and transparent privacy. Where hsag only provides the chatbot, enneo is the bigger conversation for email, phone and agent workspace.
