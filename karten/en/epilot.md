## Erster Satz

"epilot is strong for portals, sales journeys and processes at municipal utilities, and we come in at the inbox: with us, the AI handles the customer email itself, all the way to the booking in the billing system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

epilot is a platform for utilities and grid operators: product catalog, order journeys, processes, customer portal, partner portal and customer management. In this area, epilot is widely used at municipal utilities, which means exactly our customers. For customer service there is an email inbox with AI: summary, labels, reply suggestions, action suggestions and an AI mail agent, orchestrated by the AI layer "Luna". Our advantage lies in handling all the way to the action in the billing system, in additional channels and in the voicebot.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **The full process instead of a suggestion:** enneo reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, executes the action in the ERP and replies, also fully automatically. At epilot, according to their help center, the AI drafts a reply that agents review and send "with one click". AI replies and action suggestions are listed there as beta.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply. We found nothing on this at epilot.
- **Attachments and photos:** enneo reads PDFs and photos, e.g. a meter photo. epilot has a feature that fills attributes from documents. Whether the mail agent evaluates attachments in customer service is not stated in the help center.
- **All channels and voicebot in one system:** At enneo, email, letter, chat, WhatsApp, Telegram, phone and voicebot belong to one agent workspace with skill-based routing. For customer service, epilot names email and self-service in the portal. We found no phone, chat or voicebot.
- **Ready-made processes all the way into the ERP:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo, the ERP connection is built per customer. For third-party systems, epilot only mentions "flexible data integration". Booking by the AI in the billing system is not described.

## Wo epilot vorn liegt

- **Portals and order journeys:** Customer portal for residential and business customers, partner portal, no-code order journeys, product catalog with pricing logic, quotes and documents. We don't have that, we have no help center for end customers.
- **Many municipal utilities as customers:** According to the website more than 200 energy companies, logos include RheinEnergie, Stadtwerke München, badenova, enercity, Gelsenwasser, Stadtwerke Bochum. Where epilot is already in place, the AI inbox is just a switch away.
- **Processes for sales and grid:** Processes for commodity, non-commodity and grid, validated templates (Blueprints), automatic assignment of orders to partners, Data Lake for analytics.
- **Certification:** ISO 27001 and Trusted Cloud seal. We have no certification of our own.

## Wo epilot schwächer ist

- **AI in service still young:** In 2024, epilot was only developing reply drafts. In the help center, AI replies and action suggestions are listed as beta, and an agent sends the reply.
- **Only email in service:** We found nothing at epilot for phone, chat or voicebot. A utility needs another system for that.
- **Billing system connection open:** Booking by the AI in SAP IS-U, Schleupen or Wilken is not described, only "flexible data integration" and APIs from the Professional package upward.
- **Vague data protection statements:** The security page names data centers in Germany, the AI page says "All data stays in Europe". epilot does not name its language model providers.

## Einwände

- **"We already have epilot, and it does AI now too."** That's true, epilot summarizes emails and suggests replies that your agents send. With us, the AI books the request in the billing system and replies itself, even with several requests in one email. epilot remains a good fit alongside for portal and order journeys.
- **"epilot knows the energy industry, you're smaller."** Both of us specialize in utilities. We're happy to show you our ready-made agents for meter reading, installment and bank details with plausibility checks, using your own emails.
- **"epilot is ISO certified."** That is a real advantage. At enneo, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public.
