## Erster Satz

"Onlim has a lot of experience with chat and phone bots for utilities. Emails are where we come in: with us, one AI handles everything itself, all the way to posting in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Onlim is a specialist for chatbots and voicebots, with long experience at utilities in Austria. Wien Energie's bot BotTina has been running since 2017. Onlim offers public entry-level pricing, a fresh ISO 27001 certification and, since 2026, outbound phone campaigns. Onlim names email as a channel but does not describe how handling works, and an agent workspace exists only as a live chat.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI through to posting:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, posts in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. Onlim only names email as a channel ("Automate your email traffic"). We found nothing on how handling works.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles all of it in one pass with one reply, and the AI reads a meter photo in the attachment along with it. At Onlim, neither is documented.
- **One agent workspace for everything:** Ticketing, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one system with skill-based routing at enneo. Onlim hands over to agents via a live chat for website and Facebook Messenger. Onlim does not describe ticketing or an inbox.
- **Data protection documents public:** Our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are available, and data is stored at Hetzner in Germany. At Onlim, the DPA is available on request, and subprocessors, data center operator and language model provider are not public.

## Wo Onlim vorn liegt

- **Experience with utilities in Austria:** BotTina at Wien Energie since 2017, according to Onlim the first chatbot of a utility in the German-speaking region. Plus Wiener Netze, Energie Graz, Energienetze Steiermark, e-netze, Energie Klagenfurt and a grid operator from Braunschweig with meter reading via voicebot.
- **Public pricing and quick entry:** Packages from €149 per month with fixed voice minutes, additional minute €0.28 to €0.30, minimum term 3 months, go-live within a few weeks according to Onlim.
- **Outbound phone campaigns:** Since early 2026, Onlim offers voice agents for outbound campaigns, for example appointment scheduling, lead qualification and surveys, with results written back to the CRM. enneo has no dialer for outbound campaigns.
- **Certification:** ISO 27001:2022 since May 2026 and Cyber Trust Austria. We have no certification of our own.
- **Languages and voices on the phone:** According to Onlim over 20 languages, voices via Amazon Polly, Google or Microsoft Azure. We have less choice of speech providers.

## Wo Onlim schwächer ist

- **Email barely described:** Email is on the channel list, but there is no dedicated page, process or reference for email handling. All case studies concern chat or phone.
- **No ticketing system of its own:** Whatever the bot doesn't resolve goes to a live chat or to the appropriate team. According to the website, the customer needs a second system for emails and cases with follow-ups.
- **Data protection only partly documented:** Subprocessors, data center operator and language models are not public, and the EU AI Act statement is linked as a preliminary version. The FAQ also says there are no standard packages for chatbot projects, while the package page lists fixed prices.
- **Few user voices:** On OMR Reviews, Onlim does not have a single review yet.

## Einwände

- **"Onlim has been running at Wien Energie for years."** That's true, Onlim has long experience with chatbots for utilities. Ask whether emails are also handled automatically all the way into the billing system there. We found no such reference.
- **"Onlim starts at €149 a month."** For simple FAQs and standard appointments, that's a good entry point. For emails with customer identification, posting in the ERP and an agent workspace for your team, please compare against the Enterprise package plus the additional ticketing system.
- **"Onlim is ISO certified, you're not."** That's a real lead. With us, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, while at Onlim subprocessors and language models are not visible.

## Fazit

We don't win against Onlim on entry price, certification or outbound campaigns, and Onlim has the longer history with chatbots for Austrian utilities. We can win where a lot of written correspondence should be resolved automatically all the way into the target system, and where bot and agent workspace should be one system. Where Onlim already runs as a chat or voicebot, enneo for email alongside it is often the better conversation than a complete switch.
