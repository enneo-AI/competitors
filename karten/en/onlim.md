## Erster Satz

"Onlim has a lot of experience with chat and phone bots for utilities. Where we come in is email: with us, one AI handles everything itself, through to posting in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Onlim is a specialist in chatbots and voicebots, with long experience at utilities in Austria. Wien Energie's bot BotTina has been running since 2017. Onlim offers public entry prices, a fresh ISO 27001 certificate and, since 2026, outbound phone campaigns. Onlim lists email as a channel but does not describe how it is handled, and an agent workspace exists only as a live chat.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI through to posting:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, posts in the target system and replies, also fully automated. Onlim only lists email as a channel ("Automate your email traffic"). We did not find how the handling works.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** enneo handles a meter reading, a new IBAN and a new installment in one email in a single pass with one reply, and the AI also reads a meter photo in the attachment. Nothing is documented on either at Onlim.
- **One agent workspace for everything:** Ticketing, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one system with skill-based routing at enneo. Onlim hands over to agents via a live chat for the website and Facebook Messenger. Onlim does not describe ticketing or an inbox.
- **Data protection documents public:** Our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are available, and data is stored at Hetzner in Germany. At Onlim, the DPA is available on request, subprocessors and data center operator are not public, and language models are named only occasionally.

## Wo Onlim vorn liegt

- **Experience with utilities in Austria:** BotTina at Wien Energie since 2017, according to Onlim the first chatbot of a utility in the German-speaking region. Plus Wiener Netze, Energie Graz, Energienetze Steiermark, e-netze, Energie Klagenfurt and a grid operator from Braunschweig with meter readings via voicebot.
- **Public pricing and quick entry:** Packages from €149 per month with fixed voice minutes, additional minute €0.28 to €0.30, minimum term 3 months, go-live within a few weeks according to Onlim.
- **Outbound phone campaigns:** Since early 2026, Onlim offers voice agents for outbound campaigns, for example appointment scheduling, lead qualification and surveys, with results written back to the CRM. enneo has no dialer for outbound campaigns.
- **Certification:** ISO 27001:2022 since May 2026 and Cyber Trust Austria. We have no certification of our own.
- **Languages and voices on the phone:** According to Onlim, more than 20 languages, voices via Amazon Polly, Google or Microsoft Azure. We have less choice of speech providers.

## Wo Onlim schwächer ist

- **Email barely described:** Email is in the channel list, but there is no dedicated page, process or reference for email handling. All case studies concern chat or phone.
- **No ticketing system of its own:** Anything the bot can't resolve goes into a live chat or to the relevant team. According to the website, the customer needs a second system for emails and cases with follow-ups.
- **Data protection only partly documented:** Subprocessors and data center operator are not public, a list of language models is missing, and the EU AI Act statement is linked as a preliminary version. The FAQ also says there are no standard packages for chatbot projects, while the package page lists fixed prices.
- **Few user reviews:** Onlim has not a single review on OMR Reviews so far.

## Einwände

- **"Onlim has been running at Wien Energie for years."** True, Onlim has long experience with chatbots for utilities. Ask whether emails there are also handled automatically through to the billing system. We did not find such a reference.
- **"Onlim starts at €149 a month."** For simple FAQs and standard appointments, that's a good entry point. For emails with customer identification, posting in the ERP and an agent workspace for your team, please compare against the Enterprise package plus the additional ticketing system.
- **"Onlim is ISO certified, you are not."** That's a real advantage. Our ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. On the other hand, our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, while at Onlim no list of subprocessors and language models is available.

## Fragen an den Kunden

- **How does Onlim handle incoming emails for you, through to posting in the billing system?** Expected answer: unclear, the bot runs mainly in chat and on the phone. Transition: "With us, the same AI handles that from intake to reply, also with multiple requests and attachments."

## Fazit

We don't win against Onlim on entry price, certification or outbound campaigns, and Onlim has the longer history with chatbots for Austrian utilities. We can win where a lot of written correspondence needs to be handled automatically through to the target system and bot and agent workspace should be one system. Where Onlim already runs as a chat or voicebot, adding enneo for email alongside it is often the better conversation than a complete switch.
