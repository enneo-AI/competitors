## Erster Satz

"Lime Connect is strong in website chat and WhatsApp and inexpensive to start with. enneo comes in for emails and letters all the way to the booking in the billing system, and on the phone."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Lime Connect comes from website chat and is now a messaging platform: chat, WhatsApp, social channels and email flow into a shared inbox, the Message Center. The AI agent mainly answers knowledge questions and can query third-party systems via MCP and API Actions. Email is one channel among many, and AI handling of an email all the way to the booking in the billing system is not publicly described. Lime is inexpensive to start with and widespread among municipal utilities in chat. Our advantage lies in written correspondence and on the phone.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI all the way to the booking:** With enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, books in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. At Lime, emails arrive in the Message Center via forwarding. According to the Lime FAQ, the new AI agent can handle email, but the documentation only describes it in the website widget and in WhatsApp.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** enneo handles a meter reading, a new IBAN and a new installment in one email in a single pass with one reply, and the AI reads PDFs and meter photos along with it. At Lime, neither is documented for the AI agent.
- **Ready-made workflows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo, and we build the ERP integration per customer. For utilities, Lime mentions an FAQ chatbot and meter readings via a WhatsApp form. Lime does not mention any integration with billing systems.
- **APIs without limits:** With enneo, integrations are built as code, in any format and with any logic. Lime connects systems via API Actions and MCP, with an auth token and without OAuth for the MCP server. According to Lime, setting up your own MCP server requires IT and programming skills.
- **Phone in the same product:** enneo has telephony and a voicebot, with its own line or the customer's SIP trunk. According to the documentation, Lime's phone channel is in alpha and not publicly available.

## Wo Lime Connect vorn liegt

- **Messaging channels:** Website chat, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, SMS, Telegram, Threema, video calls and its own mobile app in one inbox. Plus WhatsApp campaigns for outbound messages.
- **Entry point and price:** Free plan, Pro from €87 per month, AI agent from €99 per month with 100 conversations and a trial without going through sales. Unused conversations roll over for the term of the contract.
- **References with municipal utilities:** Eight utilities on the industry page. According to Lime, Allgäuer Überlandwerk resolves more than 90% of requests in chat.
- **Satisfied users:** 4.5 out of 5 stars from 211 reviews on OMR, with ease of use and support praised most often.

## Wo Lime Connect schwächer ist

- **AI under reconstruction:** The previous AI Automation Hub, developed with a partner and without email, will be shut down on Dec 31, 2026. Customers have to switch to the new Connect AI.
- **Thin documentation on the AI agent:** Channels, handoff to agents and limits are barely described in the documentation. What a conversation beyond the 100 included ones costs is not stated on the pricing page.
- **Long messages:** Users on OMR report that the bot struggles with long or unclearly worded messages. The price and how it's made up of add-on modules are the most common point of criticism.
- **Little transparency on subprocessors:** The hosting operator and AI providers are listed neither in the DPA nor on the AI info page.

## Einwände

- **"Lime is much cheaper, there's even a free plan."** That's true for the entry point. Ask what an AI conversation beyond the 100 included ones costs, because that's not on the pricing page. And factor in who resolves the emails all the way to the booking, because for utilities that's usually where the biggest effort lies.
- **"Lime has many municipal utilities as references."** That's true, in chat. The references are about chat. Lime does not mention any integration with billing systems, while at enneo meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made specialist agents.
- **"We already have Lime for chat."** Then Lime can stay there, and enneo takes over written correspondence and, if you want, the phone. Lime is shutting down the old AI Automation Hub on Dec 31, 2026, so a migration is coming up anyway.

## Fazit

When it comes to website chat, WhatsApp and a low-cost entry point, Lime Connect is strong and well known among municipal utilities. We can win as soon as a lot of email and letters should be handled automatically: multiple requests, attachments, booking in the billing system, plus phone in the same product. Where Lime is already running in chat, enneo for written correspondence is often the more fitting conversation than a switch.
