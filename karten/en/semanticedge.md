## Erster Satz

"SemanticEdge has a lot of experience with large hotlines on the phone, and that is exactly where we come in alongside: with the emails and letters an AI handles on its own at enneo, all the way to the booking in the target system, in one system with your agents' workspace."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

SemanticEdge has been building speech dialogue systems for hotlines since 2000 and is well established with banks, insurers and utilities in the German market. Its offering is voice and chat assistants that are built into the customer's existing telephony or contact center platform, plus development and operation as a service. We did not find email handling or a dedicated agent workspace on the website. SemanticEdge is ahead on the phone and in on-premises operation, our advantage lies in written correspondence and one end-to-end system.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Written correspondence through to action:** At enneo, the AI reads the email or letter, identifies and authenticates the customer, executes the action in the ERP and replies, also fully automatically. SemanticEdge lists phone, chat and SMS as channels, not email.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** Meter reading, new IBAN and installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply. The AI also reads PDF attachments and meter photos. We found nothing on this at SemanticEdge.
- **One system instead of a building block:** Agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one product with skill-based routing at enneo. According to its website, SemanticEdge is integrated via the existing telephony or contact center platform and hands over to agents there.
- **Change AI agents yourself:** At enneo, AI agents are described in plain language, changed with Neo via chat and checked with test cases. SemanticEdge offers development, tuning and optimization as a service. The business department maintains FAQs, routing and callback times itself.
- **Data protection reviewable upfront:** Our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, data is stored at Hetzner in Germany. At SemanticEdge, hosting location, subprocessors and certificates are not publicly stated.

## Wo SemanticEdge vorn liegt

- **Long experience on the phone:** Speech dialogue systems since 2000, according to the website "hundreds of million conversations". Request catalogs with over 2,500 (insurance) and over 3,000 (banking) requests, speech models trained on 100,000 real customer utterances per industry.
- **Installed base at large hotlines:** Sparkassen, Commerzbank, R+V, Allianz and Westnetz in productive use for many years according to the website. For utilities, among others meter reading reports, bill changes, status and contract information, document dispatch.
- **On-premises operation:** Can run entirely in the customer's data center, also with specially fine-tuned language models. At enneo, this is only possible at large volume.
- **Authentication and callback:** 16 freely configurable authentication procedures in banking. A callback assistant schedules callbacks and works with workforce management or calendar systems. enneo has no workforce management.
- **Control instead of an unconstrained language model:** According to its trade fair profile, the "AI Agent Platform" combines language models with a rule-based architecture ("Control-by-Design"). Existing NLUs and knowledge systems such as Confluence or Serviceware Knowledge can be integrated.

## Wo SemanticEdge schwächer ist

- **Written channels not visible:** The website and solution pages list phone, chat and SMS. We did not find email, letters, attachments or multiple requests in one message.
- **Little public evidence:** No dated customer announcements, no release notes, no documentation. Since 08/2025 the news section contains only essays on superintelligence and AGI. The key figures on the industry pages have neither time period nor baseline. We did not find reviews on G2 or OMR.
- **Data protection and security not documented:** Hosting location, subprocessors, DPA and certificates are not publicly stated. As language models the site names GPT and Gemini among others, without stating the region.
- **Dependent on services:** Development, tuning and optimization run as project and service work. The website only describes building and testing on your own for FAQs, routing and callback settings.

## Einwände

- **"SemanticEdge has been running in our hotline for years."** That speaks for SemanticEdge on the phone, and nobody needs to replace it. For emails and letters that an AI should handle all the way into the target system, enneo alongside is often the better conversation.
- **"SemanticEdge has 25 years of experience, you are younger."** On the phone, that is true. For written requests with multiple topics and attachments we found nothing there, and that is exactly our core. Have both shown to you with your own sample emails.
- **"SemanticEdge can run in our data center."** That is a real advantage, with us that is only possible at large volume. In return, our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, and data is stored at Hetzner in Germany.
