## Erster Satz

"Synthflow is strong on the phone and connects to many phone systems. We come in where emails and letters should be handled along with calls, all the way through to posting in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Synthflow is a pure voicebot platform from Berlin: AI agents answer calls, place outbound calls, book appointments and hand over to humans by call transfer. Its strengths are connections to phone systems such as Zoom, Five9, RingCentral, 8x8 and Genesys, the choice of voices, and testing with simulations. We found no email channel and no agent workspace. Synthflow only sends emails as a summary after a call. We win where phone, email and letters should run in one system through to the action in the target system.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Written correspondence in the same AI:** At enneo, the AI reads emails and letters, identifies and verifies the customer, posts in the ERP and replies, fully automated if desired. It handles multiple requests and attachments such as a meter photo in one pass. According to its docs, Synthflow has no email channel, only a fixed summary after each call sent to up to 50 addresses.
- **Agent workspace included:** enneo comes with a ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot with skill-based routing in one product. Synthflow hands over to humans by transferring the call to a phone number or SIP destination, so agents continue working in another system.
- **WhatsApp without a US detour:** At enneo, WhatsApp runs in the same system, stored at Hetzner in Germany. At Synthflow, WhatsApp and SMS chat are only available in US workspaces according to the docs.
- **Ready-made flows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made specialist agents with plausibility checks at enneo, only the ERP connection is built. At Synthflow, we found neither templates nor references from energy or insurance.
- **Documents in the open:** Our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. At Synthflow, the terms and SLA for DACH are public, but according to Synthflow most documents in the Trust Center are only available on request with an NDA.

## Wo Synthflow vorn liegt

- **Phone system connections:** Ready-made connections to Zoom, Five9, RingCentral, 8x8, Twilio, Telnyx, Vonage and Freshcaller, plus Genesys Cloud, Talkdesk, Aircall and Asterisk via SIP. Context can be passed in SIP headers, with warm or cold transfers. Tools such as a SIP ladder diagram and call quality metrics help with troubleshooting.
- **Choice of voices and languages:** Over 30 languages, voices from ElevenLabs up to v4, Deepgram and its own speech services, recently ten native German voices. Plus outbound campaigns and navigation through other companies' IVR menus. We have less choice and no dialer.
- **Testing and evaluation:** Test Center with simulated conversations, custom evaluation criteria after every call, comparison of any two agent versions.
- **Certifications:** SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001 and PCI DSS. We have none of our own.
- **Scale in voice:** According to Synthflow, over one million calls a day, partnership with 8x8 (April 2026), and at Freshworks up to 65% of routine calls automated according to the case study. White-label for BPOs and software vendors.

## Wo Synthflow schwächer ist

- **Phone and chat only:** Emails exist only as a summary after the call, always from `notifications@notify.synthflow.ai`, and according to the docs a custom sender domain is not supported yet. We found no letters and no inbound email.
- **EU operation with gaps:** WhatsApp and SMS chat only in US workspaces. When the old cluster is retired, customers who do not reply end up in the US cluster, and switching later is chargeable. With your own Twilio account, not all requests in the EU cluster stay in Europe.
- **Lots of change in a short time:** New customers only get enterprise contracts, and since Aug 25, 2026 all other accounts run only on its own Synthflow LLM. Salesforce must be reconnected by Nov 30, 2026, and the old cluster is retired at the end of 2026.
- **References far from our customers:** The case studies come mainly from US retail, software and real estate. We found none from energy, insurance or DACH. On Trustpilot (4.1 from 215 reviews, few recent), users complain about billing and slow support.

## Einwände

- **"Synthflow is also from Berlin and SOC 2 and ISO certified."** True, the certifications are a real lead, ours (ISO 27001 and SOC 2) are in progress. But Synthflow's data sits at AWS, and WhatsApp is only available in the US cluster. With us, everything is at Hetzner in Germany, and our documents are public without an NDA.
- **"Synthflow has more voices and connects directly to our phone system."** That's right, Synthflow is broader on the phone. Ask what happens after the handover and who handles the emails. With us, calls, emails, letters and the agent workspace run in one system.
- **"With Synthflow we only pay for call minutes."** For the phone, that's a fair model. For emails, letters and your agents' workspace, you need a second system alongside it. Compare the total cost with our package prices per ticket and voice minute.

## Fazit

Against Synthflow, we don't win on voices, phone system connections or certifications. We can win where there is a lot of written correspondence alongside calls, agents need a shared workspace, and data such as WhatsApp should stay in Germany. Anyone who only wants to put a voicebot in front of their existing phone system is well served by Synthflow.
