## Erster Satz

"Talkdesk is a strong contact center with workforce management and good certifications. It gets interesting with written correspondence: handling emails with multiple requests and attachments all the way to the booking."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Talkdesk is first and foremost a cloud contact center with a strong phone core, workforce management and reporting. Its "Autopilot" AI agents run over phone, chat and SMS, and according to Talkdesk also over email since February 2026, with actions in CRM and ERP. How good the email flow is can hardly be verified publicly: the help pages are not freely accessible, and attachments and multiple requests are not described anywhere. With C5, Frankfurt hosting and appearances in Berlin, Talkdesk is clearly targeting DACH.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI built as one piece, and provable:** At enneo, the same AI reads the email, identifies the customer, books in the target system and replies, also fully automated. Talkdesk announced email for Autopilot in February 2026. We have not found any public documentation or reference for the email flow. In the meeting, a live demo with a real energy email is worth it.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply. At Talkdesk, this is not described. The page talks about the "true intent" of an email.
- **Attachments and photos:** enneo reads PDFs and photos, e.g. a meter photo. Nothing on this is documented at Talkdesk.
- **Energy for DACH instead of the US:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. Talkdesk's industry package for utilities targets outages, billing questions, move-ins and move-outs and payments. It does not mention meter readings, and the only reference listed is a water utility in Las Vegas.
- **Letters as a channel:** enneo has letters as a dedicated channel with the same AI as email. We have not found a letter channel at Talkdesk.

## Wo Talkdesk vorn liegt

- **Full-breadth contact center:** Telephony, routing, Studio for flows, live dashboards, and from Elite upward workforce management, performance management and screen recording. We have neither workforce management nor live steering.
- **Voicebot:** Autonomous voice agents since March 2025, according to Talkdesk with 59+ languages, connection to CRM and other systems. We have less choice of voice providers.
- **Certifications:** BSI C5, ISO 27001, ISO 42001 and SOC 2 Type II. We have none of our own.
- **Ready-made industry packages:** Packages for finance, insurance, healthcare, retail, travel and utilities. The utilities package connects customer systems, outage management, geodata and meter data, and proactively reports known outages.
- **Presence in DACH:** Frankfurt hosting, sales team in Germany, reseller with existing customers. Talkdesk names Villeroy & Boch, Enpal and On as DACH customers.

## Wo Talkdesk schwächer ist

- **AI prices not public:** Licenses are published, Autopilot and Copilot only through sales. What the AI costs per conversation or email cannot be seen in advance.
- **Email AI is new and hard to verify:** Announced in February 2026, described in a press release and a blog post. The help pages are locked without login, and we have not found an email reference.
- **Utilities package built for the US market:** Geared toward outage reports and US systems, the named reference is a water utility in Las Vegas. Energy processes such as meter reading and installment are not described there.

## Einwände

- **"Talkdesk offers telephony, workforce management and AI from a single source."** True, Talkdesk is clearly ahead on phone system, workforce management and live steering. For written correspondence, the email AI has been announced since February 2026 and is barely documented publicly. enneo can run alongside Talkdesk for email and letters.
- **"Talkdesk has BSI C5 and SOC 2."** Correct, we don't have certifications of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress. We store data at Hetzner, a German provider, and our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are publicly available.
- **"Talkdesk has a ready-made package for utilities."** Yes, geared toward outages, billing questions, move-ins and move-outs and payments, with a reference from the US. Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made agents with plausibility checks at enneo.
