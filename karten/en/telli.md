## Erster Satz

"telli is quick to set up on the phone and really strong at outbound calls. We come in where your customers write: with us, an AI handles emails and letters through to posting in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

telli builds AI agents for the phone, inbound and outbound, for B2C companies from mid-sized businesses to large enterprises. Its strength is sales and outreach: dialer, lead qualification, appointment booking, reactivation. Its energy references are solar and heating providers, not utilities. We found no email channel and no agent workspace in the product or the docs, handover happens by transferring the call. Our advantage lies in written correspondence and in AI and team working in one system.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email and letters with AI:** enneo reads emails and letters, recognizes requests and customers, posts in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. telli names email in the press release on its funding, but the product pages and docs only show phone, SMS, WhatsApp and chat. We found no email channel.
- **One system for AI and team:** At enneo, agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one product with skill-based routing. telli transfers the call to a colleague and writes results to the CRM or helpdesk. We found no agent workspace of its own.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** enneo handles meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email in one pass with one reply, including PDF and photo attachments. Nothing on this is documented at telli.
- **Ready-made flows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. For energy, telli names billing questions and appointments, the page does not mention meter reading or installments.
- **Stored with a German provider:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany, with backups in Germany and Finland. telli names EU hosting, but neither operator nor region, the details are handled by sales.

## Wo telli vorn liegt

- **Outbound calls:** Auto-dialer with calling hours and retries, callbacks at preferred times, phone numbers per agent and brand, display of the verified company name to the person called. Use cases such as lead qualification, reactivation and payment reminders. We have no dialer for outbound campaigns.
- **Fast to go live and self-service:** Sign-up without sales, the assistant Charlie researches the company and builds a first agent. References cite go-live in two and a half to four weeks. Plus simulations with checks before go-live and a check for the AI disclosure under the EU AI Act.
- **Many ready-made integrations:** 61 integrations, including native Salesforce and HubSpot, calendars such as Calendly and Cal.com, contact centers such as Genesys, Five9, Avaya, Talkdesk and Amazon Connect, plus Make, n8n and Zapier. A browser agent operates systems without an interface. Our ecosystem of ready-made connectors is smaller.
- **Certification:** SOC 2 Type II with the report in the Trust Center, HIPAA-ready environments. We have no certifications of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress.
- **Choice of language models and voices:** OpenAI and Gemini, also with your own key, voices from ElevenLabs, Cartesia and others, your own voice via cloning. Our voicebot runs on OpenAI or Deepslate, with less choice.

## Wo telli schwächer ist

- **Only one channel in depth:** The docs describe the phone, plus SMS and WhatsApp as template messages. For email, letters and the work of agents, the customer needs a second system.
- **Little public on storage and models:** According to the security page, operator, region and model providers are handled by sales, publicly it only says that EU hosting is possible. Whether customer data is used for training is not stated there. The Trust Center could not be read without signing in.
- **Young company:** Founded in 2024, seed funding, references mainly from sales, real estate and recruiting. We found no utilities or large insurers among the references.
- **Limits according to users:** In reviews on OMR, users mention a lack of deep CRM integration, unreliable recognition of phone numbers and email addresses, and the effort for setup and fine-tuning.

## Einwände

- **"telli is faster to go live and cheaper."** For a first phone agent that may be true, telli is built for a fast start. But factor in what happens to emails, letters and the cases that end up with your team. With us, inbox, agent workspace and telephony are included in the product.
- **"telli has SOC 2, you don't."** That's a real lead, our ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, we say publicly where we store data, and our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are available to review.
- **"telli also has energy customers."** True, mainly solar and heating providers, mostly for sales and appointments. For utilities, we bring meter reading, installment and bank details ready-made with plausibility checks, over the phone and in written correspondence.

## Fragen an den Kunden

- **How much of your volume comes in by email and letter, and who handles it if telli only takes over the phone?** Expected answer: a dedicated team in another system. Transition: "With us, the same AI also handles emails and letters through to posting, in the same system."
- **Which system does your team keep working in when telli transfers a call?** Expected answer: the existing CRM or helpdesk. Transition: "With us, the agent workspace is part of the product, with inbox, telephony and skill-based routing."
- **Has telli confirmed to you in writing which region your calls are stored in and which language models run?** Expected answer: still open, being clarified with sales. Transition: "With us, that's public: Hetzner in Germany, EU models possible under the DPA."

## Fazit

Against telli, we don't win on outbound calls, fast self-service start or the number of ready-made integrations. We can win with utilities that have a lot of written correspondence: email and letters through to posting, multiple requests, ready-made utility flows and one workspace for AI and team in one product.
