## Erster Satz

"telli is quick to set up on the phone and really strong at outbound calls. Where we come in is where your customers write: with us, an AI handles emails and letters all the way to posting in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

telli builds AI agents for the phone, inbound and outbound, for B2C companies from mid-sized businesses to large enterprises. Its strength is sales and outreach: dialer, lead qualification, appointment booking, reactivation. Its energy references are solar and heating companies, not utilities. We found no email channel and no agent workspace in the product or docs, handover happens by transferring the call. Our advantage lies in written correspondence and in AI and team working in one system.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email and letters with AI:** enneo reads emails and letters, detects requests and customers, posts in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. telli mentions email in its funding press release, but its product pages and docs only show phone, SMS, WhatsApp and chat. We found no email channel.
- **One system for AI and team:** At enneo, agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge and telephony are one product with skill-based routing. telli transfers the call to a colleague and writes results to a CRM or helpdesk. We found no agent workspace of its own.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles all of it in one pass with one reply, including the attached PDF and photo. telli has documented nothing on this.
- **Ready-made workflows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. For energy, telli names billing questions and appointments, its page does not mention meter readings or installments.
- **Stored with a German provider:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany, with backups in Germany and Finland. telli names no storage location, hosting and region are clarified with its sales team.

## Wo telli vorn liegt

- **Outbound calls:** Auto-dialer with calling windows and retries, callbacks at the caller's preferred time, phone numbers per agent and brand, verified business name shown to the person called. Use cases such as lead qualification, reactivation and payment reminders. We have no dialer for outbound campaigns.
- **Fast to go live and self-serve:** Signup without sales, the assistant Charlie researches the company and builds a first agent. References cite go-live in two and a half to four weeks. Plus simulations with checks before going live and a check for the AI disclosure under the EU AI Act.
- **Many ready-made integrations:** 61 integrations, including native Salesforce and HubSpot, calendars such as Calendly and Cal.com, contact centers such as Genesys, Five9, Avaya, Talkdesk and Amazon Connect, plus Make, n8n and Zapier. A browser agent handles systems without an API. Our ecosystem of ready-made connectors is smaller.
- **Certification:** SOC 2 Type II with a report in the Trust Center, HIPAA-ready deployments. We have no certifications of our own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress.
- **Choice of language models and voices:** OpenAI and Gemini, also with your own key, voices from ElevenLabs, Cartesia and others, custom voice via cloning. Our voicebot runs on OpenAI or Deepslate, with less choice.

## Wo telli schwächer ist

- **Only one channel in depth:** The docs describe the phone, plus SMS and WhatsApp as template messages. For email, letters and the work of the agents, the customer needs a second system.
- **Little public on storage and models:** According to its security page, hosting, region and language models are clarified with sales. Whether customer data is used for training is not stated there. The Trust Center could not be read without signing in.
- **Young company:** Founded in 2024, seed funding, references mainly from sales, real estate and recruiting. We found no utilities or large insurers among its references.
- **Limits according to users:** In reviews on OMR, users mention the lack of deep CRM integration, unreliable recognition of phone numbers and email addresses, and the effort for setup and fine-tuning.

## Einwände

- **"telli is faster to go live and cheaper."** For a first phone agent that may be true, telli is built for a quick start. But factor in what happens to emails, letters and the cases that end up with your team. With us, inbox, agent workspace and telephony are included in the product.
- **"telli has SOC 2, you don't."** That is a real lead. Our ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, we state publicly where we store data, and our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are available to read.
- **"telli also has energy customers."** True, mostly solar and heating companies, usually for sales and appointments. For utilities, we bring meter reading, installment and bank details ready-made with plausibility checks, by phone and in writing.
