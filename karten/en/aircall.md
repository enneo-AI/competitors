## Erster Satz

"Aircall is a very simple and popular phone system for sales and support. We focus on written correspondence: our AI handles emails with multiple requests and attachments all the way to the booking in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Aircall is first and foremost a cloud phone system for small and mid-sized teams, strong in sales with its Power Dialer and over 250 ready-made connections to CRM and helpdesk tools. The AI Voice Agent has been available since April 2025, AI agents for SMS and WhatsApp since May 2026. Aircall does not handle email and has no ticketing system of its own: tickets are created via integrations, for example in Zendesk. For enneo, Aircall is therefore rarely a replacement, but usually the phone system we run alongside or replace.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email and letters, where Aircall has nothing:** At enneo, the same AI reads the email, identifies the customer, books in the target system and replies, also fully automated. Letters are a dedicated channel with the same AI. At Aircall, we found no AI handling of emails or letters, only AI drafts of follow-up emails after a call.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** Meter reading, new IBAN and meter photo in one email: enneo handles them in one pass with one reply. Aircall's agents work with calls and short messages. Nothing is documented on attachments or multiple requests.
- **One product with its own agent workspace:** At enneo, ticketing, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one system with skill-based routing. Anyone working with Aircall additionally needs a helpdesk such as Zendesk or Freshdesk for tickets and email.
- **Data in Germany:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in German data centers, and the DPA, TOM, subprocessors and SLA are public. At Aircall, only recordings, transcripts and AI insights for EU accounts are stored in Frankfurt. Contacts, call data and messages are mostly in the US according to Aircall.
- **Ready-made processes for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. Aircall names no solution for this, and its only utility reference is a water utility in the US.

## Wo Aircall vorn liegt

- **Simple phone system for sales:** Quick to set up, Power Dialer, voicemail drop, click-to-dial, and since 2026 outbound campaigns with an AI agent and "Local Presence". Users on OMR praise usability (92%) and setup (90%). We have no dialer for campaigns.
- **Integrations:** Over 250 ready-made integrations, including Salesforce, HubSpot, Zendesk, Intercom, Pipedrive, Attio and Shopify, plus an open API and webhooks. Our ecosystem of ready-made connectors is smaller.
- **Self-built voicebot with a fixed per-minute price:** AI Voice Agent without code from templates, 38 languages according to Aircall, actions such as creating a ticket or updating the CRM, prices public. Since May 2026 with technology from the acquired voice AI provider Vogent. We offer less choice of voice providers.
- **Size and reach:** 24,000+ customers, in DACH including SumUp, trivago, Chrono24 and Zenjob, plus a sales team in Berlin.
- **Certification:** SOC 2 Type II and CSA STAR. We have no certifications of our own.

## Wo Aircall schwächer ist

- **Voice and short messages only:** No email channel, no ticketing of its own. For written correspondence, the customer needs a second system and a second AI.
- **Data only partly in the EU:** According to Aircall, account data, contacts, call data, SMS and WhatsApp are stored "in most cases" in the US. AI data from older accounts is only due to migrate by the end of 2026. All listed AI and speech services are based in the US.
- **Built for small teams:** Entry price from 3 licenses, self-service, references mainly from retail, travel and services. We found no references from energy or insurance in DACH.
- **Quality and support:** OMR users criticize fluctuating call quality, connection problems and support that is hard to reach after onboarding (support 83%, the weakest score). Getting German phone numbers without an office in Germany is said to be slow.

## Einwände

- **"We just signed with Aircall."** That is not an obstacle. Aircall stays your phone system, calls can be forwarded to enneo or connected via SIP, and our AI handles email, letters and chat in one agent workspace. When the contract runs out, it is worth checking whether our own telephony can replace Aircall.
- **"Aircall has AI agents now too."** True, for calls since 2025 and for SMS and WhatsApp since May 2026, with actions in CRM and helpdesk. For email and letters, Aircall has no AI handling, and that is where utilities have the most workload.
- **"Aircall is cheap and its prices are transparent."** The license prices are public and low, but AI minutes, messages and the required helpdesk come on top. With us, ticketing, AI and telephony are one system with package prices per ticket and voice minute.

## Fragen an den Kunden

- **What do you use today to handle emails and letters that come in alongside Aircall?** Expected answer: a separate helpdesk or inbox, mostly by hand. Transition: "That is exactly where we come in. The AI handles emails all the way to the booking in the target system."
- **Where are your contact data, call data and WhatsApp histories from Aircall stored, and has your data protection team reviewed this?** Expected answer: unclear, according to Aircall mostly in the US. Transition: "We store data in German data centers, and our documents are public."
- **Have you seen how Aircall handles a customer email with three requests and a meter photo?** Expected answer: no, Aircall can't do that. Transition: "Send us a real email and we'll show you the handling live."

## Intern

- **Aircall or enneo, not both:** A prospect (energy supplier) evaluated Aircall for routing and transcription and saw the same functions at enneo: "So it would be either or." Part of the company already uses Aircall, but the service team would not introduce it alongside enneo. *Attio call, Oct 14, 2026, min. 36:37*
- **Aircall as the phone system next to enneo:** The same prospect initially wanted only email via enneo plus "an Aircall connection", and a switch to Aircall was being discussed. They said they had "very bad experiences" with AI phone systems, in general, not with Aircall. *Attio call, Sep 22, 2026, min. 7:11 and 45:42*

## Fazit

Against Aircall, we don't win on the dialer, usability, ready-made integrations or the entry price of the phone system. We can win wherever written correspondence comes in: email and letters with multiple requests and attachments all the way to the booking, ticketing and telephony in one system, storage in Germany. Where Aircall is already in place, enneo is the conversation alongside it, and at the end of the contract our telephony can replace Aircall.
