## Erster Satz

"smao is a lean, sovereignly hosted phone assistant and quick to set up. It gets interesting when calls, emails and letters should be handled in one system through to posting in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

smao is a young Berlin startup with five employees and an AI phone assistant. It answers calls, handles standard questions from the knowledge base and hands everything else over in structured form to agents or to a third-party ticketing system. smao is strong on sovereignty and entry: its own telephony, self-operated open-source models in Germany, public pricing from €0.35 per minute. We found no automated processing of incoming emails and no agent workspace of its own, and that is where our advantage lies.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI through to posting:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, posts in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. At smao, a team member drafts replies to customer emails by hand in the dashboard chat with the "E-Mail beantworten" template. We found no automated processing of incoming emails.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles it in one pass with one reply, and the AI also reads attached PDFs and photos. At smao, neither is documented.
- **Agent workspace and AI in one product:** enneo comes with a ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot with skill-based routing in one system. After a call, smao hands requests over to third-party systems such as HaloPSA, TANSS, Autotask, Zendesk or HubSpot. It has its own inbox only for the website chat.
- **Ready-made flows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made specialist agents with plausibility checks at enneo, only the ERP connection is built. smao offers solutions for IT support, appointment booking, complaints or order status. We found no templates or references for utilities.
- **All channels including letters:** enneo covers email, letters, chat, WhatsApp, Telegram and phone, and letters run through the same AI as emails. smao covers phone and website chat, with SMS and WhatsApp only as outbound notifications.

## Wo smao vorn liegt

- **Language models in Germany:** According to smao, it operates open-source and open-weight models itself, with inference at Krämer IT Solutions in Germany, without OpenAI. With us, US models are the default, EU models (Mistral, Nebius, Media Trooper) are possible under the DPA.
- **Price and entry:** Public pricing, Pay as you Go with no base fee, 50 free minutes without a credit card, live in 15 minutes according to smao. With us, there are package prices per ticket and voice minute.
- **Telephony in detail:** Its own SIP carrier in Germany (Reventix), 70+ voices, 10+ languages including Swiss German, outbound, IVR navigation, VIP forwarding per contact, pronunciation via phonetic notation. We have fewer choices of voice providers.
- **Ready-made connectors for IT service providers:** TANSS, HaloPSA, Autotask, Jira Service Management, Zendesk and HubSpot with one click, plus partners such as Synaxon and ComTeam with around 800 partner companies, as well as white label and reselling.
- **Hosting with C5:** Primarily at STACKIT, whose data centers are BSI C5 attested. Our host Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. Neither has certifications of its own.

## Wo smao schwächer ist

- **Very young and very small:** Five employees, the company has only been called smao since January 2025, and we found no investors. References are mid-sized companies and IT service providers, no utilities, insurers or large contact centers. OMR lists 8 reviews.
- **Phone only at its core:** The AI doesn't answer emails on its own, a team member has drafts written in the dashboard chat. Whatever happens after the call continues in a third-party ticketing system.
- **Sovereignty claims with caveats:** The website says "ohne US-KI-Subprozessoren" (no US AI subprocessors), but its own subprocessor list names ElevenLabs Inc. (USA) as contracting party for part of the voices, with processing in Belgium. The SLA explicitly states 99.5% availability as a target, not as a guaranteed minimum.
- **Recognition in practice:** OMR users report that names and company names are not always recognized reliably and that rigid prompts led to hallucinations and routing errors.

## Einwände

- **"smao is sovereign, without OpenAI and on German servers."** That is a real lead on language models. With us, data is also stored with a German provider in Germany, and EU models are possible under the DPA. Also ask when smao uses ElevenLabs Inc. as a US contracting party for voices.
- **"smao is much cheaper and live in 15 minutes."** For a phone assistant that answers and passes on calls, that's true. With us, you pay for a system in which emails, letters and calls are handled through to posting in the target system, with an agent workspace for your team.
- **"We only need the phone for now."** Understandable. But look at where requests end up after the call and how much follows by email. With us, the same AI works on the phone and in the inbox, with the same knowledge and the same agent workspace.
