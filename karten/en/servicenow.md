## Erster Satz

"ServiceNow is very strong as a platform for IT and processes, and customer emails with several requests plus telephony are where we come in: both are ready to use in one product with us."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

ServiceNow is the workflow platform of many large enterprises, first for IT, alongside that for customer service (CSM), field service and HR. For email there is a prebuilt AI workflow, "Triage cases", that classifies emails, extracts data including from attachments, and replies on its own. Out of the box it is read-only and has to be cloned, activated and partly adjusted with scripts. ServiceNow only brings its own voice in the browser; regular phone calls come in through a third-party contact center system.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Several requests, one reply:** enneo handles a meter reading, a new IBAN and a new installment in one email in a single pass with one reply. According to its docs, the ServiceNow workflow determines the customer's goal and then follows one path. We found nothing on several requests in one email.
- **Telephony in the product:** With enneo, inbox, AI agents, telephony and voicebot are one system, with our own telephony or the customer's SIP line. With ServiceNow, calls from the phone network only arrive through a third party's contact center system such as Genesys, NICE or Amazon Connect.
- **Ready-made workflows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo; we build the ERP integration per customer. We found no utility-specific workflows at ServiceNow. Its examples are returns, complaints and product recalls. The energy reference Landis+Gyr uses CSM for business customers; we found no CSM reference from a German utility.
- **AI agents in plain language instead of scripts:** With enneo you describe an AI agent in plain language and change it with Neo via chat. ServiceNow's AI Agent Studio also works in plain language, but according to ServiceNow, extracting data from emails requires adjusting a script file.
- **German provider:** enneo is a German GmbH without investors, stores data at Hetzner, on-premises on request. ServiceNow also stores data in Germany, but it is a US corporation.

## Wo ServiceNow vorn liegt

- **Platform for the whole company:** IT, customer service, field service, HR and custom apps on one platform. A customer issue can directly trigger a workflow in finance or field service. 85% of the Fortune 500 use ServiceNow.
- **Contact center management:** Workforce management with demand forecasting and shift scheduling, a manager workspace with real-time queues, call monitoring and coaching. We don't have that.
- **Certifications:** ISO 27001 since 2012, plus 27017, 27018, 27701, 42001 for AI, SOC 2 and C5. We have none of our own.
- **Channels and portal:** Email, chat, SMS, WhatsApp, Apple Business Messages and a customer portal with self-service. We don't have a help center for end customers.
- **Broad contact center integration:** The AI voice agents also speak German and work with Genesys, NICE, Five9, Amazon Connect, Vonage, Webex and Zoom. ServiceNow connects third-party AI agents via A2A and MCP.

## Wo ServiceNow schwächer ist

- **Prices hard to pin down:** No public prices, only packages on request. The autonomous AI Specialists are only available in the higher Prime package.
- **Effort to implement and run:** On G2, implementation takes 4 months on average. On OMR, users mention high running costs for smaller companies and a lot of training ("Wir benötigen intern sehr viel Schulung").
- **Email AI is a construction kit:** The AI workflows are read-only out of the box. You clone them, activate agents and triggers, and adjust data extraction via a script file. If the customer's sentiment is negative, the case goes to a human, according to ServiceNow.
- **Product in transition:** Since May 2026, "ServiceNow Otto" replaces Now Assist and Moveworks across all products. Older documents and offers still use the old names.

## Einwände

- **"We already have ServiceNow in-house."** That's often the case, mostly for IT, and it will stay. For areas with lots of customer emails and calls, enneo alongside is often the better conversation: ready-made utility workflows, telephony in the product, no rebuild of the IT platform.
- **"ServiceNow is the market leader and can now do everything with AI agents."** The platform is broad and the agents are to be taken seriously. Ask for a reference where one email with several requests is handled in one pass and booked in the billing system. And ask which contact center system the calls run through.
- **"ServiceNow has C5 and ISO 27001, you don't."** That's fair, we don't have our own certifications, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, enneo is a German GmbH, stores data in Germany, and our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public.

- **Which package do you need for AI agents that close cases on their own, and what does it cost at your volume?** Expected answer: the higher package, price still open. Transition: "With us, you calculate with package prices per ticket and voice minute."

## Intern

- **ServiceNow stays, opportunity alongside:** A prospect (municipal waste management company) uses ServiceNow for IT and is currently rolling out CSM, in its own words "kurz vor Fertigstellung". Because of a long license contract, it does not want to replace ServiceNow or put enneo in front of it. It sees opportunities in the units serving commercial and municipal customers, which have no ticket system yet. *Attio call, Oct 28, 2026, min. 2:56, 21:18 and 24:52*
- **Don't present as a general AI layer:** A partner (consultancy) advises against positioning enneo as "AI-Layer schlechthin". Customers with ServiceNow hear exactly the same story from ServiceNow. Better a sharp use case with a clear benefit. *Attio call, Apr 14, 2026, min. 14:22*

## Fazit

Against ServiceNow, we don't win on platform breadth, certifications or contact center management, and where ServiceNow is established for IT, we don't replace it. We can win in customer service with lots of written correspondence and calls: several requests in one email, telephony in the product, ready-made utility workflows and a German provider. Best as a clear use case alongside ServiceNow, not as a second platform.
