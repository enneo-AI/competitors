## Erster Satz

"Superchat is very strong for WhatsApp and the social channels and quick to set up, and email is where we come in: with us, one AI handles it from receipt all the way to the booking in the target system, even with several requests in one email."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Superchat is a messaging inbox: WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Telegram, SMS, live chat and email in one place, plus WhatsApp newsletters and review management. The AI agent answers chats and emails, takes calls as an AI phone assistant, and according to a help article from September 2026 can call your own APIs via HTTP requests. Superchat leads on price, onboarding and channel breadth. Our advantage is depth in written service processes: multiple requests, attachments, letters, customer identification and ready-made workflows for utilities.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Multiple requests, one reply:** A meter reading, a new IBAN and a new installment in one email are handled by enneo in a single pass with one reply, fully automated if desired. Superchat documents nothing on this, and nothing on reading attachments and photos either.
- **Full-depth email processing:** At enneo, the AI identifies the request, identifies and verifies the customer, books in the target system and replies. According to its own help center, Superchat's email feature cannot send emails with CC or BCC, and we found no verification rules.
- **Telephony in the same system:** enneo has its own telephony, voicebot and agent workspace in one product. Superchat's AI phone assistant works through call forwarding to a Superchat number and hands calls back to the customer's phone system by forwarding.
- **Letters as a dedicated channel:** At enneo, the same AI that handles emails processes scanned letters. We found no letter channel at Superchat.
- **For utilities in addition:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made AI agents with plausibility checks at enneo. Superchat offers industry templates, but we found no energy workflows of this kind.

## Wo Superchat schwächer ist

- **Email is a secondary channel:** According to the help center, sending emails with CC or BCC is "currently not supported". Email is connected via the service Nylas. We found no documentation on multiple requests or attachments in the AI agent.
- **Phone only bolted on:** The AI phone assistant needs call forwarding to a Superchat number in +44 format. If the phone system does not pass through the caller's number, Superchat stops answering calls. Depending on the plan, only 2, 4 or 8 calls run in parallel.
- **Costs grow with add-ons:** Each extra seat €19, each extra AI agent €49, AI copilot €29, credits for AI and automations, WhatsApp fees per conversation. On OMR, users call the cost per number "relatively high" and the automations "relatively complex".
- **References tend to be small:** The success stories mainly show agencies, medical practices, car dealerships and travel agencies. At Enpal it is about WhatsApp during the construction phase, AI is not mentioned. We found no reference with automated email processing at a utility.

## Einwände

- **"Superchat is much cheaper."** For getting started, that is true, prices are public and low. Add the extras: more seats, AI agents, credits, copilot and WhatsApp fees. And look at which cases still need manual work without a booking in the target system.
- **"Superchat also has an AI agent for email and phone."** True, and according to its help center it can also call your own APIs. Nothing is documented on emails with multiple requests and attachments, and on the phone it works through call forwarding. Test both with your own sample emails.
- **"We already use Superchat for WhatsApp."** Superchat is strong for WhatsApp, newsletters and reviews. For service processes by email and letter with a booking in the ERP, enneo is the better conversation, and WhatsApp runs on the same logic with us.

## Fazit

We do not win against Superchat on price, onboarding, WhatsApp marketing or the number of channels. We can win where a lot of written correspondence with a booking in the target system should be handled automatically: multiple requests, attachments, letters, verification and ready-made utility workflows, plus telephony in the same system. For small teams focused on WhatsApp, Superchat is often the better fit.
