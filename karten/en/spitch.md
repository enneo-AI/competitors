## Erster Satz

"Spitch is really strong on the phone, especially with dialects and voice recognition, and written requests are where we come in: with us, an AI handles the email on its own, all the way to the booking in the target system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

Spitch comes from speech recognition and is at home on the phone: voicebot, analysis of every call, voice biometrics against fraud, quality management and training. Spitch is well established in Switzerland and with German cooperative banks and insurers. Its known deployments in DACH mainly route calls to the right place instead of resolving cases end to end. We found nothing on AI processing of incoming emails at Spitch, and that is where our advantage lies.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email AI through to the booking:** With enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, takes the action in the ERP and replies, fully automated if desired. Spitch lists email only as a connected channel. We found no AI processing of incoming emails.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** enneo handles meter reading, IBAN and installment from one email in a single pass with one reply, and reads PDFs and photos too. Spitch documents nothing on this.
- **One system with an agent workspace:** At enneo, the ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot are one product. Spitch stores summaries in the customer's CRM or ticketing system and sells eight separate products that customers add step by step.
- **Ready-made workflows for utilities:** enneo ships meter reading, installment and bank details as ready-made specialist agents with plausibility checks. Spitch's utility offering centers on call routing, outage reports and announcements. Meter readings and installments are not mentioned there.
- **Data protection documents public:** enneo's DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are publicly available. At Spitch, we only found the website privacy policy.

## Wo Spitch vorn liegt

- **Speech recognition and dialects:** Its own speech analytics for Swiss German since 2015, and according to Spitch for all national languages and dialects. The "Berna" voicebot of Gebäudeversicherung Bern recognizes dialects and transcribes them into standard German.
- **Voice biometrics against fraud:** Voice Biometrics identifies callers by their voice, keeps verifying during the call and detects recorded or synthetic voices. enneo has no comparable identity check.
- **Speech analytics, quality and training:** Analysis of every conversation, AI scoring of calls, training simulations for new agents. We have scorecards, but no speech analytics and training suite of this depth.
- **Installed base in DACH with banks and insurers:** Over 20 Volksbanken since 2021, according to Spitch over 5 million calls, plus Versicherungskammer, Baloise, CSS, GVB, Migros Bank. Ready-made integrations with Genesys, Microsoft Teams and Luware.
- **Certificate and on-premises operation:** ISO 27001:2022, operation in Switzerland, in the customer's cloud or on-premises, also with its own open-source language models. We have no certificate of our own.

## Wo Spitch schwächer ist

- **Written requests barely visible:** Product pages, Knowledge Agent and Agent Assist mention no automatic processing of incoming emails and no draft replies for emails.
- **A lot of routing, little resolving:** The public DACH references mostly measure correct routing: Volksbanken with over 40 categories, Versicherungskammer with misrouted calls down from 40% to under 15%, Baloise with 85% reaching the right expert. Which cases the bot closes itself in the core system is not stated there.
- **Energy proven only outside Germany:** Utility references are SES and AMB in Ticino and Hera in Italy. We found no German utility as a customer.
- **Prices and contract documents not public:** No prices, no public DPA or subprocessor list, certificate scope not stated.

## Einwände

- **"Spitch understands dialects too, which matters to us on the phone."** That is a real strength of Spitch, especially in Swiss German. Ask whether the bot then also closes the case or only routes it. We are happy to show you a test call in your dialect with a real booking.
- **"Spitch is ISO certified, you are not."** True, the certificate is an advantage. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress for us, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. In return, our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, and we did not find them for Spitch.
- **"Spitch is already running on our hotline."** Good, Spitch does call routing and speech analytics well. For emails and letters that an AI should handle all the way into the target system, enneo alongside it is often the better conversation than a full switch.
