## Erster Satz

"moinAI has a good website chat, especially for utilities. It gets interesting with everything that reaches you afterward by email, letter or phone."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

moinAI is a website chatbot provider that now calls itself an "Agentic AI Plattform". moinAI is strong in chat self-service with a knowledge base, forms and AI actions that write into the customer's system via APIs. According to the case study, at the utility TEAG the chat posts installments, meter readings and SEPA mandates directly via SAP and the TEAG API. AI handling of incoming emails is not publicly described: the documented "E-Mail-Takeover" sends a chat transcript by email to a ticketing system.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email through to posting:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, posts in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. At moinAI, the help center describes the "E-Mail-Takeover" as a handover: the chat is sent as an email to a ticketing system's support address. The pricing page does mention "Automated handling of email enquiries", but we found no description of it.
- **Multiple requests and attachments in one email:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles all of it in one pass with one reply, and reads attached PDFs and photos along with it. At moinAI, neither is documented for email. OMR reviews say the bot works through multi-part questions one after another.
- **Agent workspace and AI in one product:** enneo brings ticketing workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot in one system with skill-based routing. moinAI has its own live chat. Cases beyond that go as a ticket to Zendesk, Salesforce, Freshdesk or by email to another system.
- **All channels instead of website chat:** enneo covers email, letter, chat, WhatsApp, Telegram and phone. At moinAI, WhatsApp and Voice (via Aristech) are only "on request" on the pricing page, and we found no letter channel.
- **Verifiable data protection:** At enneo, DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public. moinAI names OVH and Azure West Europe but only sends the DPA by email on request, and we found no list of subprocessors.

## Wo moinAI schwächer ist

- **Email and phone are not core:** Website, product overview and industry page revolve around chat. Email appears as a handover path, WhatsApp and Voice only "on request".
- **No ticketing system of its own:** Whatever isn't resolved in the live chat ends up in a third-party system. So the customer also needs Zendesk, Salesforce or another ticketing system.
- **Data protection only partly documented:** No certification mentioned, no list of subprocessors found. The help center does not explicitly say whether text chats are used for training. For voice recordings, it rules out training.
- **Criticism in reviews:** On OMR, reviewers mention high effort building the knowledge base, requests that have to be trained manually, basic analytics, little customization of integrations and forms only via support.

## Einwände

- **"moinAI has a visible reference at a large utility for installments and meter readings."** That's true, and the chat there is well done. Ask what happens to the emails: at moinAI only the handover to a ticketing system is documented, with us it is the posting.
- **"At €750 a month, moinAI is much cheaper."** For the website chat, that's true. Add the ticketing system, the telephony and the manual email handling that are missing at moinAI or only available on request. With us, agent workspace, AI, knowledge and telephony are one product.
- **"We already use moinAI for chat."** Then the chat can stay. enneo takes over email and phone alongside it. We already run it side by side like that.

## Fazit

We win against moinAI where there is a lot of written correspondence and the customer wants email, letter, phone and agent workspace from a single source. Where a utility mainly wants an inexpensive, quick-start website chat with self-service for installments and meter readings, moinAI is well positioned with its public pricing and the TEAG reference. There, it's worth asking who handles emails and handovers afterward.
