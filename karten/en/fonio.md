"fonio is quick to set up on the phone and a great fit for many businesses, where it gets interesting is when emails, letters and calls need to run in one system all the way to the posting in the billing system."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

fonio is a young, fast-growing AI phone assistant from Vienna with public pricing and setup in minutes. According to OMR, almost 90% of its customers are small businesses, and its references come from medical practices, law firms, trades, real estate and retail, not from energy or insurance. On top of that, fonio is currently building out WhatsApp, web chat, an email assistant and its own phone system, and the website still lists email and the phone system as "coming soon". We win where written correspondence and phone need to be handled together all the way to the action in the target system, with an agent workspace for the team.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email all the way to the posting:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, posts in the ERP and replies, fully automated if desired. fonio's email assistant drafts, replies, labels and forwards, since October 2026 with read access to a CRM. We found no documentation of posting in the target system or of customer verification there.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** enneo handles a meter reading, a new IBAN and a new installment in one email in a single pass with one reply, and the AI reads PDFs and photos in the attachment. At fonio, neither is documented for email.
- **Workspace and AI in one product:** enneo brings a ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot with skill-based routing. At fonio, calls and chats land in a shared inbox with filters and tags, agents take over chats in the mobile app, and calls are forwarded to numbers or ring groups. We found no ticketing with skill-based routing.
- **Ready-made workflows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made specialist agents with plausibility checks at enneo, only the ERP connection is built. fonio offers industry pages for medical practices, law firms and trades, and we found neither a template nor a reference for utilities.
- **All channels including letters:** enneo covers email, letters, chat, WhatsApp, Telegram and phone, and letters run through the same AI as emails. We found no letter channel at fonio.

## Wo fonio.ai vorn liegt

- **Price and entry:** Public pricing from €99 per month, setup in minutes according to the website, 30-day money-back guarantee. Users on OMR mainly praise usability, setup and support.
- **Breadth of the voicebot:** 120+ voices, 60+ languages, outbound calls from the Team plan, custom voice in the Scale plan, requests to your own systems before, during and after the call (GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE). We have fewer voice providers to choose from.
- **Voice services in the EU:** According to the DPA, OpenAI, LiveKit, Soniox and Inworld run with EU processing, and Deepgram, Google and ElevenLabs are EU fallback providers. At enneo, the voicebot via OpenAI in the US is the default, with Deepslate (EU) as the alternative.
- **Many ready-made integrations for small businesses:** Around 30 native integrations, including Google Calendar, Calendly, Pipedrive, Odoo, onOffice, WooCommerce, Make and n8n, plus HubSpot for the phone system. We have a smaller ecosystem of ready-made connectors.
- **Speed and capital:** New features in the changelog almost daily, €14.73m in seed capital, new offices planned in Munich among other places. Many visible customer logos such as Volkswagen, BRITA, Hagebau, Bechtle, Haufe.

## Wo fonio.ai schwächer ist

- **Built for small businesses:** According to OMR, around 88% of reviews come from small businesses, only 3 of 329 from enterprise customers. The public case studies show a hotel, a practice, building supplies, real estate and car dealerships, no utilities, insurers or large contact centers.
- **Email and phone system still new:** Both are listed as "coming soon" on the website, and the features have only appeared in the changelog since October 2026. We found no references for them.
- **Criticism of billing logic and recognition:** OMR users complain about the billing, a minimum top-up of €300, paid test calls and errors with names, places and strong dialects.
- **No SAP, Salesforce or Dynamics as a ready-made integration:** The integrations page lists tools for small businesses. Larger systems are connected via HTTP requests or partners, according to fonio for example via the agency softwarebude.

## Einwände

- **"fonio is much cheaper."** For a phone assistant in a medical practice or a trades business, that's true. With us, you pay for one system in which emails, letters and calls are resolved all the way to the posting in the billing system, with a workspace for your team.
- **"fonio has Volkswagen and BRITA as customers."** The logos are real, but the case studies are mainly about phone availability. Ask for a reference in which fonio processes emails with multiple requests all the way into the target system.
- **"fonio can do email now too."** Since October there are drafts, automatic replies and forwarding, but the website still calls the assistant "coming soon". Ask them to show you how an email with a meter photo and three requests gets posted.

## Fragen an den Kunden

- **How much of your volume comes in by email and letter rather than by phone?** Expected answer: a large share, often more than by phone. Transition: "That's exactly where our AI posts all the way into the target system, in the same system as your voicebot."
- **Have you seen how fonio handles an email with three requests and a photo attached?** Expected answer: no, that wasn't part of the demo. Transition: "We're happy to show you that live with your own sample emails."
- **Where do your agents work on the cases the AI can't resolve on its own?** Expected answer: in a separate ticketing system or by email. Transition: "With us, the workspace is included, no second system needed."

## Fazit

Against fonio, we don't win on price, speed of setup or the choice of voices and languages.
