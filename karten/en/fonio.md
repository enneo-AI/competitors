## Das Wichtigste vorweg

fonio is a young, fast-growing AI phone assistant from Vienna with public pricing and setup in minutes. According to OMR, almost 90% of its customers are small businesses. The references come from medical practices, law firms, trades, real estate and retail, not from energy or insurance. On top of that, fonio is currently building out WhatsApp, web chat, an email assistant and its own phone system. The website still lists email and the phone system as "coming soon". We win where written correspondence and phone should be handled together through to the action in the target system, with an agent workspace for the agents.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email through to posting:** At enneo, the AI reads the email, identifies and verifies the customer, posts in the ERP and replies, fully automated if desired. fonio's email assistant drafts, replies, tags and forwards, and since October 2026 has read access to a CRM. We found no documentation of posting in the target system or customer verification there.
- **Multiple requests and attachments:** Meter reading, new IBAN and new installment in one email: enneo handles it in one pass with one reply, and the AI also reads attached PDFs and photos. At fonio, neither is documented for email.
- **Agent workspace and AI in one product:** enneo comes with a ticketing agent workspace, inbox, AI agents, knowledge, telephony and voicebot with skill-based routing. At fonio, agents take over chats in the mobile app, and calls are forwarded to numbers or call groups. We found no ticket workspace.
- **Ready-made flows for utilities:** Meter reading, installment and bank details are ready-made specialist agents with plausibility checks at enneo, only the ERP connection is built. fonio offers industry pages for medical practices, law firms and trades. For utilities, we found neither a template nor a reference.
- **All channels including letters:** enneo covers email, letters, chat, WhatsApp, Telegram and phone, and letters run through the same AI as emails. We found no letter channel at fonio.

## Wo fonio.ai vorn liegt

- **Price and entry:** Public pricing from €99 per month, setup in minutes according to the website, 30-day money-back guarantee. Users on OMR mainly praise ease of use, setup and support.
- **Voicebot breadth:** 120+ voices, 60+ languages, outbound calls from the Team plan, custom voice in the Scale plan, requests to your own systems before, during and after the call (GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE). We have less choice of voice providers.
- **Voice services in the EU:** According to the DPA, OpenAI, LiveKit, Soniox and Inworld run with EU processing, and Deepgram, Google and ElevenLabs are EU fallback providers. With us, the voicebot runs via OpenAI in the USA by default, with Deepslate (EU) as the alternative.
- **Many ready-made integrations for small businesses:** Around 30 native integrations, including Google Calendar, Calendly, Pipedrive, HubSpot, Odoo, onOffice, Shopify, WooCommerce, Make, n8n, Zapier. We have a smaller ecosystem of ready-made connectors.
- **Pace and capital:** New features in the changelog almost daily, €14.73m in seed capital, new offices planned, including in Munich. Many visible customer logos such as Volkswagen, BRITA, Hagebau, Bechtle, Haufe.

## Wo fonio.ai schwächer ist

- **Built for small businesses:** According to OMR, around 88% of reviews come from small businesses, only 3 of 328 from enterprise customers. The public case studies show a hotel, a medical practice, building materials retail, real estate and car dealerships, no utilities, insurers or large contact centers.
- **Email and phone system still young:** Both are listed as "coming soon" on the website, and the features have only appeared in the changelog since October 2026. We found no references for them.
- **Criticism of pricing logic and recognition:** OMR users criticize the billing, a minimum top-up of €300, paid test calls and errors with names, places and strong dialects.
- **No SAP, Salesforce or Dynamics as a ready-made integration:** The integrations page lists tools for small businesses. Larger systems run via HTTP requests or partners, according to fonio for example via the agency softwarebude.

## Einwände

- **"fonio is much cheaper."** For a phone assistant in a medical practice or a trades business, that's true. With us, you pay for a system in which emails, letters and calls are handled through to posting in the billing system, with an agent workspace for your team.
- **"fonio has Volkswagen and BRITA as customers."** The logos are real, but the case studies are mainly about phone availability. Ask for a reference in which fonio handles emails with multiple requests through to the target system.
- **"fonio can do email now too."** Since October there have been drafts, automatic replies and forwarding, and the website still calls the assistant "coming soon". Ask them to show you how an email with a meter photo and three requests gets posted.
