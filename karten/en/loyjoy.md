## Erster Satz

"LoyJoy does chat and voicebots for insurers and municipal utilities really well. It gets interesting when we ask who handles your incoming emails and letters all the way to the booking."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

LoyJoy builds AI agents for website chat, WhatsApp and phone, all without code and on a shared knowledge base. LoyJoy is strong with insurers (R+V, HanseMerkur, Zurich, WGV, LVM) and increasingly with municipal utilities, now also on the phone there. We did not find an email inbox with AI handling: at LoyJoy, email is only used for sending and for handing off to the team. Our advantage lies in written correspondence and in the agent workspace, not in chat or voicebot.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Email and letters all the way to the booking:** enneo reads the incoming email or letter, identifies and verifies the customer, books in the target system and replies, fully automated if desired. At LoyJoy, the agent can only send emails or hand off a request to the team by email. AI handling of incoming emails is not documented.
- **Multiple requests, one reply:** enneo handles a meter reading, a new IBAN and a new installment in one email in a single pass with one reply. The AI also reads attachments such as PDFs or meter photos.
- **One agent workspace for everything:** With enneo, agents handle all channels in one ticketing workspace with skill routing. LoyJoy has a live chat for web and WhatsApp. Calls go to the customer's phone system, and captured cases go to the business department by email.
- **Stored with a German provider:** enneo stores data at Hetzner in Germany, and on-premises for large volumes. LoyJoy stores platform data at Google Cloud EMEA.

## Wo LoyJoy vorn liegt

- **References with insurers:** According to LoyJoy, R+V grew from 2,000 to more than 35,000 dialogs per month in eight months, and the project won the German Brand Award 2026. Plus HanseMerkur on the phone, WGV, CosmosDirekt, DA Direkt, LVM and Zurich.
- **Proven voicebots at municipal utilities:** According to the case study, Stadtwerke Velbert resolves more than 70% of calls on the phone, live three weeks after the workshop. Stadtwerke Witten around 50%, since fall 2026, with multiple requests per call one after another. At Stadtwerke Herne, the chat agent reads and writes meter reading, installment and installment plans in SAP IS-U.
- **Own language models in Germany:** By default, the chat model runs on its own hardware in Münster, without an external model provider. External models only on explicit instruction.
- **Price and entry point:** Public prices from €390 per month, 30-day trial, phone number in a few clicks, according to LoyJoy live in 2 to 4 weeks.
- **Business departments build it themselves:** No-code with BPMN flows, more than 100 process modules, more than 30 ready-made integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Brevo), and building also directly from Claude or ChatGPT. OMR rating 4.8 from 55 reviews.

## Wo LoyJoy schwächer ist

- **No written correspondence:** LoyJoy positions itself for "chat and phone". Anyone who wants to automate a lot of emails or letters needs another system alongside LoyJoy.
- **The agent often only captures:** At Witten, the phone agent records the installment or meter reading and hands the case over to the business department. At Velbert, the chat leads to the customer portal via a link. Booking in the billing system is publicly documented only for Herne.
- **Setup and usability:** Users on OMR call the initial setup time-consuming, the BPMN editor overwhelming at first and the interface partly cluttered. They want more analytics and versioning, which according to the pricing page are only available in the Enterprise plan.
- **No certification of its own:** Like us, there is no ISO 27001 certification of its own, only certified data centers.

## Einwände

- **"LoyJoy has the better references with insurers and municipal utilities."** That's true, the case studies with insurers and municipal utilities are well documented. Ask whether written correspondence also runs automatically there: at LoyJoy that is not documented, and for us it is the core.
- **"LoyJoy is cheaper and faster to get started."** For chat and phone, the entry point from €390 per month really is low. But factor in the system for email, letters and the agent workspace that you'll need alongside LoyJoy.
- **"We already use LoyJoy for chat and phone."** Then nothing needs to be replaced there. enneo takes over email and letters all the way to the booking and gives your agents one workspace for all channels.

## Fazit

Against LoyJoy, we win where emails and letters should be resolved automatically all the way to the booking and agents need a shared workspace. For chat and voicebot at municipal utilities and insurers, LoyJoy is well documented, quick to go live and inexpensive to start with, and we don't win on the product there. Where LoyJoy already does chat or phone, enneo for written correspondence is the better conversation than a complete switch.
