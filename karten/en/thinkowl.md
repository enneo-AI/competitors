## Erster Satz

"ThinkOwl is broadly positioned and well known among utilities. The difference is in how your processes get built: ready-made specialist agents in plain language instead of a BPMN project with a partner."

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Multiple requests, one reply:** When an email contains several requests, enneo handles all of them in one pass and replies once, fully automated if desired.
- **Agents in words instead of a process model:** At enneo, you describe what the AI agent should do and change it with Neo via chat. At ThinkOwl, it takes BPMN models and scripts.
- **One platform instead of six products:** Ticketing, phone, voicebot and AI agents built as one piece. At ThinkOwl, separate products, with AI as an add-on module.
- **Data with a German provider:** enneo stores data at Hetzner, on-premises if desired. ThinkOwl only in the cloud with Oracle and AWS.
- **Additionally for utilities:** Meter reading, installment, bank details and more are ready-made agents. ThinkOwl builds these in a project with a partner.

## Einwände

- **"ThinkOwl has telephony, bots and signature, that's the complete solution."** ThinkOwl is broad, and ThinkOwl is clearly ahead on signature and identity verification. But we also have ticketing, telephony, voicebot and AI agents, in one product, while ThinkOwl spreads them across several products with AI as an add-on module.
- **"You offer the same thing as ThinkOwl."** At first glance, yes: same channels, same cases at utilities. The difference is in how it's built: at ThinkOwl, BPMN models and scripts in a project with a partner. With us, ready-made specialist agents in plain language, plus multiple requests in one email handled in one pass.
- **"ThinkOwl has its own ISO 27001."** Correct, we don't have our own certificate. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. Our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, and from high volumes on-premises operation is possible.

## Fragen an den Kunden

- **Who builds the installment or meter reading process at ThinkOwl, and how long does it take?** Expected answer: In a project with a partner, as a BPMN model with scripts. Transition: "With us, these agents are ready-made. The only thing we build is the ERP connection."
- **What happens at ThinkOwl with an email that contains a meter reading, a new IBAN and an installment change?** Expected answer: Unclear, multiple requests in one pass are not documented. Transition: "With us, one solution plan is created for the whole ticket, with one reply."
