## Erster Satz

"ThinkOwl is broad and well known among utilities. The difference is how your processes get built: ready-made specialist agents described in plain language instead of a BPMN project with a partner."

## Das Wichtigste vorweg

ThinkOwl is a broad platform from Mülheim-Kärlich with an office in Cologne and separate products for service desk, AI automation, self-service, telephony and quality management. With municipal utilities, ThinkOwl pitches straight-through processing and references such as EVN Netze and Stadtwerke Münster, delivered through a concept, a workshop and an implementation partner. Whether several requests in one email are handled in a single pass is not publicly documented. ThinkOwl scores with its own ISO 27001, e-signature and identity verification. enneo scores with ready-made specialist agents, agents described in plain language and one product instead of several modules.

## Unsere Verkaufsargumente

- **Several requests, one reply:** When one email contains several requests, enneo handles all of them in a single pass and replies once, fully automated if desired.
- **Agents in words instead of process models:** With enneo you describe what the AI agent should do and change it with Neo via chat. At ThinkOwl, processes are built as BPMN models.
- **One platform instead of many products:** Ticketing, phone, voicebot and AI agents built as one. ThinkOwl has separate products, with AI as an add-on module.
- **Data with a German provider:** enneo stores data at Hetzner, or on-premises on request. ThinkOwl describes OwlDesk as cloud software on Oracle and AWS, and we found no on-premises option.
- **For utilities in addition:** Meter reading, installment, bank details and more are ready-made agents. ThinkOwl builds these in a project with a partner.

## Wo ThinkOwl vorn liegt

- **E-signature and identity:** ThinkOwl CONVERSATIONS offers video ident, 2FA and electronic signatures up to qualified signatures. enneo has no signature or ID verification.
- **Own certificate:** ThinkOwl holds its own ISO 27001 (TÜV Saar, valid until 02/2027). enneo has no certificates of its own, ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress.
- **Self-service for end customers:** ThinkOwl CONVERSATIONS for self-service across all channels, plus fileee as a document assistant for end customers. enneo has no help center for end customers.
- **Quality management as a separate product:** OwlCoach scores conversations with AI and coaches agents.
- **Utility references:** Publicly named are EVN Netze, Stadtwerke Bruchsal, Stadtwerke Münster, Hamburger Energienetze and Stadtwerke Klagenfurt, plus connections to SAP IS-U, Schleupen, Wilken and Powercloud.

## Wo ThinkOwl schwächer ist

- **Several requests not documented:** According to the product page, OwlForce recognizes various customer concerns. Nowhere does it say that several requests in one email are handled in a single pass with one reply.
- **Built in a project:** Processes are built as BPMN workflows, and for utilities through a concept, a workshop and an implementation partner. Ready-made specialist agents are not described.
- **AI spread across modules:** Automation sits in OwlForce with a base fee plus transaction costs, self-service in CONVERSATIONS billed per transaction, each on top of the OwlDesk license per user.
- **Inconsistent impact claims:** Depending on the section, the utilities page cites 10 to 25 percent, 20 to 30 percent, up to 75 percent or up to 80 percent automation, all vendor claims.

## Einwände

- **"ThinkOwl has telephony, bots and e-signature. That's the complete solution."** ThinkOwl is broad, and on e-signature and identity verification ThinkOwl is clearly ahead. But we also have ticketing, telephony, voicebot and AI agents, in one product, while ThinkOwl spreads them across several products with AI as an add-on.
- **"Aren't you offering the same thing as ThinkOwl?"** At first glance, yes: same channels, same utility cases. The difference is how it's built: at ThinkOwl, BPMN models in a project with a partner. With us, ready-made specialist agents in plain language, plus several requests in one email handled in a single pass.
- **"ThinkOwl has its own ISO 27001."** True, we don't have our own certificate. ISO 27001 and SOC 2 are in progress, and Hetzner is ISO 27001 certified. Our DPA, TOMs, subprocessors and SLA are public, and at high volume you can run us on-premises.

## Fazit

Against ThinkOwl we don't win on e-signature, identity verification, end-customer self-service or our own certificate. We can win at utilities with a lot of written correspondence: ready-made specialist agents instead of a BPMN project with a partner, several requests handled in one pass, and one product instead of several modules billed separately. Since even partners see the two solutions as similar at first glance, the difference has to be shown in the demo.
